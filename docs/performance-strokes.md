# Performance: paint strokes and undo history

Split from [performance.md](performance.md): undo history (copy-on-write snapshots, stroke patches, the byte budget, history restores and History-panel jumps), layer growth under a stroke, and what keeps a paint stroke's per-move work bounded (patched display mips, coalesced input, tiled stroke state, round-brush and stamp-segment skips, the stroke perf scenarios).

## History: snapshots, stroke patches, and the byte budget

`DocumentSession::history` owns a `SessionHistory` (`ui/session_history.{hpp,cpp}`): its stacks, state ids, selection coalescing, rotations, stroke compaction and retained-byte accounting. Its public stack views are const. MainWindow retains snapshot timing, pending-stroke identity guards and canvas/panel refresh; `enforce_session_history_budget` applies the shared budget over the session store.

Every command pushes a copy-on-write `Document` snapshot (`push_undo_snapshot` / `record_history_push`, main_window_history.cpp), so an entry retains exactly the buffers the edit detached. Paint strokes (Brush, Eraser, Clone, Healing, Smudge, Dodge/Burn/Sponge/Blur/Sharpen, Mixer, Pattern Stamp) shrink that snapshot to a `StrokePatch` (core/stroke_patch.hpp) instead of retaining the whole painted layer (GitHub issue 109): the stroke's dirty bounds inside the one buffer it wrote (layer pixels, layer mask, or saved alpha channel) with the pre-stroke pixels, plus the post-stroke pixels once the first undo captures them. The canvas reports strokes through `set_paint_stroke_history_callback` (Begin right after the before-edit push; Finish on release or focus loss with the union of every dirty rect the stroke reported); `MainWindow::handle_paint_stroke_history_event` converts the top entry only when nothing else changed: same session and state ids, no intervening push or undo, and `capture_stroke_patch`'s storage walk finds the target as the ONLY detached buffer with unchanged geometry. Anything else (a mask that grew during the stroke, Stroke Path's scripted strokes, Quick Mask and Smart Filter mask strokes) keeps the whole-document snapshot, so correctness never depends on the patch. The transient cost during a stroke is unchanged (the live layer detaches from the snapshot); only the retained cost shrinks. `PATCHY_STROKE_PATCH_HISTORY=0` disables the shrink for diagnosis; `MainWindowTestAccess::set_stroke_patch_history_enabled` is the test seam (tests/ui/history_stroke_patch_tests.cpp compares both paths pixel for pixel).

A `HistoryState` is therefore a document or a patch (`is_stroke_patch`). `rotate_history_state` swaps documents for snapshot entries and writes a patch into the live document in place (through the revision-bumping accessors, so thumbnails and caches refresh). A patch is relative to the next newer entry (or the live document), which is why eviction from the stack front is always safe and why `materialize_history_state` (New Document From This State on a patch row) starts from the nearest newer snapshot and writes the intervening patches back.

History is byte-budgeted: every push and every patch shrink runs `MainWindow::enforce_history_memory_budget`, a COW-aware pointer walk (`core/document_memory.hpp`) plus the patches' own bytes, evicting the oldest undo states across all sessions once their marginal bytes exceed `history_memory_budget_bytes()` (`ui/memory_info.hpp`; `PATCHY_HISTORY_BUDGET_TEST_MB` is the test override), keeping at least `kMinUndoStatesUnderPressure` states per session under the 40-state cap.

Restores go through `CanvasWidget::set_document_for_history_restore`, which keeps the previous frame + render diagnostics when the restored document has the same size (plain `set_document` dumps both). Together with the paint deferral ([performance.md](performance.md#deferred-full-refresh-paint)), history steps on big documents swap frames instead of flashing checkerboard. Rotations accumulate one `HistoryRefreshRegion` (full, or the union of changed areas: a snapshot step's per-layer revision diff ([performance.md](performance.md#smart-invalidation-and-style-mask-caching)), a patch step's document rect plus the layer's effect bounds when they moved), and History-panel jumps (`jump_to_history_state`) rotate any number of states and run the canvas/UI refresh tail once, so a 20-state jump costs one repaint, not 20.

## Layer growth under a stroke

Every new pixel layer is canvas-sized except a pasted one, which is its pixels' size; a dab outside a layer grows it (`expand_layer_to_include_rect`). Growth re-copies the layer, so per dab it is quadratic over a stroke that leaves a small layer. Interactive strokes open a `LayerGrowthReserve` (core/pixel_tools.hpp) in `arm_paint_stroke_history` for a layer smaller than the canvas: growth then takes headroom (half the old size, at least 256 px, clamped to the canvas), and the release and `finish_paint_stroke_history` close it, trimming the layer to exactly the bounds and bytes per-dab growth gives, before the stroke is reported or recorded. Same-format growth copies rows with `memcpy`. Pinned by `tool_small_layer_grows_cheaply_under_a_long_stroke` (core) and `ui_brush_stroke_off_a_small_layer_ends_with_exact_bounds`.

## Paint strokes: patched display mips and coalesced input (GitHub issue 165)

Fast strokes on big images must not paint straight chords: no per-move work may scale with the whole image or view.

- **Display mips are patchable.** Every zoomed-out chain (composite, move/transform/warp bases, curves clipping, aligned patch draws) halves with `halved_display_mip` (canvas_widget_shared): each output pixel averages its own 2x2 block, alpha-weighted for straight-alpha formats, odd edges average what they have. `patch_render_cache_patches` recomputes only the blocks under the patches (`patch_display_mip_cache`), bit-identical to a rebuild; `RenderCacheDiagnostics::display_mip_rebuilds` counts the full builds and a stroke adds none. `PATCHY_DISPLAY_MIP_PATCH=0` restores the rebuild for diagnosis. `document_changed_impl` repaints the dirty widget rects grown by the smooth scale's reach (`widget_repaint_rect_for_document_rect`) at every zoom, never the whole widget.
- **Every folded mouse position reaches the stroke.** `mouseMoveEvent` feeds `coalesced_stroke_positions` (src/ui/coalesced_pointer_input) through `advance_paint_stroke` before the event's own point and repaints once. Windows recovers the positions from `GetMouseMovePointsEx` (the event's native point is its window position times the device pixel ratio; history entries map back as deltas over that ratio). X11 stops dropping queued motion because `UncompressedPointerInputScope` holds `Qt::AA_CompressHighFrequencyEvents` off exactly while `painting_` is set (`sync_stroke_input_compression` at every `painting_` change). The same scope turns AppKit's mouse coalescing off on macOS (`set_mac_mouse_coalescing_enabled`, which also covers pen drags there). Pen input needs nothing else: Qt already delivers the Windows pen history and never compresses X11 tablet motion. `CanvasWidget::set_coalesced_mouse_positions_provider` is the test seam (`ui_brush_stroke_paints_every_coalesced_mouse_sample`).
- **Per-pixel stroke state is a tiled grid, never a hash map.** The opacity cap, accumulated alpha, alpha caps and the Wet Edges footprint are read for every pixel of every dab; `StrokePixelMap` (src/ui/stroke_pixel_map.hpp: 64x64 dense tiles plus a one-tile cache); a hash map cost a 300 px brush about 0.14 ms per document pixel of travel. Same semantics, so stroke bytes are unchanged.
- `patchy_perf_tests.exe liquifydrag`: Liquify drag frames and Apply on large layers, with ceilings ([liquify.md](liquify.md)).
- `patchy_perf_tests.exe brushstroke` times one move and its paint on a white 4872x6836 image at fit, 100%, 150%, 200% and 800% zoom, plus a full-view repaint per zoom (`PATCHY_PERF_SAMPLER=1` attributes each case).
- `patchy_perf_tests.exe toolbudgets` (also in the default run) holds the core tool strokes' speed budgets, which the core suite only hang-guards: a 240 px round segment, a filled ellipse, a scattered count-8 stroke and a 500 px stamp stroke under 1 s, a pasted layer grown across 3000 px under 8 s exact and 2 s reserved. It reports each median of three and fails over budget; `PATCHY_TEST_TIME_SCALE` scales the budgets (4x in Debug).
- **Big round brushes test only new ground (GitHub issue 135).** Round dabs and hard round segments visit only each row's span of their footprint, and a hard round segment skips the disk the stroke's previous segment painted (`EditOptions::stroke_covered_disk`, set by `draw_brush_segment` while color, opacity and gate hold and no feathered selection applies). `patchy_perf_tests.exe a5brush` times 50/300/731 px strokes on A5 at 300 ppi; `idle` reports a fresh canvas's CPU, paints and timer events with nobody touching it.
- **Clone and Healing stamp segments test only what can change (GitHub issue 597).** Every folded mouse sample is a full segment, so per-segment cost must not scale with the brush's bounding box. `clone_brush_stamp_segment` (and its deep twin) visit each row's capsule span (`CloneSegmentRows`); a segment continuing the previous one from its end with the same round brush (radius, softness, opacity equal, roundness 99+) skips the half-plane behind its start and the fully covered disk there, since the stroke's alpha cap already holds those targets. 8-bit segments of 8,192+ pixels fan out over whole 64-row bands (`StrokePixelMap::reserve_tiles` plus one `Cursor` per worker, layer bytes detached first; `PATCHY_RENDER_SINGLE_THREADED` honored). Bytes equal the full single-threaded walk (`ui_clone_stamp_large_brush_skip_keeps_bytes`); `patchy_perf_tests.exe clonestamp` times 204/529 px on 3580x5370 and 93 px on 858x619, press to first frame included (`PATCHY_PERF_CLONE_FULL_WALK=1` turns the skip off).
- **Clone and Healing strokes coalesce queued moves and start from the render cache (GitHub issue 597 follow-up).** Wayland delivers every pointer motion, so a fast mouse kept the queue full and the canvas painted every 0.15 to 0.6 s. A stroke's moves coalesce per event-loop pass like a Move drag ([interactive-previews.md](interactive-previews.md#drag-move-coalescing)) but drop no position: each recorded move and its folded samples ride on the replayed move as folded samples (`pending_stroke_positions_`), so the bytes equal the moves applied one by one (`ui_clone_stroke_coalesces_queued_moves_and_keeps_bytes`). The All Layers press snapshot shares the settled render cache (`render_settled()`, palette mode excluded) instead of rendering the document again, about 40 ms of a 3580x5370 press (`retouch_snapshot_cache_reuses`). `render_settled()` knows only the canvas's own invalidations: a caller that queues document refreshes (the script host's `pending_refresh_`) flushes them before a retouch operation reads the snapshot, as `ScriptEngineHost::remove_object_in_selection` does (`ui_script_remove_object_heals_selection`). `clonestamp`'s `queued240` lines time a 240-move queue both ways.
- **Smudge and the toning brushes follow the Clone recipe (GitHub issue 660).** Dodge, Burn and Sponge segments (`local_adjustment_brush_segment` and its deep twin) visit `CloneSegmentRows` spans with the continuing-segment skip (the chain is `clone_stroke_segment_end_` with Strength as the opacity) and paint through `walk_segment_rows`: 8,192+ pixels over two or more 64-row bands run as strips of whole bands with a `StrokePixelMap::Cursor` each, layer bytes detached on the launching thread. A Smudge dab (core `smudge_brush_segment`) visits only its disk's row spans and fans dabs of 16,384+ pixels out over row bands (`run_smudge_dab_bands`; each pixel's smear and pickup touch only its own bytes and carried-sample entry; `SmudgeState::single_threaded` mirrors `PATCHY_RENDER_SINGLE_THREADED`). Blur and Sharpen paint spaced dabs that read the current pixels ([brushes-local-adjustments.md](brushes-local-adjustments.md#blur-and-sharpen)), so they have no continuing-segment skip; a dab of 8,192+ pixels fans its rows out over bands, each band reading the dab's own copy. All of them coalesce queued moves like Clone (`toning_stroke_tool_active`). The press snapshot was already a copy-on-write share; the layer's detach at the first dab remains (7 to 17 ms on 3580x5370). Bytes equal the whole-rectangle walk on one thread (`ui_toning_brush_span_and_strips_keep_bytes`, `tool_smudge_banded_dabs_keep_bytes`, `ui_toning_stroke_coalesces_queued_moves_and_keeps_bytes`). `patchy_perf_tests.exe toningstroke` times each press step (`last_retouch_press_timings`), a 60-move stroke and a 240-move queue per tool at 60 and 400 px on 3580x5370; `PATCHY_RENDER_THREADS=4` prices a 4-core laptop.
- **Big toning brushes paint by distance, not by pointer sample (GitHub issue 660, second report).** Every Dodge, Burn or Sponge segment re-evaluates the brush's soft front edge (about 60,000 pixels at 775 px, Soft 12%) however short the step, and every Smudge segment stamps a whole disk at its end, so at 775 px a pointer that reports every motion (Wayland, a zoomed-out view where a screen pixel is several image pixels) queued more work than a 4-core laptop could do, and the image kept changing for seconds after the pointer stopped. A segment now waits until the stroke has moved a tenth of the brush from the last painted point (`toning_segment_waits_for_spacing`; Smudge its dab spacing, a fifth of the radius, `smudge_segment_waits_for_spacing`); the held point is painted by the next segment, by the release, or after a 30 ms pause of real (spontaneous, tablet or Default-coalesced) input (`toning_tail_timer_`, `paint_toning_tail`; a test's sent or AllMoves events never start it, so bytes stay deterministic). Blur and Sharpen keep their own dab spacing. Brushes under 20 px are unaffected. The 8-bit Dodge/Burn walk also reads a per-setting curve table (`dodge_burn_stroke_table`: the lightness change for every `54R + 183G + 19B` with Protect Tones, the adjusted byte per channel byte without) and the snapshot by row, and a Smudge dab does smear and pickup in one pass; those three keep the bytes. `ui_toning_big_brush_burst_paints_by_spacing_and_keeps_bytes` checks the tables against the curve, the segment count of a queued burst and that queued and sent bursts give the same bytes. `patchy_perf_tests.exe toningbig` (PATCHY_PERF_TONING_SIZE, default 775; PATCHY_PERF_TONING_TOOLS) times every tool with his settings at fit and 100%: sent moves and a 240-move queue until it drains, with `RenderCacheDiagnostics::toning_segments`, `toning_segment_pixels` and `toning_segment_ms`. On MAPPY with `PATCHY_RENDER_THREADS=4`, his settings at fit, a 240-move queue went from 240 segments and 0.26 s of segment time to 30 and 0.04 s (Dodge; Burn 0.56 to 0.04 s, Sponge 0.57 to 0.10 s, Smudge 3.5 to 0.8 s); at 400 px and 100% a Dodge move costs 1.0 ms (2.6 ms in 1.29).
- **A Clone or Healing press copies no whole image (GitHub issue 597 follow-up 3).** The 8-bit source (`clone_source_cache_`) is an uninitialized canvas-sized image filled in 64 px tiles (`take_clone_source_snapshot`, `LazyCloneSource`): from the render cache it shares (All Layers, `held`) or from a copy-on-write copy of the active layer through `active_layer_sample_region` (Current Layer, `held_layer`). A stamp fills the tiles its destination rect samples first (`fill_clone_source_for_destination`: the offset, or the transform's corner bounds plus two pixels), the Healing release fills its grid and the grid through the offset, and `patch_render_cache_patches` fills the tiles a patch overwrites, drops `held` while it paints so the cache is written in place, then shares it again: outside the filled tiles `held` always holds the press's composite. Before, the first frame copied the 77 MB cache (22 ms on wbench) and a Current Layer press converted the whole layer (1 s on wbench). Current & Below still renders its own snapshot. What remains whole-image is the layer's copy-on-write detach at the first dab (the undo snapshot shares the pixels until the stroke's patch replaces it). `patchy_perf_tests.exe clonepress` times each step of a 529 px press on 3580x5370 (`CanvasWidget::last_retouch_press_timings`, `render_cache_patch_detach_ms`); `ui_clone_lazy_source_keeps_bytes_and_undo_restores` pins the bytes against the whole-image source and the undo.
