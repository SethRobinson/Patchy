# Perspective Planes

`Filter > Perspective Planes...` (command id `filter.perspective_planes`, default Ctrl+Alt+V) opens a modal workspace that pastes, clones and paints onto flat surfaces in perspective: a wall, a floor, a sign, the side of a box. It works on the active 8-bit or 16-bit RGB pixel layer; 32-bit documents disable it, Smart Objects must be rasterized first, and text and shape layers get the usual rasterize prompt. Patchy's name and layout are its own (see "Legal boundary").

## Workspace

- Layout: an options strip on top (what the current tool or the pasted image can change), a tool column (Create Plane C, Edit Plane V, Clone S, Paint B, Hand H, Zoom Z, then Paste...), the view, and a bottom row with a one-line hint for the current step, Fit, the zoom, Result (New layer or Current layer), Undo, Redo, OK and Cancel. Object names start with `perspective`.
- The hint always says what to do next and turns `status_error_text` with a reason when four corners cannot be a plane (too close, two sides crossing, a corner pointing inward or three in a line). Create Plane draws the grid live while the fourth corner follows the pointer and refuses an impossible click.
- Edit Plane: square corner handles, round edge handles (move both corners of that edge), drag inside to move a plane, Delete or Backspace removes it, Grid sets the cell size (0 is Auto, about 8 cells on the long side). A round + tab outside each edge of the selected plane pulls out a joined plane: it hinges on that edge at Angle (90 folds toward the viewer, 180 continues flat, 270 folds away), and a diamond on its far edge sets its depth. A joined plane follows its parent until one of its own corners is dragged.
- Paste... lists the clipboard image, the selection on the active layer and up to 12 other pixel layers (Ctrl+V takes the first). The image floats on the selected plane, fitted and turned upright; dragging it onto another plane moves it there. On a plane it has corner handles (scale) and a round handle above its top edge (rotation); Scale and Rotation are also in the strip. Enter or Place writes it, Esc or Remove drops it, a click elsewhere places it.
- Clone: Alt-click (Option on macOS) a plane to set the source, then paint; the first stroke after a new source fixes the offset (aligned). Paint lays a color (the foreground color at open, Color... changes it). Size (plane units, so the brush is its stated size where the plane is nearest and shrinks with distance), Hardness and Opacity apply to both; the outline drawn is the brush circle mapped onto the plane.
- Zoom and pan: the wheel zooms at the pointer, Space-drag, middle-drag or Hand pans, Ctrl+0 fits, Ctrl+1 is 100%, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- step. Keys are window shortcuts on the modal dialog, so none reaches the document.
- History: every committed paste, every stroke and every plane change is one workspace step (up to 60; Grid and Angle edits of one plane merge). Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z and Ctrl+Y walk it; Undo first drops a floating image or the corners being placed.
- OK writes the edits as ONE document undo step (`MainWindow::apply_perspective_edits`): by default a new layer named "Perspective" directly above the active layer, holding only the edits cropped to what they cover; Current layer composites them onto the layer, which grows to hold them. Current layer is offered only where the layer can take the edits unchanged: an 8-bit layer with transparency, or an opaque one covering the canvas; a 16-bit layer always gets a new 16-bit layer (the 8-bit edits widened). A document selection scales the edits' alpha by its coverage. Cancel leaves the document untouched.
- Planes are kept per document for the session (`DocumentSession::perspective_planes`), after OK and after Cancel, and reopen in Edit Plane. They are never written to a file: no Photoshop-accepted container for them has been verified, and Photoshop's own Vanishing Point resource is not authored. The Result choice is remembered for the session.

## Geometry and pixels (`core/perspective_planes.hpp`)

- `homography_from_unit_square` is Heckbert's square-to-quad map. `check_plane_quad` checks sides of at least 4 px, crossing sides, an area of at least 64 px^2, then convexity with a 0.02 relative turn floor (three corners within about 1.1 degrees of a line count as concave).
- Plane units: `plane_geometry` orders the corners clockwise on screen (plane space is never mirrored), recovers the surface's proportions from a pinhole camera (aspect = |K^-1 h1| / |K^-1 h2|), and scales so plane units are document pixels along the side nearest the viewer. `perspective_camera_for` takes the focal length from the first free plane whose sides imply one (Zhang's single-rectangle constraint, kept within 0.25 to 6 times the document diagonal), else the diagonal, centred on the document.
- Joined planes (`connected_plane_quad`): the parent is lifted into camera space (X(u, v) = b1 u + b2 v + b3 with B = K^-1 H), the new plane leaves the hinge edge along cos(180 - angle) outward + sin(180 - angle) normal (normal toward the camera), reaching `depth` times the parent's extent across the edge; a result behind the viewer or not a usable quad is refused. `connected_plane_depth_at` solves the depth whose far edge passes nearest the pointer.
- Determinism: plain IEEE double with no library transcendental (`perspective_sin_cos_degrees` is a fixed series, exact on quarter turns), the source built with `-ffp-contract=off` off MSVC, integer pixel math. `perspective_planes_results_are_deterministic` pins a digest.
- `PerspectiveSampler`: a premultiplied RGBA8 mip chain (2x2 box, transparent outside), 8-bit fixed bilinear weights, and trilinear blending between levels picked from the footprint (log2 from the exponent and a linear mantissa step), so strong foreshortening averages instead of aliasing.
- `render_perspective_image` draws an image through image -> plane -> target, source-over, with an anti-aliased plane edge from each pixel's distance to the plane rectangle in target pixels (plane units over the gradient length). The workspace uses it at full resolution for OK and at screen resolution for the floating preview (the view transform composed in), so a repaint costs the view's size.
- `PerspectiveCanvas` holds the layer on the canvas (straight RGBA8), the edits layer the tools write, their composite and the composite's sampler. Strokes accumulate a max coverage mask per pixel and lerp from the stroke's starting pixels, so overlapping dabs never exceed Opacity; clone reads the sampler, which refreshes only when a stroke ends (no feedback). Every change is a list of 64 px tile pairs for the workspace's undo.

## Legal boundary

Binding rule: [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md), "Perspective editing"; claim record: [patent-research-perspective.md](patent-research-perspective.md). Every edit is clipped to the one plane it starts on: a dab's square is cut to its plane rectangle before mapping and every pixel's weight is multiplied by its coverage of that plane; a paste is clipped the same way; a floating image is on exactly one plane; a clone source is read only inside its own plane. Nothing is ever continued onto an adjacent plane (`perspective_planes_edits_never_leave_their_plane`). Planes come only from the user's clicks and drags (or a script's corners): no line, vanishing point or plane detection and no automatic straightening. The name is descriptive; the interface draws on no other product's icons, colors or layout.

## Scripting

`patchy.perspective` (`src/ui/script_perspective.cpp`): `checkPlane(corners)`, `getPlanes(doc)` / `setPlanes(doc, planes)` (the session planes the workspace shows), and `paste(layer, {plane, source, placement, target})`, `clone(layer, {plane, sourcePlane, from, points, size, hardness, opacity, target})`, `paint(layer, {plane, points, color, size, hardness, opacity, target})`, each one edit through `apply_perspective_edits` riding the run's undo snapshot. The guide and `patchy.d.ts` have the details.

## Not built yet

A perspective rectangle selection, moving a selection's content in perspective, heal-blending of clone strokes, saving planes in a file, and Perspective Warp. Edits that wrap onto the next plane wait for the boundary above.
