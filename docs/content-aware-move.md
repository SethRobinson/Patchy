# Content-Aware Move

The Content-Aware Move tool (GitHub issues 599 and 369) moves or copies a selected object and heals it into its new place; in Move mode it also fills the spot the object left. It is the Patch tool's drag ([healing.md](healing.md#patch-tool)) with a different release, and it sits inside a recorded patent boundary: read the Content-Aware Move bullet in [legal-constraints-filters.md](legal-constraints-filters.md) and the claim record in [patent-research-content-aware-move.md](patent-research-content-aware-move.md) before changing anything here.

## Gesture

- `CanvasTool::ContentAwareMove` (appended to the enum), hotkey id `tools.content_aware_move` (no default key), in the Healing flyout after Patch and in its Shift+J cycle ([tool-flyouts.md](tool-flyouts.md)). Icon `tool-content-aware-move.svg`: a dashed rounded square (the old spot), a solid one with an accent dot (the new spot) and a bent arrow between them.
- Drawing the outline is the Patch tool's lasso (`uses_patch_drag` in `ui/canvas_types.hpp` routes both tools through the same press, cursor and release code): freehand outline, the New/Add/Subtract/Intersect combine modes with the tool's own slot (selection tool index 9), Shift/Alt forcing outline drawing. An existing selection of any origin works too. The Lasso's Alt switch to straight segments is not shared (the Patch tool does not have it either).
- A press inside the selection in Replace mode starts the drag (`begin_patch_tool_drag`, which latches `patch_tool_drag_content_aware_move_`). While dragging, the canvas shows the untouched composite, a raw ghost of the region (the frozen retouch snapshot cut to the selection's soft mask) at the pointer offset, and the statically dashed outline at that offset. **Nothing is drawn, healed or written in the original spot until release.** A release below the drag threshold is a no-op; Escape, a tool switch, edit locks and document teardown cancel with no history entry; Enter commits a live drag like a release.
- Switching to the tool shows a how-to status line; committing an outline says where to drag (Extend says "copy").
- The outline takes the tool's own Feather and Anti-alias ([Options](#options)).

## Release (`commit_content_aware_move`, canvas_widget_patch_tool.cpp)

Runs once, on release, in one `begin_edit("Content-Aware Move")` pixel step, followed by the selection step that moves the selection onto the copy (`move_selection_with_patch_drop`, shared with Patch Destination).

- **Move**: (1) the old spot is filled first (`content_aware_move_hole_fill`, on a worker under the "Moving object..." overlay before the edit opens). The hole is the selection's coverage grown by 6 px (`kContentAwareMoveHoleGrow`, so a glow, soft shadow edge or outline fringe the selection just misses goes too) and padded by one uncovered cell. In order:
  - **Block match** (`exemplar_block_match`, core/exemplar_inpaint.hpp): one exhaustive exemplar search for the whole hole. The hole plus an 8 px band is one target patch; every translation within `clamp(2 * max(w, h), 96, 320)` px whose shifted hole lies on the image, whose shifted block misses the hole's box and whose shifted hole misses the drop region's box is scored by integer L1 over the band (a fixed sample grid of at most 4096 cells, off-image samples a fixed miss) plus 1/8 level per sample per pixel of offset; the first smallest wins. The block is taken only when its band difference is at most three times the band's own neighbour-to-neighbour difference (`ExemplarBlockMatch::texture`) plus 8 levels per channel: busy grass differs from any shifted grass, while a block that brings a curtain into a window does not match. The hole is then healed in from that offset like a Patch Source drag (`heal_mask_from_surroundings` with a translation), with the membrane faded to an edge band (below).
  - **Exemplar fill**, when no offset fits (large objects: the free space beside the hole is narrower than the hole) or the best one matches poorly: Remove Object's exhaustive fill (`exemplar_inpaint`, 9 px patches) with the drop region as `ExemplarInpaintOptions::excluded`, then `exemplar_match_tone` at 100%, written with an identity map.
  - **Shape-derived map** (`spot_heal_source_map`, drop region excluded as a shape) when neither finds a clean source. A map that finds no unselected pixels refuses with a status error and no history entry.

  (2) The retouch snapshot is taken again (the filled document), and the copy is healed in at the drop point through `write_patch_heal`, the Patch tool's Destination heal (texture from the original snapshot at the dragged offset, boundary tone from the refreshed snapshot, so a drop overlapping the old spot blends into the fill), with the edge band.
- **Edge band** (`fade_heal_offsets_to_edge_band`, core/heal_membrane.hpp; `content_aware_move_edge_band`, a quarter of the selection's smaller side, 6 to 48 px): the solved membrane offsets apply in full at the outline and fall linearly to none that far inside (3-4 chamfer distance, integer weights). The moved object keeps its own colours and only the band along the outline takes the new surroundings' tone; Patch Destination shifts the whole region, which turned a dark walker on a sunlit beach pale. The same band keeps a glow or shadow on the old spot's ring from spreading across the block-matched fill.
- **Extend**: the copy and heal only (step 2 against the original snapshot); the original stays.
- 16/32-bit layers heal narrowed copies (`NarrowedLayerEdit`, per step), as Spot Healing and Patch do.

No Structure or Color setting and no Transform on Drop: each needs its own legal check first ([legal-constraints-filters.md](legal-constraints-filters.md)).

## Real photos

On the nine real photos in `local-test-fixtures/content-aware-move` (sources and licences in its README), a shape-derived mirror alone pastes whatever block sits beside the hole (a curtain rod into a ceiling, half a lawn bowl into grass) and a whole-region membrane recolours the moved object, which is why the block match runs first and the membrane fades to an edge band. Remove Object's exemplar fill alone smears walls and leaves step triangles on smooth ceilings; Patch Source looks best whenever its source is clean, which is what the block match finds. Large objects (a chair filling a room corner) still fall back to the exemplar fill and look rough; a smaller, tighter selection helps. `ui_content_aware_move_real_photo_sheets` writes `cam_<photo>_<run>.png` for Move, Extend, Patch Destination, Patch Source over the old spot and Remove Object on each photo (skipped without the fixtures).

## Options

Options bar, in this order: the four combine-mode buttons | Mode (`contentAwareMoveModeCombo`: Move, Extend), Destination (`contentAwareMoveDestinationCombo`), Object Selection ([below](#object-selection)) | Feather and Anti-alias | the shared retouch Sample (`retouchSampleLayersCombo`, Current Layer / Current & Below / All Layers, with Ignore Adjustment Layers; [healing.md](healing.md#sample)), which stands in for a Sample All Layers box. Mode is session-only like the Patch tool's: every start is Move, a mid-session choice reaches new documents (`main_window_sessions.cpp`). The Info panel lists the mode, the destination, the Sample choice, Object Selection when on, and the feather.

- **Feather and Anti-alias** (GitHub issue 599 follow-up) are the selection tools' shared controls (`selectionFeatherSpin`, `selectionAntiAliasCheck`) with the tool's own slot (selection tool index 9, keys `tools/contentAwareMoveFeather` and `tools/contentAwareMoveAntiAlias`; defaults 0 and on, as the Lasso's; [selection-tools.md](selection-tools.md)). They shape the outline the tool draws, through the Lasso's mask commit; the release already writes the copy by the selection's coverage, so a feathered outline lands with a soft edge. A selection made with another tool keeps its own edge. Feather is not a Structure or Color setting: it only changes the selection mask, never the fill or the heal.

## Object Selection

`contentAwareMoveObjectSelectionCheck` (GitHub issue 599 follow-up), key `tools/contentAwareMoveObjectSelection`, default off; built only when `segmentation_available()` (a build without the Object Selection model has no checkbox, and the canvas pass refuses there too). On the release of an outline drawn with the tool in New mode (Add, Subtract and Intersect outlines and selections from other tools are left alone), `content_aware_move_object_pass` (canvas_widget_object_select.cpp) runs right-click Select Object's solve: the outline's bounding box is the one box prompt, sampled as Select Object samples (the Object Selection tool's Sample All Layers). The mask is thresholded at 50%, grown 4 px (`kContentAwareMoveObjectGrow`) with Select > Modify > Expand's `expanded_region`, then softened by the tool's Feather (Anti-alias off thresholds first), and replaces the outline as its own "Object Selection" undo step after the outline's. The user then drags as usual; the release is unchanged. A failure (no model files, nothing to sample on the active layer, an empty mask or one that misses the outline) leaves the outline as the selection with a status line and no extra step. This is one prompt per solve on a release, combined by plain boolean and grown and blurred as ordinary selection edits: inside the "Automatic selection" bullet of [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md); the segmentation never feeds the fill or the heal, which see only the resulting selection.

## Destination

`ContentAwareMoveDestination` (`canvas_types.hpp`), stored as the token `tools/contentAwareMoveDestination` (`currentLayer`, `newLayer`; missing or unknown reads as Current Layer).

- **Current Layer** (default): the release writes the active layer, as above.
- **New Layer**: the release runs exactly as for Current Layer, then `split_content_aware_move_to_new_layer` compares the active layer with its pixels from before the move, puts every pixel that changed (the filled old spot and the healed copy, cropped to their bounds, at the layer's depth) on a new Normal, 100% layer named "Content-Aware Move" right above it, restores the active layer's pixels and bounds, and selects the new layer (so the Move tool can nudge the result at once). All inside the one "Content-Aware Move" pixel step; the selection step follows as usual, and one Undo of the pixel step removes the layer. Over an opaque layer the result looks exactly like Current Layer. The new layer does not take the source layer's opacity, blend mode or mask. With Sample set to Current Layer a second move then reads the new layer, which holds only the last result; use Current & Below or All Layers for chained moves.
