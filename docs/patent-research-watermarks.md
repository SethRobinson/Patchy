# Patent research: invisible watermarks

Engineering record behind the "Watermark detection" rule in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md#watermark-detection)
(feature design in [watermarks.md](watermarks.md) and [file-info.md](file-info.md#watermarks)). Conventions follow
[patent-research.md](patent-research.md).

## What Patchy does

- Stable Diffusion / SDXL: decodes the open DWT-DCT mark of the MIT invisible-watermark library with a
  Patchy-owned integer decoder. Detection only.
- Adobe TrustMark: runs Adobe's own MIT-licensed ONNX models, unmodified and byte-identical to Adobe's
  published files (SHA-256 pinned), to read marks (variants Q, P, C) and to add a mark (variant Q encoder):
  the encoder output minus the cover, a pixel-space residual, is added to the image. The models are a user
  download from Adobe's host and never part of a Patchy package. MIT has no patent grant; keeping Adobe's
  unmodified models is part of the reasoning recorded here.
- No removal, no remover model, no C2PA soft-binding assertions written.

## Search (Google Patents, 2026-10-23)

- No Adobe patent or published application covering TrustMark itself was found.
- **US 12450782 B2** (Adobe, granted 2025-10-21, priority 2023-09-21, "Latent space based steganographic
  image generation"). Claims 1 and 17 require embedding the secret as a latent offset to the latent code of a
  pretrained autoencoder (VQGAN) that renders the image. Claim 10 (decoding with a perturbation-trained decoder
  and showing the characters in a UI) is limited to images generated by incorporating an embedding of the
  secret within latent code used to render them. Patchy's design differs: TrustMark adds a pixel-space
  residual from an end-to-end trained encoder to an existing image, and no latent code renders the image.
- **US 2023/0385980 A1** (content-adaptive watermarks): visible watermark placement; Patchy places no visible
  watermark.

## Limits and watch items

- Adobe-assignee search only; Digimarc and other holders were not searched, and applications under 18
  months old are unpublished.
- A new model (another TrustMark variant's encoder, a different watermark family), the remover, or writing
  C2PA soft-binding assertions needs a new review.
- Hosting the models on a Patchy release keeps them byte-identical to Adobe's, with Adobe's MIT text beside
  them and Adobe credited as the source, without implying endorsement; it is a publish step and needs Seth's
  go-ahead.
