# Shadow and glow render calibration

Split from [layer-effects-render.md](layer-effects-render.md): Drop Shadow, Outer Glow, Inner Shadow and Inner Glow (the exterior and interior soft-effect pipelines, Continuous shadow, Layer Knocks Out Drop Shadow, Contour, Size 0, Technique and Range). Its conventions and [rules shared by every effect](layer-effects-render.md#rules-shared-by-every-effect) (the burn-mode color fold included) apply here; the Satin tent kernel these blurs reuse is defined there.

## Shadows and glows

A spread radius over 8 px and the Precise falloff give each painted pixel the strength of its connected shape's most opaque pixel. That is a property of the whole layer: a render window of a layer larger than the canvas reads it from `layer_matte_strengths_for_window`, never from its own pixels ([why](performance-compositor.md#strip-renders-against-the-one-pass-walk)).

- **Drop shadow and outer glow share ONE soft-effect pipeline** (`prepare_photoshop_soft_effect_mask`, layer_style_mask_ops.cpp): (1) Spread expands the matte by the INTEGER radius `lround(spread% * size)` as a GRAYSCALE dilation (alpha x area-sampled disc; radii above 8 fall back to the binarizing chamfer band, which overshoots AA text ~20%); (2) the remaining size blurs with Satin's tent kernel, `N = max(2, lround(size)) - spread_radius` (NOT a triple-box). Spread 100 or size 0 = hard band with 1 px AA ramp; sizes 1-2 still blur with N=2. Never reimplement spread as a post-blur gain: saturating a blur's tail exposes the kernel support as per-glyph boxes.
- **Continuous (long) drop shadow** (Patchy-only, no PS counterpart; `LayerDropShadow::continuous/fade`): `sweep_layer_style_mask_in_place` (layer_style_mask_ops.cpp) extrudes the UNSHIFTED matte along the integer offset before the spread/size pipeline above runs, so Size and Spread soften the swept band and `layerConceals`, masks, opacity, and blend mode apply unchanged. The grid is partitioned into digital lines along the major axis (intercept `minor - floor((2 u step + L) / 2L)`, u counted in the sweep direction, L = max(|dx|, |dy|)); each line runs a monotone-deque trailing maximum over L + 1 samples, O(W x H). Fade multiplies the swept value by `1 - fade * t / L`, t = samples since the last matte value above zero (exact on hard mattes, approximate on AA edges). `render_drop_shadow` unites the source and shifted rects for the effect bounds and adds L to the mask apron so windowed renders match full ones, then bounds that apron by the window plus the source (the only matte the sweep can carry), so a 30000 px shadow stays canvas-sized; a zero offset takes the plain path bit-for-bit. Pinned by `compositor_continuous_drop_shadow_sweeps_to_offset_and_fades`.
- **"Layer Knocks Out Drop Shadow" (DrSh `layerConceals`, default ON)**: the layer's transparency shape multiplies its own shadow by `(1 - matte)`, regardless of fill opacity (fill-0 shows no shadow inside), master opacity, or a Stroke Overprint knockout; OFF shows the shadow through proportionally. Invisible under opaque content. `LayerDropShadow::layer_conceals`; the pinned 12-item DrSh shape carries the key. Fixture `photoshop-shadow-conceals.psd/bmp`.
- **Drop Shadow Contour (DrSh `TrnS`, `AntA`)**: the curve maps the soft-effect mask before Opacity (`LayerDropShadow::contour`, `sample_style_contour_lut`; without Anti-aliased the nearest LUT entry). A Linear contour writes no `TrnS` (the pinned 12-item DrSh); a shaped one goes after `AntA` (13 items) and opens clean in Photoshop 2026, whose flatten moves with it the same way (a hard 90% black GIMP shadow over white: 88 in GIMP, Patchy and Photoshop; October 29, 2026, #507). GIMP imports use it for GEGL's linear-light falloff ([xcf-layer-effects.md](xcf-layer-effects.md#layer-effects-gimp-3-filters)). Pinned by `drop_shadow_contour_maps_the_shadow_mask`. Known difference, contour or not: the shadow of a soft-edged layer (a blurred stroke, size 7, spread 14%) reaches farther and stronger in Photoshop than in Patchy.
- **Size 0** (`photoshop-size-zero-effects.psd/bmp`): Inner Shadow draws the inverse matte offset by the rounded distance vector as a hard band, choke irrelevant (7 px at distance 10 / angle 135, 5 px at angle 90, nothing at distance 0); Inner Glow Edge renders nothing on opaque pixels while Center fills the whole shape (the complement of the empty edge field); Drop Shadow is a hard offset band; Outer Glow renders nothing even at spread 100. `prepare_inner_shadow` and `prepare_inner_glow` therefore do not return early at size 0; the interior soft mask degenerates to the unblurred inverse matte there, so on a soft pixel of coverage `s` an Edge glow keeps `1 - s` and a Center glow `s` (feathered squares, November 2026: 203 where a black square with a white glow lets 140 of the white page through). Precise takes the same field at Size 0.
- **Interior soft-mask falloff** (Inner Shadow, and Inner Glow's Softer technique): `prepare_photoshop_interior_soft_mask` (layer_style_mask_ops.cpp) is the interior mirror of the exterior pipeline: the INVERSE matte expands by `R = lround(choke% * size)`, then tent-blurs with `N = max(2, lround(size)) - R` (choke 100 or size 0 = hard band). The expansion is `clamp(R + 1 - d)` on the [soft-matte distance](#soft-mattes-under-choke-and-precise) `d`: on a hard matte the exact Euclidean disc at any radius, and without a choke radius the inverse matte as it is. Byte-matched, thin-bar overlaps and dot corners included. An exactly-half shadow offset (cos60 x 5 = 2.5) rounds away from zero ([soft edges](#interior-effects-on-soft-edges)). Inner shadows clamp each offset axis to the layer extent plus size + 2, past which every interior sample is already outside the layer.
- **Inner glow Technique/Range** (`photoshop-inner-glow{,-range}.psd/bmp`, `photoshop-inner-shadow.psd/bmp`): Edge source = interior falloff, then the Range gain `min(1, blur * 100/Inpr)` applied AFTER the blur. **Center source = the exact complement of the gained Edge field**, `1 - min(1, edge * gain)`. A descriptor omitting `Inpr` renders at Range 100 in BOTH glows (never fall back to 50; legacy Patchy files always wrote an explicit 50). The regenerated IrGl is PS's 12-item shape with `GlwT` between `Opct` and `Ckmt` plus stored `Inpr` (`LayerInnerGlow::technique/range`). Overlapping edges can differ by 1/255 from Photoshop's byte-rounded tent passes.
- **Inner Glow Precise** uses the Euclidean distance in pixels to the outside of the shape. For size 18 and choke 30%, Photoshop's Range 50% Center ramp begins at distance 5 and reaches full strength at 19. Range 100% halves the Edge field before the Center complement. The general Edge ramp is `clamp((size + 1 - distance) * 50 / (range * (size + 1 - round(size * choke / 100))), 0, 1)` for integer Size. Seven Photoshop controls cover Edge and Center, sizes 8 and 18, and ranges 50%, 75% and 100%. The distance is the [soft-matte distance](#soft-mattes-under-choke-and-precise), and with a Choke it is counted back from the choked shape. A Choke that takes the whole Size draws a hard band at any Range (Size 10, Choke 100, Range 100: 255 through distance 10, then nothing).
- **Outer glow "Softer"** (`photoshop-outer-glow{,-range}.psd/bmp`): spread dilation, tent blur `N = max(2, lround(size)) - spread_radius`, then the Range gain `min(1, blur * 100/Inpr)`. **Range's UI default is 50, so real files render TWICE the raw blur**; an Action-Manager-authored glow omitting `Inpr` gets 100, which is why naive probes miss it. `prepare_outer_glow_softer_mask`; `GlwT`/`Inpr` parsed, modeled (`LayerOuterGlow::technique/range`), and written (PS round-trips them). "Precise" keeps an uncalibrated distance-ramp render (approximate; Range not applied). Residuals: spread radii above 8 chamfer; spread-100 corner arcs differ ~1 px.

Inner Shadow, Inner Glow and Outer Glow apply the contour to the falloff mask before opacity and knockout. The Softer glow’s Range gain precedes the contour. For shaped curves, Anti-aliased samples the mask at thirds of a pixel on each axis and averages the mapped coverage. The Linear path skips this transfer. An Outer Glow with a constant white curve reveals a second limit. The mapped coverage rises by the raw blurred matte divided by 0.12 until it reaches the curve output. Constant white and constant 50% gray gave the same initial ramp. This keeps a curve with a raised left endpoint inside the glow’s reach. Photoshop 2027 produced identical solid-color glow BMPs at Jitter 0% and 60%.

Gradient Outer Glow samples the near stop at full strength and the far stop where the matte fades away. The position is one minus the Range-scaled, contoured coverage. The lower of the gradient's sampled opacity and the raw matte divided by 0.12 controls transparency. Photoshop 2027 controls on black and white cover solid color, three RGB stops, constant and ramped stop opacity, and Range 50%, 75% and 100%. Gradient jitter is retained on save. Its effect on the stop position is uncalibrated.

## Gradient Inner Glow

An Inner Glow's Fill is a color or a gradient (`LayerInnerGlow::gradient`, GitHub issue 746). Photoshop 2027 captures of November 2026 are in `local-test-fixtures/layer-style-746/probes` (made by `probe.jsx` there; `check_model.py` holds the rule below against every capture): 20 documents, one glow geometry each, with a row of hard squares and a row of feathered ones and a column per fill.

- **Stop position.** With `c` the coverage the same glow has with a solid color (after the Range gain, the Center complement and the contour), the gradient is sampled at `1 - c`. The first stop sits where the solid glow is strongest: at the edge of an Edge glow, in the middle of a Center glow. Past the glow's reach an Edge glow shows the last stop and a Center glow the first.
- **Opacity.** The gradient's own opacity at that position is the glow's alpha, times the layer's alpha and the effect's Opacity. The coverage does not limit it, unlike the Outer Glow's matte limit: opaque stops cover the whole shape, 100% to 0% stops give the solid glow's alpha again, and 0% to 100% stops leave the strongest part clear.
- **Size 0.** A solid Edge glow draws nothing on opaque pixels there. A gradient Edge glow still draws: zero coverage is the last stop, over the whole shape, and a soft pixel of coverage `s` samples the gradient at `s` (a contour is not applied to an Edge glow at Size 0, unprobed). Center at Size 0 fills the shape with the first stop.
- **Method.** The coverage goes through the gradient as tones go through a Gradient Map: Perceptual takes the Classic smoothness ease too (`gradient_color` with `ease_every_method`; without it a red to blue Perceptual glow was 15 levels off, with it 6). Linear is assumed to follow, as it does in a Gradient Map; Smooth and Stripes have no ease. The Outer Glow still samples without it (unprobed there).
- **Noise** moves that opacity (`effect_noise_coverage` on the sampled opacity) and never the stop position: opaque stops at Noise 50 keep 51.9% of their pixels and average 87.7% (87.5% by the rule).
- **Gradient jitter** (`ShdN`) scatters the stop position in Photoshop. Patchy keeps the value and does not render it, as for the Outer Glow.
- **Fit.** `check_model.py` holds the position and opacity rule within 2 levels in every capture: Softer and Precise, Range 50 and 100, Choke 30, Size 0, 20 and 60, a Cone contour with and without Anti-aliased. Patchy's own render of the hard squares is within 2 for the solid glow, within 6 for two-stop gradients (straight, smooth, Perceptual, moved midpoints) and within 9 for a smooth three-stop gradient. The feathered squares stay inside the same figures over their whole soft edge, Precise and Choke included.
- **Unmodelled.** Where the glow saturates (the middle of a Center glow, the rim of an Edge glow under Range 50), Photoshop stops a smooth three-stop gradient short of its end stop: (250, 217, 42) for a first stop of (250, 220, 40) at Range 100, 6 levels at Range 50, 9 with Choke 30 as well. The same gradient in the Perceptual method ends 12 levels short in blue at Range 50. Two-stop gradients reach their stop, and Size 0 reaches it exactly.

`inner_glow_sample` (src/render/layer_compositor.hpp) samples with `gradient_color` and `gradient_stop_opacity`, endpoint smoothing on. A glow without a gradient takes the code path it always did. Tests: `compositor_inner_glow_gradient_follows_solid_coverage`, `compositor_inner_glow_gradient_matches_photoshop_samples`, `inner_glow_gradient_noise_moves_opacity_only`, `photoshop_inner_glow_gradient_probes_match_if_available`.

## Interior effects on soft edges

An Inner Glow or Inner Shadow belongs to the layer's own color, and the layer meets the backdrop once, by its coverage. With coverage `s` (pixel alpha x mask) and effect strength `f` at a pixel, the page keeps `1 - s`, the effect gets `s x f` and the layer's color `s x (1 - f)`. Layer Opacity scales `s`. Over a transparent backdrop the result's alpha is `s` and its color is the styled color. Each effect blends with the layer's color by its own mode, whatever lies behind the layer.

`composite_pixel_layer` (src/render/layer_compositor.hpp) folds both effects into the layer's color in the base pass, after the overlays and Satin: `prepare_inner_glow` / `prepare_inner_shadow` resolve the falloff masks, `fold_prepared_inner_glows` / `fold_prepared_inner_shadows` apply them through `fold_effect_color`, and the base pass composites the styled color by the layer's coverage. Until November 2026 both effects ran as passes over the finished layer with alpha `s x f`. That left `s x (1 - s x f)` of the layer's color and `(1 - s)(1 - s x f)` of the page, widened the alpha over a transparent backdrop, and at a layer Opacity below 100% blended the effect with the half-transparent result. Hard edges of an opaque layer at 100% Opacity render the same either way.

Photoshop 2027 captures (November 2026) are in `local-test-fixtures/layer-style-soft-edges` (`probe.jsx`, `make_jobs.py`, `make_jobs2.py`; `analyze.py` checks the rule on Photoshop's own pixels, `compare.py` compares a Patchy render): 100 px squares feathered by 4 px, each over a white, a blue and a mid-tone page and over nothing, plus the feathered row of the gradient Inner Glow captures above.

- **The rule holds in Photoshop for every cell**: taking `s` from the capture over nothing, each page shows through by `1 - s` exactly, and the color over a page is the color over nothing mixed by `s`. That covers Inner Glow and Inner Shadow at 100% and 50%, a shadow with Distance, a glow under a shadow, a Color Overlay under a glow, the Screen, Multiply, Color Dodge, Overlay and Linear Burn modes, a feathered layer mask, a square painted at 50%, and layer Opacity 50%.
- **Largest difference from Photoshop, before and after the fold** (levels of 255, white page): Inner Glow 42 to 1 (50%: 21 to 1), an opaque gradient glow 64 to 1, Inner Shadow 42 to 1 (50%: 21 to 1), a shadow at Distance 8 59 to 0, a glow under a shadow 72 to 2, Screen 31 to 1, Color Dodge 25 to 1, Overlay 22 to 1, Multiply 9 to 1, the default Inner Shadow (Multiply 75%, Distance 5, Size 5) 34 to 1, a feathered mask 46 to 1, a square painted at 50% 55 to 0, layer Opacity 50% 33 to 1, a Multiply layer 35 to 1. Every folded cell is within 2 on all four backdrops.
- **Blend Interior Effects as Group**: an Inner Glow is part of the group, an Inner Shadow is not. On a Multiply layer the glow cell changes with the option (up to 65 levels on the blue page) and the shadow cell does not change at all. The fold follows: the glow folds with the overlays and Satin, before or after the layer's blend as the option says; the shadow always folds after the blend.
- **Layers that keep the destination passes** (`render_inner_glow`, `render_inner_shadow`), as for the overlays and Satin: Blend If, a Fill Opacity below 100%, a shape whose effect silhouette is wider than its fill, the vector fill/stroke split under an overlay, a clipping base with prefolded interiors, and pass-through groups. Fill Opacity 0% is exact there (within 1 of Photoshop: nothing of the layer is left to leak). Fill Opacity 50% is 21 levels off on a soft edge (50 in the color over nothing).
- **A half-pixel shadow offset rounds away from zero**: Size 0 shadows on hard squares draw bands of 3 px for Distance 5 at 120, 60, 30 and 150 degrees (2.5 px), and 1, 2 and 4 px for Distances 1, 3 and 7. The float cosine of 60 degrees is a hair under one half, so `prepare_inner_shadow` nudges the product past the tie before rounding; without that the default Inner Shadow sat 1 px off across (11 levels). Offsets below the horizontal are unprobed: Photoshop clamps an angle beyond 180 degrees to 180.
- **Not covered**: a gradient whose two stops are the same color (red to red) draws white in Photoshop; Patchy draws the stop color, and stops one level apart draw red in both.

Tests (tests/core/layer_style_inner_effects_soft_edge_tests.cpp): `compositor_inner_effects_composite_with_the_layer_coverage_once`, `compositor_opaque_gradient_inner_glow_replaces_the_layer_color_on_soft_pixels`, `compositor_inner_effects_ride_the_layer_opacity`, `compositor_inner_glow_size_zero_edge_keeps_inverse_coverage_on_soft_pixels`, `compositor_inner_glow_joins_the_interior_group_and_inner_shadow_does_not`, `deep_compositor_inner_effects_fold_like_8_bit`, and `photoshop_interior_effects_soft_edge_probe_matches_if_available` (both captures, every backdrop). `photoshop_inner_glow_gradient_probes_match_if_available` compares the feathered squares of every document over their whole soft edge.

## Soft mattes under Choke and Precise

How Inner Glow (Softer with a Choke, and Precise) and Inner Shadow with a Choke read a matte that is not hard. Photoshop 2027 captures of November 2026 are in `local-test-fixtures/layer-style-interior-soft-matte` (`make_shapes.py`, `make_shapes2.py`, `probe.jsx`; `model.py` holds the rule against every capture): one green layer of exactly known alpha (hard, blurred and anti-aliased shapes, flat squares of alpha 64 to 254, ramps of 1 to 51 levels a pixel, staircases, blobs that never reach full opacity, corners, thin bars, random alpha) under 39 red effects, saved over nothing, so the red channel is the effect's strength.

- **The distance.** A pixel below alpha 255 is an edge pixel and sits its own coverage `s` away from the edge (0 to 1 px). A fully opaque pixel takes the smallest `|p - q| + s(q)` over the edge pixels `q`. A hard edge gives the plain Euclidean distance (1 for the first pixel inside); the opaque pixel beside a 50% pixel sits 1.5 away. On a ramp falling one level a pixel the glow starts at the column of alpha 254, not at half coverage.
- **Choke** expands the outside to `clamp(R + 1 - d)`: every pixel within `R` of the opaque boundary. A feathered band is outside as a whole, and the band a choked glow or shadow fills starts where the layer becomes opaque. A choke radius of 0 (Choke under about half a pixel of Size) leaves the gray inverse matte, as before.
- **Precise** ramps on the same distance, so a soft pixel glows at nearly full strength whatever its coverage.
- **A layer that never reaches full opacity is all edge.** A flat square of alpha 254 is filled completely by any choke radius of 1 or more, and a Precise glow gives it one value (`d = s`). Photoshop does this in the middle of an opaque shape too: one pixel of alpha 254 there is ringed by the effect.
- **Precise with a Choke** ramps from the choked shape `C` (coverage `clamp(d - R)`): inside `C` the distance is `d`, outside it is `R + 1 - e` with `e` the same distance measured to `C`. Straight edges give `d` either way. A convex corner within the radius is brighter (255 at the corner pixel of a hard square against 213 along its edge at Size 10, Choke 50, Range 100), and a layer the choke swallows whole glows at full strength. Range 50 and below saturate that zone, so only a wider Range computes `e`.
- **Opaque means 255 in 8 bits**: coverage of `254.5/255` and more (`kInteriorSolidCoverage`), so float dust on an opaque matte is not an edge. 16-bit layers follow the same threshold (unprobed in Photoshop).

`for_each_soft_edge_distance` (src/render/layer_style_mask_ops.cpp) computes it exactly: one exact distance transform finds each opaque pixel's nearest edge pixel at distance `D`, the answer lies below `D + 1`, and the offsets of that ring are searched in distance order until none can win. A hard matte ends on its first ring. The result depends on the matte alone, so a render window gives the same values as the whole layer with a padding of Size + 2 (Size + 6 for a Precise glow that computes `e`).

- **Fit** (largest difference from Photoshop in levels of 255, before and after): hard squares 1 before and after, except Precise with Choke 50 at Range 100 (42 to 3, the corners); blurred squares 255 to 4; flat translucent squares 255 to 1; ramps and staircases 255 to 3; anti-aliased discs and rotated squares 46 to 7; random alpha of 200 to 255 255 to 1. The #746 feathered squares: Precise 60 to 2, Softer with Choke 30 151 to 2. `photoshop-inner-glow.psd` (choke on an anti-aliased ellipse, a choke radius over 8): mean 0.29 to 0.10, its two corpus digests re-pinned.
- **What is left** sits on curves, corners and tips: Photoshop's distances are not exactly Euclidean there. A single transparent pixel in an opaque square is ringed up to 7 levels differently at Choke 60 and 9 for Precise with Choke 30 at Range 50. Precise with Choke 60 at Range 75 is 10 off at the corners of a hard L, 18 at a 30 degree tip and 24 around a scattered translucent pixel. The rim of the Choke 100 hard band lands a pixel apart on curves (91 Softer, 70 Precise).
- **Unprobed**: Drop Shadow and Outer Glow Spread on a soft matte (the known difference above is probably this rule's exterior mirror), and Bevel.

Tests (tests/core/layer_style_inner_effects_soft_edge_tests.cpp): `compositor_interior_effects_measure_a_soft_edge_from_its_opaque_boundary`, `compositor_interior_effects_treat_a_translucent_layer_as_all_edge`, `compositor_precise_inner_glow_choke_ramps_from_the_choked_shape`, `compositor_precise_inner_glow_full_choke_is_a_hard_band` (Photoshop's own rows), `interior_choke_on_a_hard_matte_is_the_exact_disc_dilation`, `interior_soft_matte_masks_do_not_depend_on_the_window`, and `photoshop_interior_soft_matte_probe_matches_if_available` / `photoshop_interior_soft_matte_corner_probe_matches_if_available` (both sheets, per region).

## Noise

Quality > Noise (`Nose`, percent) on Drop Shadow, Inner Shadow, Outer Glow and Inner Glow (GitHub issue 742; Photoshop 2027 captures of November 2026 in `local-test-fixtures/layer-style-742/noise`):

- Each pixel adds `noise x v` to the effect's coverage and clamps the sum to 0..1, with `v` spread evenly over -1..1 in 256 steps. This happens before Opacity, after the contour and the Range gain. Flat full coverage at Noise 100 leaves half the pixels untouched and spreads the rest evenly down to zero (mean 74.26% in Photoshop, 75% by the rule); Noise 50 gave 87.99% (87.5%).
- The pattern belongs to the document: a pixel keeps its value when the layer moves, a repeated render gives the same bytes, and Drop Shadow and Outer Glow show the same grain at the same place. Patchy draws `v` from splitmix64 of the pixel's document position (`effect_noise_coverage`, src/render/layer_style_mask_ops.hpp), so strips, tiles and whole renders agree. It reproduces Photoshop's statistics, not its pixels: Photoshop's generator was not identified.
- Zero coverage stays zero. On Drop Shadow and Outer Glow the noisy coverage is also limited to the blurred matte divided by 0.12 (`exterior_glow_opacity`, the limit the glow contours use), so the grain never reaches past the blur. The two interior effects have no such limit: any pixel the falloff reaches can jump to full strength.
- On a gradient Outer Glow, Noise moves the opacity only. The stop position, and so the color, is the same as without Noise.
- Noise 0 takes the code path it always did and renders the same bytes.

Tests: `layer_effect_noise_matches_photoshop_statistics_and_is_document_anchored`, `layer_effect_noise_stays_inside_each_effect`, `psd_layer_effect_noise_round_trips`. Unprobed: Noise with a shaped contour (Patchy applies the contour first), and with the Precise technique.

## Reference cells

Photoshop reference measurements use 80 by 80 pixel cells with a gray square on white. Four curves (Cone, a double peak, an asymmetric rise and a descending curve) cover sizes 7 and 12, Range 50% and 75%, and Anti-aliased off and on. Across the four cells per effect, the largest mean absolute errors on the 0 to 255 scale were 0.039 for Inner Shadow, 0.090 for Inner Glow and 0.164 for Outer Glow. The largest single-pixel errors were 1, 3 and 3 respectively. The reference pairs are `layer-style-735/contours` and `layer-style-735/asymmetric` in the local fixtures.
