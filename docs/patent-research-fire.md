# Patent research: procedural fire (Filter > Render > Flame)

Engineering record behind the Lens Flare and Flame rule in
[legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md#filters-and-smart-objects); the design is in
[stylize-render-filters.md](stylize-render-filters.md#flame). Searched 2026-10-28 (web and
Google Patents): Adobe flame and path patents, procedural fire and flame texture patents,
flames along curves or strokes.

## Findings

- **No patent on Photoshop's Flame filter turned up.** That filter (2014) renders flames along
  a user's path; searches by Adobe as assignee with flame, fire and path terms found only
  tutorials and unrelated path-editing patents (path modification and extension, remembered
  path properties).
- **US 10997770 B1** (Adobe, "Automated animation of fonts", filed 2020-05-05, to about
  2040-05-05). Every independent claim computes glyph point positions at time points by an
  effect function and generates keyframes for a font animation; claim 18 modifies only the
  glyph segments whose direction meets a condition, per keyframe. Flame is a still-image
  filter: it makes no keyframes or time points and does not modify glyph segments; it places
  heat fields along a flattened path. Watch item: an animated Flame (frames over time) or
  flames generated by rewriting glyph outline segments needs a new check against this patent.
- **US 7626590 B2** (Samsung, procedural textures for natural phenomena including flame, from
  shared and phenomenon-specific parameters): listed expired (fee related).
- Prior art for the techniques used: value noise and fractal sums (Perlin 1985), domain warping
  of noise coordinates, flame skeletons along curves for 2D fire (Yu and Patterson 1996),
  black-body style color ramps.

## Design boundary

Flame stays a still-image procedural filter: hashed tongue roots along a base line or a
flattened path, fixed teardrop heat fields, shared value-noise sway and erosion, a fixed color
ramp per preset, box-blur glow, noise smoke and hashed sparks. No keyframes, frame sequences or
time-varying output, no editing of glyph or path segments, and no reproduction of Photoshop's
flame types, presets or dialog layout without a new check.
