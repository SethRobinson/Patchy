# Performance: compositor and filter kernels

Split from [performance.md](performance.md): the compositor's fast paths (mask coverage plane, float row kernels, MSVC link-time optimization), the byte-identity corpus that pins their output, the remaining slow paths and why GPU compositing stays unpursued, and the shared Gaussian line filter.

## Compositor row kernels, mask plane, and the byte-identity corpus

The per-pixel general loop in `composite_pixel_layer` (render/layer_compositor.hpp) is the compositor's slow path: a layer falls off the integer scanline fast path the moment it has a mask, a non-Normal blend mode, or any advanced feature, where throughput falls from ~860 Mpx/s to ~30 Mpx/s. These fast paths cut it without changing a single output byte:

- **Mask coverage plane.** `build_mask_coverage_plane` folds the raster and vector mask factors over the draw rect in one row-walked pass, replacing the per-pixel `layer_mask_alpha_for_render` call chain (which paid two bounds-validated `PixelBuffer::pixel()` calls per pixel). The plane's expressions keep `layer_mask_alpha_at`'s exact float operation order, so values are bit-identical; the general loop reads the plane for every masked layer on every target.
- **`composite_blended_row` row kernel** (QImageCompositeTarget, ui/qimage_composite_target.hpp). When no per-pixel gate beyond coverage remains (no blend-if, satin, folded overlays, knockout, special fill, or backdrop pre-blend; Dissolve excluded), the base pass runs as a row walk whose arithmetic is a verbatim replica of `composite_color` + `composite_blended_rgb` (mode dispatch through the real `blend_rgb`). NEVER reuse `composite_source_row`'s integer math for such kernels: the integer and float paths round differently by design, and pinned bytes hold only because the replica keeps the exact float expression trees.
- **MSVC link-time optimization.** CMakeLists.txt owns Release flags ([building.md](building.md#msvc-release-codegen)); `/fp:precise` keeps corpus bytes unchanged. Compile time: each compositor target is instantiated in one TU (`PATCHY_COMPOSITOR_TARGET_INSTANTIATIONS`, code-organization-modules.md). Without LTCG (opt-dev) the `layer_compositor.cpp` helpers are real calls: compare the default `patchy_perf_tests` run before and after moving one out of the header.
- **Float row kernels on the group and flatten targets.** `IsolatedClipGroupTarget` (layer_compositor.hpp) and `Rgb8PixelBufferTarget` (render/composite_targets.hpp) expose `composite_blended_row`, so isolated/masked/non-passthrough groups, clip runs, and PSD flatten leave the general per-pixel loop for gate-free layers. Each kernel is a float-exact transcription of that target's `record_clip_coverage` + `composite_color` chain (the tier dispatch never calls `record_clip_coverage`, so the isolated kernel replicates it internally, including the frozen clip-matte gate and max-alpha update; the blend reads the CLAMPED destination alpha while the plane update reads the stored value). Neither target gets an integer `composite_source_row`: byte identity is against the float general loop, and tier-1-eligible layers fall through to the float kernel automatically (tier 1's conditions are a strict subset of tier 2's).

Guarding all of it, the **byte-identity corpus**: `composite_corpus_flatten_digests_are_stable` (tests/core/composite_corpus_tests.cpp) and `composite_corpus_render_digests_are_stable` plus the override-equivalence test (tests/ui/composite_render_tests.cpp) flatten/render every committed `test-fixtures/psd` document plus anything dropped into `local-test-fixtures/composite-corpus/`, single-threaded, and compare FNV-1a digests against the tracked baselines in `test-fixtures/psd/*-digests.txt` (plus a local overlay). For known optional documents, `test-fixtures/composite-corpus/*-digests.txt` pins deliberate rendering changes per checkout. The pinball poster uses custom Satin contours. Substituting Linear for its Satin curves reproduces its earlier flatten digest exactly. Any compositor optimization must keep these green; after a DELIBERATE rendering change, delete the tracked files, rerun, and commit the diff. Isolated-group bounds are override-aware (`layer_render_bounds_for_render`): move/transform preview overrides do not force full-clip isolation buffers, and the styled pass-through silhouette includes override-moved children.

Remaining slow paths (future tiers): `merge_layer_into` and the channel-restricted/`GroupMaskedTarget` wrappers (deliberately per-pixel: fusing their factors into a kernel row would change float association order), the three float divides per pixel (skipped only at output alpha 1.0), exact-value SIMD for the row kernels, a thread pool replacing the per-strip `std::async` spawns, and display-resolution interactive compositing. GPU compositing stays deliberately unpursued: byte identity with the CPU reference is effectively unachievable on GPU float paths, and a preview-only GPU tier comes only after the CPU tiers are exhausted.

## Gaussian line filters

Gaussian Blur, High Pass, Unsharp Mask (RGBA8, smart_filter_renderer.cpp) and the 16/32-bit plane filter (`filter_plane_with_photoshop_kernel`) share one separable line filter, `filter_gaussian_lines`: it filters several interleaved lines at once (the channels of a row, or every channel of a block of columns), giving each lane exactly a lone line's operations in order, so output never depends on lane count. Rows, then 16-column blocks (64 for one-channel planes), fan out through `run_filter_blocks`; the column pass reads a 64-byte slice of the byte-rounded row plane per row instead of striding a whole row per sample. `gaussian_line_filters_match_pinned_bytes` pins 152 digests taken from the earlier single-threaded, one-channel-at-a-time code; any change to these filters keeps them. `patchy_perf_tests.exe gaussian` times a 6000x4000 image (`PATCHY_PERF_GAUSSIAN_SIZE=WxH`): about 0.2 s per RGBA8 Gaussian at any radius on a 32-thread box (GitHub issue 564).

## Strip renders against the one-pass walk

The one-pass walk (`PATCHY_RENDER_SINGLE_THREADED=1`) is the reference; the canvas shows strip renders and region patches. Every render window (a strip, a patch, the canvas) must give a pixel the same value, so an effect may read only its neighborhood, or else data that does not depend on the window. `patchy_perf_tests stripcheck` (`PATCHY_PERF_MANYLAYERS_PSD=<psd>`) renders a document both ways, then each visible leaf alone and each of a differing leaf's effect kinds alone, printing the differing pixels, the largest channel difference and their extent; it reports only. `movesweep` with `PATCHY_PERF_VERIFY_FINAL=1` compares the canvas with a threaded and a one-pass render after every drag.

Two effects read past their window, found November 22, 2026 on the 4000x2781 pinball poster (16 strips of 174 rows; 5.3 M pixels differed by up to 44/255, and a committed Move patch differed from a fresh render by 17/255). Both predate the patch-strip work and are fixed:

- **Component strengths.** A spread radius past the exact dilation (over 8 px) and the Precise falloff expand the matte with a band whose strength is the largest alpha of the pixel's connected shape (`layer_style_source_strengths`, a flood fill). A window of a layer wider than the canvas holds only part of each shape: the poster's two "GRID" layers (12597 and 13484 px wide, Outer Glow size 63, spread 17) got a different glow in every window, up to 36/255. `layer_matte_strengths_for_window` (layer_compositor.hpp) now hands a windowed Drop Shadow or Outer Glow mask the strengths of the layer's whole matte, from one plane over the layer's visible bounds cached as `StyleMaskKind::SourceStrengths` under the layer's content revision (computed per call without a provider, as in a core flatten). A window that already holds every painted pixel takes none, so every render of a layer inside the canvas keeps its bytes; the tracked corpus digests did not move.
- **Stacked Strokes.** Several Stroke effects knock each other out through the list `composite_pixel_layer` resolves before the base pass. That list was built only when the clip touched the layer's own pixels, so a strip that started just below a layer drew its lower strokes without the knockout: the poster's "ARCADE copy" layers (Strokes of 11 and 10 px) differed by up to 140/255 in the 2 to 8 rows between their bottom edge and the next strip boundary. The list is now resolved whenever effects are visible; the knockout plane still covers only the layer's pixels.

After both, the poster renders the same bytes threaded and one-pass, whole and leaf by leaf, and after every `movesweep` drag. Pinned by `effect_windows_match_the_whole_layer` (a lattice wider than the canvas that fades along both axes, and plates with two strokes at every strip-boundary offset).

Still window-dependent, measured or known, not fixed:

- A Stroke below full opacity rounds one level differently in some windows: 1,470 pixels at 1/255 in that test, with or without the fixes above.
- The interior choke over 8 px (Inner Shadow, Inner Glow) flood-fills the inverse matte of its window, and a continuous Drop Shadow the swept matte. Neither showed on the poster.
- A Stroke's contour is supersampled only when its matte is under 1024x1024 (`stroke_subpixel_distance_fields`), which for a layer that crosses the canvas edge is the window's size.
