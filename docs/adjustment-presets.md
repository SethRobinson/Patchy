# Adjustment dialog presets

Shipped presets, the user's own presets, and the dialog layout of the Hue/Saturation, Levels, Curves, Color Balance and Black & White dialogs (#588, #589, #591, #592, #528). Code: `src/ui/adjustment_presets.{hpp,cpp}` (store, shipped Hue/Saturation and Levels presets, `AdjustmentPresetButtons`), `src/ui/adjustment_dialogs.cpp` (dialogs, `AdjustmentPresetCombo`, `GradientTrackSlider`), `src/formats/photoshop_adjustment_presets.{hpp,cpp}` (.ahu, .alv, .blw). Curves presets: `src/ui/curves_presets.cpp`; Black & White presets: `builtin_black_white_presets` ([adjustment-kinds.md](adjustment-kinds.md)).

## User presets

- One file per preset in the kind's Photoshop format, named after the preset, in `<app data>/adjustment-presets/<folder>`. Folder names are permanent: `hue-saturation` (.ahu), `levels` (.alv), `curves` (.acv), `black-white` (.blw). `PATCHY_ADJUSTMENT_PRESETS_DIR` replaces the root; the UI suite points it at `test-artifacts/adjustment-presets/<pid>`.
- `user_adjustment_preset_path` turns a name into a file name: `<>:"/\|?*` and control characters become `_`, surrounding spaces and dots go, Windows device names get a leading `_`. The list is the folder's files of that suffix, sorted case-insensitively; a file that does not read is left out.
- `AdjustmentPresetButtons` (Save..., Delete, Import..., Export...; object names `<prefix>SavePresetButton` and so on): Save asks for a name (`adjustmentPresetNameDialog`) and asks before replacing; Delete asks first and is enabled only on a user preset; Import checks the file, then copies its bytes into the folder under the file's name (a number is added on a clash) through `write_file_bytes_atomically` and applies it; Export writes the current settings. Arrangements: a row (Hue/Saturation, Levels, Curves) or a column (Black & White). Every button is as wide as the widest; a row fills the width its layout gives it, split evenly, so under a preset combo it spans the combo edge to edge (#588).
- A just-saved or imported preset stays selected while the settings equal it, even when a shipped preset holds the same values; otherwise shipped presets win the match.

## Formats

The three new files hold exactly the PSD adjustment block payload, so they share the PSD codec ([psd-layer-blocks.md](psd-layer-blocks.md#adjustment-presets)). Verified on Photoshop 2026 over COM: its `Lvls`, `HStr` and `BanW` events with `presetKindCustom` and a `Usng` path applied Patchy's exported files (and a 100-byte .ahu without the trailer); on five probe colors per file Photoshop's result was within 1 level of Patchy's (November 2, 2026). Photoshop has no scripted way to save a preset, so the read fixtures in `local-test-fixtures/adjustment-presets/` are block payloads Photoshop wrote into a PSD; see its README.

## Shipped presets

Names and values are Patchy's own; none repeats a Photoshop preset's values (checked against the values known to overlap, never by opening Photoshop's preset files; [legal-licensing.md](legal-licensing.md)). Ids are permanent.

- Hue/Saturation (`builtin_hue_saturation_presets`): `hue_saturation.default`; colorize `sepia_wash` (32, 28, 0), `blueprint` (200, 40, 0), `golden_mono` (42, 45, 0), `moonlight` (222, 30, -5); master `faded_film`, `warm_vintage`, `vivid_film`, `soft_pastel`, `cool_shift`, `warm_shift`; one range each `warm_skin` (Reds), `autumn_leaves` (Greens), `aqua_skies` (Blues), `deep_ocean` (Cyans). A range preset switches Edit to its range.
- Levels (`builtin_levels_presets`): `levels.default`, `brighten_slightly`/`medium`/`strong` (gamma 1.10, 1.30, 1.55), `darken_slightly`/`medium`/`strong` (0.90, 0.80, 0.65), `slight_contrast`, `medium_contrast`, `strong_contrast`, `dramatic_contrast`, `deep_blacks`, `crushed_blacks`, `brilliant_whites`, `silhouette`, and with output levels `faded_light`/`medium`/`strong`, `matte`, `washed_out`. Matching ignores the channel tab.
- The Preset combo (`AdjustmentPresetCombo`: Hue/Saturation, Levels, Curves) lists Custom (selected when nothing matches), the shipped presets, a separator and the user's presets (item data `user:<name>`). Given an icon maker (Curves: `curves_adjustment_thumbnail` at 30x20), every entry shows its settings as an icon and Custom the current ones.

## Dialog layout

- Shared (#588, #591, #592): a separator line under the Save/Delete/Import/Export buttons (`<prefix>PresetSeparator`, Levels too); Preview at the left of the OK/Cancel line, at the bottom right, in every dialog that has one (the generic builder, Curves, Levels); OK as wide as Cancel everywhere. Controls stacked under `GradientTrackSlider`s (Preset, Edit, Colorize, Preserve Luminosity) are indented by `kGradientTrackInset` (5 px, where the painted track starts) through `track_aligned_row`; tracks are at least 180 px.
- Hue/Saturation: form rows Preset, the button row (in the field column, indented like the combo), the separator, Edit, Hue:, Saturation:, Lightness:, Colorize with Reset at the right (`hueSaturationResetButton`: the triple the sliders show back to its defaults, zeros or the Colorize 0/25/0; Colorize stays as it is). An Edit entry whose hue, saturation or lightness is not zero shows `*` after its name (item text only; indices are the ranges). The Edit combo stays a combo (no tabs: seven are too wide). Hue, Saturation and Lightness are `GradientTrackSlider`s: hue is the wheel around the edited range's centre (red for Master, 0 to 360 for Colorize), saturation runs from gray to the shifted hue, lightness from black through it to white. The swatch beside Colorize (`hueSaturationColorizeSwatch`) shows what Colorize makes of a mid gray, through the core transfer.
- Levels, top to bottom (#589 follow-up): the Preset form row (the label column is `preset_caption_column_width`, the widest Hue/Saturation caption, which the Hue/Saturation Preset label also takes, so both combos start at the same x; follow-up 2), the button row under the combo, the separator, the channel tabs, "Input Levels:" with Auto at its right, the input graph and fields, the output range and fields, then Preview with OK/Cancel. A tab whose channel's record is off the defaults shows `*` after its name (text only; the swatch icons stay). OK, Cancel and Auto share one width. Red, green and blue squares on the channel tabs (`set_channel_tab_swatches`, Curves too); B:, G:, W: fields at 40 instead of 64, each with an eyedropper (`levelsBlackPointButton`, `levelsGrayPointButton`, `levelsWhitePointButton`) on the Curves canvas hooks. Over the canvas every point picker (Levels and Curves) shows the plain `eyedropper_cursor()`, with no foreground/background badge, and the active tool's outline hides (GitHub issue 638); Curves' on-image drag keeps the cross. A pick sets every color channel's record (`levels_adjustment_from_eyedropper_samples`: sampled black and white inputs, and the gamma that takes the gray sample to mid-gray, `gamma = ln(x) / ln(0.5)`); the composite record is kept.
- Levels clipping view (#687): Alt held during a drag of the black input handle shows the Curves Shadows clipping view on the canvas (`CurvesDialogHooks::clipping_changed`, `CanvasWidget::set_curves_clipping_preview`), the white handle shows Highlights, and a color tab passes its channel. The gray handle and the output handles show none. It ends when Alt or the mouse button is let go and when the dialog closes; Alt pressed again mid-drag brings it back. `LevelsInputGraph` reads Alt from each mouse event and, for the length of a drag only, from an application event filter (Alt key events go to the focus widget, not the graph). The view reads the composite, so the dialog previews the adjustment while it shows even with Preview unticked, and every Levels dialog has it (Image > Adjustments, a new or re-edited adjustment layer, a clipped one, a Smart Filter). Tests: `ui_levels_alt_drag_input_handles_report_clipping`, `ui_levels_alt_drag_shows_canvas_clipping_view`.
- Clipping view cost (#687 follow-up, a 6000x4000 photo lagged about a second per move): a background canvas refresh (`start_async_render_cache_refresh`) builds the clipping image and its mip chain on its worker for the mode and channel that showed when it started (`AsyncCanvasFrame::clipping`), and every landed frame installs it, intermediate frames included (`install_async_clipping_preview`; before, an intermediate frame kept the stale view until the drag paused). A mode or channel change since then rebuilds it on the GUI thread. `render_curves_clipping_preview(..., workers)` is a per-component table over row bands, byte-identical for any worker count; GUI-thread rebuilds and the clipping mips use the banded forms. Alt pressed while a background refresh is coming no longer recomposites synchronously. With Preview unticked a drag sends no preview: `AdjustmentPreviewGate` (filter_workflows_internal.hpp) drops a disabled request that follows another, and `make_destructive_adjustment_preview_state` skips restoring a layer that still holds its original pixels, so no refresh and no busy notice run. Curves' destructive render fans out over row spans like Levels. Timing: `patchy_perf_tests levelsclip` (`PATCHY_PERF_LEVELSCLIP_SIZE=WxH`). Tests: `ui_curves_clipping_preview_bands_match_reference`, `ui_levels_and_curves_preview_off_drags_send_no_preview`, `ui_levels_large_image_alt_drag_follows_handle_and_preview_off_stays_idle`.
- Color Balance: Shadows/Midtones/Highlights tabs (`colorBalanceToneTabs`) instead of the Tone combo; tracks cyan to red, magenta to green, yellow to blue; tab order tabs, slider/field pairs, Preserve Luminosity, Reset, Preview, buttons; Reset (`colorBalanceResetButton`) sits at the right of the Preserve Luminosity line, under the Yellow/Blue field, and restores the settings the dialog opened with.
- Curves, top to bottom: the Preset form row (`curvesPresetCombo`, indented like Levels'; #592 follow-up 3 replaced the thumbnail strip), the button row under the combo, the separator, the canvas tools (Target to Both), the channel tabs, then the graph with a column on its right (`CurvesEditorWidget`: Input:/Output: labels above their fields, a gap, Auto, Reset, and the dialog's Before through `add_column_button`; one width), then Preview with OK/Cancel. Load/Save became Import.../Export.... The graph's minimum height (160 px) is below the column's, so at the dialog's minimum size the graph ends level with Before; it grows with the dialog and stays square inside its area.
- Black & White: a "My Presets" section (shown once there are any) at the end of the strip, and the button column right of the strip. The strip shows a header and one row of tiles and is never shorter than the column; when the row is taller, the column's spacing grows so Export... ends at the strip's bottom (#528 follow-up 2). Field labels end with a colon; what the bars beside the sliders show is in their tooltips. No Auto ([legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md)).
- Track and swatch colors are content (what the control picks), the same in both schemes; frames and handles use theme roles.

## Image > Adjustments

Hue/Saturation and Color Balance run through `direct_adjustment_dialog` like the #528 kinds: the preview covers the hue ranges and OK writes the layer's pixels in one undo step.
