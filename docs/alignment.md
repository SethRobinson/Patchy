# Alignment guides and Align/Distribute

The Move tool's visible alignment snapping (Photoshop's Smart Guides) and the Layer > Arrange > Align / Distribute commands with their options-bar buttons. Read this before changing snap targets, the guide overlay, the Align To rule, or the alignment scripting API. Legal boundaries live in the "Alignment guides and Align/Distribute" section of [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md); the research record is [patent-research-alignment.md](patent-research-alignment.md).

## Snapping engine and targets

- One walker feeds every snap path (`CanvasWidget::collect_snap_candidates`, canvas_widget_guides.cpp): guides, document bounds and center, selection bounds and center, then layers, in that order. `snapped_document_point` (guide drags, pen and shape points), `snapped_rect_delta` (crop, selection move), `snapped_marquee_current_point`, and the Move tool's `snapped_move_delta_with_matches` all read it; the grid is still applied inline because it is a rounding, not a line. The selection is skipped while a Replace marquee drag-out or a handle resize is rewriting it on every move (`selection_is_live_gesture_output`); otherwise the rect snaps back to its own previous position and advances in tolerance-sized jumps. Ties resolve to the LAST candidate at equal distance (`<=`), so within the layer set the bottom-most equal candidate wins, as before September 2026.
- A layer target is the rect the Move tool itself shows for that layer (`move_layer_outline_bounds`: the opaque raster extent through the `pixel_revision`-keyed alpha-bounds cache, or a text layer's frame), falling back to `layer_render_bounds` for layers without a Move rect (16-bit or gray pixels, adjustments, locked smart objects), so those keep snapping. Before September 2026 every layer contributed its effect-padded render bounds, so a drop shadow or a transparent margin pulled edges to the wrong line. Folders only recurse (their children are the objects a user lines up with); hidden trees and zero-opacity layers contribute nothing.
- The moving set is excluded from its own targets (`exclude_ids`), so a layer no longer snaps back to its start position when its edge crosses its own former center.
- Every feature of every moving layer (both edges and the center per axis) is a snap source regardless of where the drag was grabbed. This is a legal boundary, not a convenience (Adobe US 9250786 claims grab-point-dependent source features). Tolerance stays `kSnapToleranceScreenPixels` (8 screen px) divided by zoom, and the scan stays a plain linear comparison of edge coordinates.
- `MoveSnapResult` reports the winning `SnapMatch` per axis: kind (Guide, Grid, Document, Selection, Layer), the matched document coordinate, `source_span` (the union of moving rects that carry the winning feature, at the snapped delta) and `target_span` (the matched layer rect, the selection rect, or the document). `snapped_move_delta` is the plain wrapper the release commit still calls.

## Alignment guide overlay

- `mouseMoveEvent`'s Move branch stores the matches in `move_snap_x_` / `move_snap_y_` right where it updates the drag readout, then `update_move_snap_guides_region` repaints the union of the previous and next guide rects (clipped to the viewport; a document-edge line at deep zoom is enormous). Never a bare `update()`: the bounded drag repaints in [interactive-previews.md](interactive-previews.md) are a contract.
- Shift constraint: the constrained delta is snapped, then constrained again, which zeroes any correction the pinned axis received. A match is kept only when the final delta component equals the snap result's component, so the pinned axis shows no guide.
- Only Document, Selection, and Layer matches draw (guides draw themselves, the grid has no single line). `draw_move_snap_guides` (canvas_widget_draw_tools.cpp) runs in `paintEvent` right after `draw_guides_overlay` and before the document border and the selection ants: a 1 px cosmetic line in `theme().canvas_snap_guide` (magenta in Dark, restated in Light because `flip_for_light` pulls saturated magenta toward a mid tone), pixel-aligned like the guides, spanning the two bridged extents plus a 4 px overhang each end. No end ticks, no distance badges.
- `clear_move_snap_guides` runs from exactly the Move teardown sites: the release commit (next to `clear_drag_readout`), `cancel_pointer_gestures` (focus loss, document swap), `set_tool`, and `set_edit_locked`. It is deliberately NOT called from `cancel_move_preview`, which is the async snapshot-worker cancel and runs mid-drag. A Move drag has no Escape branch, so there is nothing to clear there.
- The guide color is a theme role, not user data (unlike the saved grid and guide colors). Making it a preference is a deliberate future change.

## Snap checkbox

`moveSnapCheck` on the Move tool's options bar mirrors View > Snap (`view.snap`, Ctrl+Shift+;): the checkbox calls `view_snap_action_->setChecked`, and one connection from the action's `toggled` pushes the state back under `QSignalBlocker`. Preferences accept and `load_view_settings` already call `setChecked` on the action, so there is no other sync point and no new settings key (`view/snapEnabled` is the one persisted value). Targets stay under View > Snap To and Preferences > Snapping.

## Align and Distribute commands

- Menu placement: nested under Layer > Arrange as `layerAlignMenu` and `layerDistributeMenu`, because the Layer menu sits at its 23-row cap (`ui_main_window_renders_color_controls`). The Align submenu ends with the exclusive Align To pair (Selection or Canvas). The choice is session-only and every launch starts at Selection (Seth, September 2026; the retired `tools/alignTo` key is neither read nor written). `set_align_to_canvas` checks the chosen action with its signals live so the `QActionGroup` unchecks the other: checking under a `QSignalBlocker` left the group's current action stale and let both entries show checked. Command ids (persisted, unbound): `layer.align_left`, `layer.align_horizontal_centers`, `layer.align_right`, `layer.align_top`, `layer.align_vertical_centers`, `layer.align_bottom`, `layer.distribute_left`, `layer.distribute_horizontal_centers`, `layer.distribute_right`, `layer.distribute_top`, `layer.distribute_vertical_centers`, `layer.distribute_bottom`, `layer.distribute_horizontal_spacing`, `layer.distribute_vertical_spacing`, `layer.align_to_selection`, `layer.align_to_canvas`. Align To persists as `tools/alignTo` (`selection` or `canvas`).
- The Move tool's options bar MIRRORS the same QActions: six icon-only buttons (`moveAlignLeftButton` ... `moveAlignBottomButton`, `optionsBarButton` style, glyphs from `align_edge_icon` in action_icons.cpp) copy the action's icon, tooltip, and enabled state through `QAction::changed` and trigger it on click. They are deliberately not `setDefaultAction` wrappers: `refresh_options_bar` hides and re-enables a button's default action along with the tool row, which would blank the Layer > Arrange entries whenever Move is not the active tool. Buttons carrying the `optionsBarMirrorsAction` property are skipped by that refresh's enable pass. The "..." `InstantPopup` button (`moveAlignMoreButton`, the `optionsBarMenuButton` QSS property pins it to the 20 px height the brush smoothing button uses) whose menu carries the eight Distribute actions and the Align To pair. Tooltips and shortcut chips come from `refresh_action_tooltip`.
- Units (`CanvasWidget::alignment_units`): the selected roots after `root_drop_layer_ids`, or the active layer with nothing selected. A selected folder is ONE unit (its movable leaves move together by one delta, the union of their Move rects is its box), like Photoshop aligning a group as a block. Leaf rects use the Move rect with the render-bounds fallback; position-locked leaves and non-movable pixels are skipped through the same walk as `movable_layer_ids` (`collect_movable_leaf_ids`). Units with no leaf or no rect are dropped.
- Reference rect (Photoshop's rule): the selection bounding rect when a marquee exists and Align To is Selection; the document when Align To is Canvas or only one unit exists; otherwise the units' union.
- Geometry is Qt-free in `src/core/layer_alignment.{hpp,cpp}`: `compute_align_deltas` and `compute_distribute_deltas` over `Rect`, centers as `x + width / 2.0`, every result rounded with `std::lround`, negative (pasteboard) coordinates legal. Distribute needs three or more units: feature modes sort by the feature, keep the first and last, and place the intermediates evenly; spacing modes sort by the leading edge, keep the outermost units, and share one gap, which goes negative (even overlap) when the units are wider than the span.
- Mutation: `CanvasWidget::offset_layers(deltas, label, record_history)` is the shared body of arrow-key nudges (`move_active_layer_by` is now a wrapper) and Align/Distribute: one history entry ("Align Layers" / "Distribute Layers"), the smart-filter re-render rollback dance, styled-ancestor dirty padding, `set_bounds` plus `translate_moved_layer_metadata`. Null deltas are skipped; all-null pushes nothing and the status bar says the layers are already aligned. `record_history = false` lets the script route ride the run's single snapshot.
- Refusals (`MainWindow::refuse_layer_alignment_command`): no document, a preview-dialog edit lock, a pending Free Transform / Warp / path transform (the shared "Finish the transform first" message), a live pointer gesture ("Finish the current drag before aligning layers"), and the channel views. Fewer units than needed reports "Select a movable layer to align" / "Select at least three layers to distribute". The 14 verb ids also join the channel-view disable list.
- Enable state: `refresh_layer_alignment_action_states` (Align with at least one unit, Distribute with three) runs beside `refresh_combine_shapes_action_states` on every selection change and layer-control refresh; `alignment_unit_count` counts roots without measuring rects.

## Scripting

`doc.alignLayers(edge, options?)` and `doc.distributeLayers(mode, options?)` on the document object. `edge` is `"left" | "hcenter" | "right" | "top" | "vcenter" | "bottom"`; `mode` adds `"hspacing" | "vspacing"` (permanent ids in `align_edge_id` / `distribute_mode_id`). Options: `layers` (an array of this document's layer objects; default the layer selection) and, for Align, `alignTo` (`"selection"` default or `"canvas"`). Unknown options, bad ids, too few units, and a pending transform throw. Both return the number of layers moved and ride the run's single undo entry through `ScriptEngineHost::align_layers` / `distribute_layers` (`prepare_mutation`, then the canvas call with `record_history = false`). `app.runCommand("layer.align_left")` reaches the same commands through their ids.
