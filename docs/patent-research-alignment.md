# Patent research: alignment guides and Align/Distribute

Research record for the Move tool's visible alignment snapping and the Layer > Arrange > Align / Distribute commands (feature design in [alignment.md](alignment.md)). The binding summary is the "Alignment guides and Align/Distribute" section of [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md); this file is an engineering record of the design and of the published patents considered, and follows the conventions of [patent-research.md](patent-research.md).

- **Alignment guides and Align/Distribute (2026-09-22, Google Patents status reads)**: static Align and Distribute commands are decades-old prior art (Illustrator's Align palette in the 1990s, Photoshop 6's Align Linked in 2000). Edge and center alignment guides that appear while dragging are prior art too: Illustrator 8 Smart Guides (1998), Keynote (2003), and Apple US 7545392 ("Dynamic guides", priority 2003-05-30, Expired - Lifetime 2023-05-30; claim 1 is guide visibility indicating overlap of a selected object's alignment guides with unselected objects', updated on movement). Active patents examined and the design choice each one fixes:
  - Adobe US 10782861 ("Objects alignment and distribution layout", priority 2016-06-29, Active, to 2036-06-29): claim 1 positions a repositioned object equidistant between at least two objects, or at a distance equal to a space between two objects, as part of the reposition input; claim 15 adds displayed positive space and numerical distance measurements. Patchy therefore never snaps to equal spacing during a drag and shows no drag-time distance badges. Static Distribute is a one-shot command with no reposition input.
  - Adobe US 9250786 ("Snapping of object features via dragging", priority 2013-07-16, Active, to 2034-03-17): claim 1 determines the SOURCE feature of the dragged object from the drag-initiation location and snaps that feature to a target feature found during the drag. Patchy considers every edge and center of every moving layer regardless of grab point (what `snapped_move_delta` always did); keep it that way. All 19 claims were read on 2026-11-13 (USPTO Patent Public Search text) for the polygon center point ([patent-research-shape-handles.md](patent-research-shape-handles.md#center-point-snapping-github-695-follow-up)). Its continuation US 10061496 (filed 2015-12-18, granted 2018-08-28; 20 years from 2013-07-16 plus a 394-day adjustment gives 2034-08-14, fees not checked) has the same shortest-distance step in claims 1, 12 and 14 without the unsnapping and repeated-target steps; the same rule keeps Patchy apart from both.
  - Title search of 2026-11-13 (USPTO Patent Public Search, Adobe as assignee with "snapping" in the title, 19 patents). Read that day, independent claims: US 12585375 (filed 2022-08-12: alignment guides limited to the objects in a region the user encloses with a second selection gesture), US 12554387 (filed 2023-12-19: snapping guides left out for objects that interface panels cover), US 10319129 (filed 2017-01-27: snapping lines generated from collinear glyph bounding-box lines), US 10762674 and US 6993709 (both in [patent-research-shape-handles.md](patent-research-shape-handles.md)). Patchy has no second region selection, no panel-coverage test and no glyph snapping lines. Listed and not read: mirror snapping during vector drawing (US 10216389, US 10705700), angular snapping guides for artboards (US 10540741, US 11250607, US 11967010), glyph snapping (US 10839139, US 11367231), snapping to similar path geometries (US 12423889), three-dimensional alignment (US 11204679). Patchy has none of those features; read the matching claims before adding one.
  - Adobe US 9442623 ("Guide line snapping", priority 2008-06-19, Active, to 2033-10-23): claim 1 computes a perpendicular distance from a cursor guide line through the cursor to a reference point, filters candidate guide lines by their own reference distances within a tolerance range (sorted multimaps, binary search), and displays the snapping location separately from the cursor. Patchy's plain linear scan of edge coordinates has none of these elements; do not introduce a reference-point distance index.
  - Microsoft US 9323436 ("Utilizing drawing guides in determining the display of smart guides", priority 2012-04-05, Active, to 2032-04-05): equal-spacing smart guides that treat a user guide as a drawing object. Absent (no equal-spacing guides at all).
  - Adobe US 11562488 (application US 2022/0122258, "Image content snapping guidelines", priority 2020-10-15, granted 2023-01-24, to 2041): snapping guidelines derived from objects SEGMENTED inside a raster (bounding borders, detected edges and corners). Patchy uses layer opaque bounds only and never detects objects inside a layer.
  - Published earlier work describing the same technique: Keynote 2003 alignment guides, Illustrator 8 (1998), Fireworks, Visio, PowerPoint smart guides (2010). No Adobe UI trade-dress enforcement was found; the guide color and glyphs are Patchy's own.
