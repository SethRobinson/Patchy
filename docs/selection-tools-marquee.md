# Marquee tools: drag-out and resize handles

Split from [selection-tools.md](selection-tools.md): the Rectangular and Elliptical Marquee drag-out modifiers, the resize handles of a committed marquee, and their tests (what every selection tool shares, such as drag inside to move and per-tool Feather and Anti-alias, stays in the parent).

## Rectangular and Elliptical Marquee: drag-out, move, and resize handles

The drag-out lives in `CanvasWidget::mousePressEvent` / `mouseMoveEvent` / `mouseReleaseEvent` (canvas_widget_events.cpp) with the geometry in `marquee_selection_rect` (canvas_widget_selection.cpp): Shift squares a new shape or adds to an existing one, Alt draws from the center with no selection, else subtracts, Space repositions the rect in flight, and both Shift and Alt are read per move and per key event mid-drag (`update_selection_square_constraint`, `update_marquee_from_center`): Alt pressed after the drag starts mirrors the rect about the press point, releasing it re-anchors at the corner, and Alt held from the press over a selection stays Subtract until it is released and pressed again, the same latch Shift uses for Add (GitHub issue 78, `ui_marquee_alt_mid_drag_draws_from_center`; on Windows, Alt+Space mid-drag reaches the canvas as Space through `canvas_alt_space_filter.cpp`, not the system menu; [platform-sites.md](platform-sites.md)), and the options-bar Style (Normal / Fixed Ratio / Fixed Size), Radius, Feather, and Anti-alias shape the commit. `marquee_selection_rect` is one of three deliberately unmerged drag-rect twins (see [tools.md](tools.md)).

A committed marquee can be resized afterwards by its edges and corners (issue #23):

- **The shape is remembered, not scaled.** A Replace-mode marquee release stores a `MarqueeShape` (unclipped rect, ellipse flag, Radius, Feather, Anti-alias at creation) in `marquee_shape_`. `marquee_shape_region` / `marquee_shape_mask` rasterize from that struct alone; the drag-out's `marquee_selection_region` / `marquee_selection_mask` are thin wrappers that pack the live tool state with `current_marquee_shape`, so drag-outs rasterize through the same code. A handle drag calls `apply_marquee_shape` with the new rect, which re-draws the same rectangle, rounded rectangle, or ellipse with the same feather (a scaled region or mask would stair-step an ellipse and resample a soft edge).
- **Every other selection write clears it.** `set_selection_from_region` and `set_selection_from_mask` reset `marquee_shape_`; the marquee commit, the resize, `apply_selection_move`, and `nudge_selection` set it back after they call the setters. Add/Subtract/Intersect commits, lassos, the wand, Expand/Contract/Border, Quick Mask, and script `selectRect` therefore leave no handles. The shape rides `SelectionSnapshot` (`capture_selection_snapshot` / `apply_selection_snapshot`, compared in `selection_snapshots_equal`) so Undo and Redo restore the handles with the selection, and `last_cleared_marquee_shape_` lets Reselect bring them back.
- **Handles show only for the two marquee tools** (`resizable_marquee_rect`: shape present, selection non-empty, not in Quick Mask, no marquee/move/resize gesture in flight). `draw_marquee_resize_handles` runs right after the marching ants and reuses the transform controls' squares (`draw_transform_handle_squares`, no box, no rotate stem). The hit-test is `marquee_resize_handle_at` over the shared `transform_handle_at` with Rotate and Move mapped to None (the interior stays the move gesture), and, unlike `can_move_selection_at`, it ignores the combine modifiers: a handle is grabbed with Shift or Alt held too (Shift holds the aspect, Alt mirrors), and the hover shows the resize cursor instead of the Add/Subtract badge (GitHub issue 66, `ui_marquee_alt_on_handle_shows_resize_cursor_and_mirrors`). Add and Subtract start anywhere else. The press branch runs before the interior-move branch because the handles overlap the interior edge.
- **The marquee never leaves the image** (GitHub issue 143): `marquee_selection_rect` clips the drag-out to the canvas (fully outside collapses to 1 px at the nearest edge), and the handle drag clamps the pointer to the canvas and clips the result, so the live rectangle and the W x H readout stop at the image edge. The ellipse is exempt (issue 307): it keeps the unclipped rect and the rasterizers clip it, so the edge cuts a circle. The readout itself is a HUD pill 12/14 px below-right of the pointer (`draw_drag_size_readout`, issue 142).
- **Drag math is the crop tool's non-ratio branch** (`update_marquee_resize_drag`): the moved edges take the snapped pointer coordinate on the exclusive right/bottom convention, at least 1 px per axis, and dragging through the opposite edge flips cleanly. Shift on a corner holds the drag-start aspect; a mid-drag Shift press or release replays the drag from `last_mouse_position_` like the square constraint. Alt held mid-drag resizes about the center (`center_anchored_handle_rect`, shared with the crop handles; GitHub issue 66, `ui_marquee_alt_handle_drag_resizes_about_center`); Alt held from the press mirrors from the first move. The options-bar Style only governs drag-outs. Space held mid-drag slides the whole rect by the snapped pointer delta (`spacebar_repositioning_drag_rect_`, the drag-out rule) from `marquee_resize_current_rect_`, and `marquee_resize_start_rect_` moves along so releasing Space resumes the resize in place.
- **History and cancel.** Release records "Resize Selection" (no coalescing; each drag is one entry). A press on a handle with no travel restores the pre-press snapshot and does not deselect. `cancel_pointer_gestures` (focus loss, document switch, tool switch) restores `selection_before_edit_`. All gesture snapshots go through `capture_selection_before_edit` / `clear_selection_before_edit`, which carry `marquee_shape_before_edit_`; a site that sets `selection_before_edit_` by hand would restore a stale shape on cancel.
