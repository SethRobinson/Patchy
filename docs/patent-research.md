# Patent and trademark research record

One-time engineering research record for Patchy's patent, format-law, and trademark position. Assessed 2026-07-14 (smart object / smart filter architecture, Gaussian Blur, High Pass, Median, Dust & Scratches, format/copyright law, trademarks), then extended for Surface Blur, Tilt-Shift Blur, classic Unsharp Mask, and classic Motion Blur, and on 2026-07-15 for Plastic Wrap, Lens Blur, Iris Blur, and the local adjustment brushes. The binding summary every feature change must follow lives in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md); read that first and never act on a legal-adjacent feature from this record alone. This is engineering research, not legal advice; if any rights holder ever sends an actual letter, involve an IP attorney.

- **Architecture cleared**: no in-force Adobe patent was found covering embedded source documents rendered into a layer, re-editable parameterized filter stacks stored with a layer, per-filter blend mode/opacity, the shared filter mask, or a gallery-style preview UI. Claim-level sweep over Google Patents and FreePatentsOnline full text (assignees Adobe and Macromedia): "smart filter" appears in zero Adobe patent specifications, "smart object" in zero Adobe claims, "filter gallery" nowhere; the CS2 (2005, Smart Objects) and CS3 (2007, Smart Filters) filing windows are expired or in their final term-adjustment months, and sweeping those windows turned up nothing on point. The Gaussian Blur, High Pass, Median, and Dust & Scratches implementations are classic pre-1999 published techniques with no in-force coverage found.
- **Plastic Wrap cleared with a narrow original renderer (2026-07-15)**: the sweep found no in-force patent on the classic image effect described in Adobe's [filter effects reference](https://helpx.adobe.com/au/photoshop/using/filter-effects-reference.html). Patchy's implementation is one fixed deterministic local height-field formula: integer alpha-weighted luminance, edge-clamped box smoothing, local gradients, and a constant upper-left relief/specular direction. It does not infer materials or lighting, classify content, search or synthesize patches, or choose an algorithm from the image. The renderer preserves bounds and alpha; the native Photoshop `PlsW` descriptor and settings round-trip independently from Patchy's original pixel treatment. Keep this fixed-method boundary unless a new patent review clears a broader implementation.
- **Expired ancestors that now serve as prior art**: Adobe's adjustment-layer patents US 5974198 and US 6185342 (priority 1996-08-26, expired 2016), Live Picture/MGI's non-destructive editing US 6181836 (expired 2017), Microsoft's effect-graph layers US 7295208 (fee-lapsed, term ended 2026-05-26), and healing brush US 6587592 (Expired - Lifetime, 2021-11-19). Patchy's implemented Healing Brush uses an explicit user-selected source and fixed local frequency separation. It performs no patch search, synthesis, or gradient-domain solve.
- **Local adjustment brushes use fixed, non-adaptive math (implemented 2026-07-15)**: Dodge, Burn, and Sponge apply direct per-pixel luminance/chroma formulas inside only the user-painted footprint; Blur uses one fixed alpha-aware 3x3 Gaussian; Sharpen subtracts that same fixed blur once. The stroke does not measure its initial color, infer a boundary, rank pixels by sharpness, choose a threshold, search patches, deconvolve, or adapt its kernel. Those omissions keep it outside Adobe US 7724980 (rank pixels by sharpness and apply a user limit, Active to 2029), Adobe US 8687913 and US 9142009 (patch/self-similarity and patch-based locally content-adaptive sharpening, Active into 2032/2033), and US 8750639 (automatic thresholded sharpening). Nik's self-adaptive brush US 2007/0188510 was abandoned, but Patchy also avoids its stroke-start characteristic/isolation behavior. The exact fixed outputs and PSD round-trip bytes are pinned by `ui_local_adjustment_brushes_use_fixed_math_and_round_trip_psd`; keep the constraint comment at `CanvasWidget::local_adjustment_brush_segment` intact.
- **Spot Healing and the Patch tool were designed inside the classic-healing carve-out (implemented 2026-08-10)**: both tools blend with the healing membrane that the expired US 6587592 (Georgiev, priority 2001, Expired - Lifetime 2021-11-19) itself taught, and which Georgiev published as "Photoshop Healing Brush: a Tool for Seamless Cloning": sample the destination-minus-source differences along the region boundary, interpolate them smoothly across the interior by relaxation (a Laplace solve on the OFFSET field with Dirichlet boundary conditions, `core/heal_membrane.cpp`, deterministic integer fixed-point cascade), and add the source texture. Practicing the express teaching of an expired patent is free prior art; a later claim reading on it would be invalid as anticipated. This is distinguished from US 9058699 (gradient-domain compositing, Active to 2029), which claims compositing SOURCE GRADIENTS through a guidance field (the Perez 2003 Poisson-editing lineage): Patchy composites no gradients and builds no guidance field, only the boundary-offset membrane of the expired patent. Sources stay outside the PatchMatch gate (US 8285055 / US 8340463 / US 8355592, into 2031): Spot Healing uses ONE coherent rigid mapping (reflection across the footprint's nearest-boundary line, or a translation clear of the footprint) chosen from the stroke mask's shape alone in a fixed candidate order with geometry-only validity scores - pixel content is never read, unlike Photoshop's Proximity Match / Content-Aware modes; the Patch tool's only source is the user's drag offset. Both heal once on mouse release with only the raw footprint trail / translated blit shown mid-drag (the Quick Select posture against US 8050498's classify-and-display claims, to Nov 3, 2029; the drag ghost itself is generic drag-and-drop prior art, and the Apple US 8971623 lesson stands: give genuinely new on-canvas gizmos their own check). Constraint comments live at every implementation site; the binding boundary is the Spot Healing / Patch bullet in legal-constraints.md.
- **Classic Unsharp Mask and Motion Blur stay fixed and user-directed (implemented 2026-07-15)**: Unsharp Mask applies one radius-selected low-pass, user-entered Amount, and user-entered Threshold uniformly over the layer. It never ranks pixels by sharpness, estimates blur, chooses a threshold, searches similar patches, deconvolves, or adapts its kernel by content. Motion Blur applies one straight line kernel from the user's Angle and Distance; it does not estimate camera shake, track objects, use optical flow, vary the path per pixel, or expose Blur Gallery widgets. Those omissions keep these classic operations outside the active adaptive-sharpening, patch-based sharpening, deblurring, tracking, and Blur Gallery families reviewed for this work. Do not turn either native Smart Filter into an automatic, content-adaptive, deconvolution, or multi-widget effect without a new patent review.
- **Auto Tone, Auto Contrast, and Auto Color use fixed-constant textbook methods (implemented 2026-08-01)**: percentile-clip histogram stretching (per-channel, or through one merged histogram for the composite variant) and a neutral-midtone gamma snap are decades-old published techniques: contrast stretching and histogram-based enhancement are standard in Gonzalez & Woods' Digital Image Processing editions since the 1970s, Photoshop has shipped Auto Levels since the 1990s, GIMP's Stretch Contrast / White Balance and ImageMagick's contrast-stretch implement the same percentile clip, and the neutral-midtone assumption is Buchsbaum's 1980 gray-world hypothesis. Patchy's kernel (`src/filters/auto_levels_math.{hpp,cpp}`) keeps every parameter a compile-time constant (0.1% clip per end, 128 midtone target), derives only whole-image 256-bin histograms and channel means (no spatial or sliding-window histograms, so the Surface Blur / integral-histogram families below are structurally inapplicable), never selects between algorithms by content, and reuses the levels transfer already shipped. No fresh assignee sweep was run for this feature; it was deliberately constrained inside the fixed/user-directed boundary pattern this record already established, with the binding line in legal-constraints.md. The Watch item below (US 2022/0207115 A1, content-derived operation features) is the standing caveat: re-check that application if it grants, since automatic adjustments derive their parameters from content by construction.
- **Surface Blur required a design-around; implemented 2026-07-14 with bit-identical output.** Adobe US 8594445 ("Fast bilateral filtering using rectangular regions", filed 2005-11-29, Active, adjusted expiration 2032-05-30 per Google Patents, 11.5-year maintenance fee paid May 2025) claims a bilateral filter whose spatial component is a 2-D box, computed by constructing a histogram of the window's pixel values and applying a range filter to that histogram centered at the center pixel's current value; the original implementation (sliding coarse/fine window histograms plus the triangle range weight) practiced every element of claim 1 and the sliding reuse of dependent claim 2, and it is almost certainly the patent covering Photoshop's own Surface Blur. Adobe US 7920741 ("integral histogram convolution", filed 2007-05-31, Active, adjusted expiration 2030-02-02) additionally claims the merge-column-histograms-then-slide-along-the-scanline structure itself, which the shared Median/Dust & Scratches machinery also practiced. The shipped design-around removes every histogram from Surface Blur (direct per-pixel accumulation of the same integer triangle-weighted sums up to `kSurfaceBlurDirectMaximumRadius` = 8 px; per-intensity-level box sums above it, the linear-shift-invariant decomposition published by Durand & Dorsey 2002) and rebuilt Median/Dust & Scratches on ONE window histogram updated a single pixel value at a time along a serpentine traversal (Huang 1979 prior art; no column histograms exist anywhere). Outputs stayed byte-identical: the calibrated `smart_filter_*` pixel tests passed unchanged and `surface_blur_direct_and_level_paths_match_reference` pins both new paths against the brute-force reference across the radius cutoff. NEVER reintroduce merged or slid window histograms in these filters, and never compute Surface Blur through ANY value histogram, before the respective expirations (2030-02-02 and ~2032-05-30; the US 8594445 figure could shorten to 2029-02-01 if a terminal disclaimer to its CIP child US 7783121 applies). Also examined and judged not to read: US 7783121 (value-range-limited histograms for HDR), US 7889923 (sparse merge skipping), US 8315473 (fractional-radius border regions), US 8655097 (brush-local bilateral with automatic tolerance), US 7675524 (enclosed-block associative convolution), US 10902569 (unique-value list pruning), US 7983511 (noise-space detail classification).
- **Tilt-Shift Blur's algorithm is clear of Adobe patents**: every independent claim in the CS6 Blur Gallery family requires an element Patchy lacks. US 8831371 needs two or more blur-pattern instances iteratively combined in one mask; US 9019310 needs field-blur pins feeding a per-pixel blur-RADIUS mask with per-pixel variable kernels; US 8693776 needs a user-variable selection-edge bleed; US 8824793 needs bokeh boost thresholds; US 8891942 is video moving-object tracking; the depth-map, motion-path, and deblurring families are facially inapplicable. Patchy's single linear pattern, 0..1 mix mask, and fixed tent-blur level blending sit outside all of them; Photoshop's on-canvas widgets appear only in specifications and dependent claims. No Adobe design patent on the Blur Gallery controls was found (all 88 Adobe "display screen" design patents reviewed). Imported Photoshop Blur Gallery smart filters stay preservation-only and preview-locked in Patchy, which creates no implementation exposure.
- **Lens Blur and Iris Blur use narrow, explicit geometry (implemented 2026-07-15)**: Lens Blur follows Photoshop's documented Depth Map Source: None use case with one aperture chosen only from Radius, Blades, Blade Curvature, and Rotation. It performs one fixed premultiplied-alpha convolution and never infers depth, detects or boosts highlights, classifies content, or varies its kernel per pixel. Iris Blur computes one fixed round aperture blur, then blends it with the original through one ellipse from explicit Center, Angle, Width, Height, and Focus values. It has exactly one pattern, one mask, and no on-canvas iris-boundary widget. These omissions keep it outside US 8831371's independent requirement for two or more blur-pattern instances iteratively combined in a mask, US 9019310's field-blur pins and per-pixel radius kernels, US 8693776's selection bleed, and US 8824793's bokeh threshold boost. The recent layered-depth Lens Blur application US 2025/0104196 is also avoided: Patchy has no learned depth, focal matte, splatting, occlusion correction, or content-derived settings. Do not add depth maps or inference, multiple pins, combined patterns, a Photoshop-style iris gizmo, per-pixel variable-radius kernels, bokeh thresholding, highlight detection, or `blurbTransform` authoring without a new review. Adobe's current behavior references are [Lens Blur](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/effects-filters/blur-sharpen-filters/create-depth-of-field-with-lens-blur.html) and [Blur Gallery](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/blur-gallery.html); the key active family is [US 8831371](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8831371).
- **Tilt-Shift preview overlay was redesigned 2026-07-14 around a non-Adobe patent**: Apple US 8971623 ("Overlaid user interface tools for applying effects to image", priority 2012-03-06, Active, expires 2032-12-26) claims receiving a selected image location, overlaying a UI item comprising a first and second line flanking that location, each dividing the image into an area containing the location and one not, and applying the effect to the areas not containing it; dependent claims add parallel lines and name tilt-shift. The original overlay (two image-spanning solid focus lines plus two dashed full-blur lines around the center handle) plausibly practiced that. The shipped overlay marks each boundary with a short draggable grip bar instead (`kBoundaryGripHalfLength`, 26 px half-length, centered on the axis through the center handle); nothing spans or divides the image, while the center handle, rotation handle, hit-testing, and symmetric width editing are unchanged. `ui_filter_gallery_tilt_shift_overlay_uses_grip_bars` pins the no-spanning-lines rendering; never reintroduce image-spanning boundary lines before 2033. Strong invalidity context exists (Instagram 2011, Snapseed 2011, TiltShift Generator 2009, tiltshiftmaker ~2008 all showed on-image band UIs before the priority date) but stays fallback only. A systematic non-Adobe UI clearance was not performed; this patent surfaced incidentally, so give any new on-canvas gizmo its own check rather than assuming Adobe-only sweeps suffice.
- **Mixer Brush pickup re-review (2026-08-14, claim-level, Google Patents + FPO full text)**: the four numbers gating the mixer's one-sample design were read claim by claim against a hypothetical LIMITED smudge-style pickup: at each dab, read the canvas under the footprint into a SINGLE running RGBA average (canvas-derived only, no spatial texture, no paint-amount state), deposit = transient per-dab linear interpolation between that average and the foreground per Wet/Load/Mix, result never written back into the average. Corrections to the earlier record: US 7768525 ("Dynamic paint pickup") is MICROSOFT's, not Adobe's, and expires 2028-03-07; US 10924633 is a B1. Element analysis: US 7768525 is the only close call (claim 14 requires a paint data store CONTAINING the injection color and injecting into it; claims 1/16 also require injection-into-store, claim 1 additionally a different-graphics-type pickup surface) - the limited design misses the injection-into-store element of every independent claim PROVIDED the stored average never contains any foreground contribution (initialize from first-dab canvas sample or transparent, never foreground; foreground enters only the transient deposit lerp). Medium-high confidence, doctrine-of-equivalents caution until 2028-03-07. US 8749572 requires a per-pixel fill channel (amount of paint) on the brush - not practiced, high confidence. US 10217253 requires an erodible height-map tip with deposition threshold and persisted worn state - not practiced, high confidence. US 10924633 B1 requires generating a Bezier curve with a curvature parameter through RGB space - plain linear interpolation is outside it, high confidence; keep all mixing linear. Also examined: US 8654143 (Adobe, to 2031-03-27, likely the intended "Adobe mixer" patent) claims a user-initiated non-uniform loading mode sweeping a selected region into a plurality of brush-model cells - a single running average with no loading mode does not read, high confidence. The CURRENT shipped one-sample design reads on nothing surveyed (every pickup claim requires sampling at a plurality of stamps after the first). Prior-art context for validity: Corel Painter pickup/mixing (early 1990s), classic smudge tools, expired US 6198489.

- **Image tracing to shapes cleared with boundaries (2026-08-23, Google Patents claim reads)**: the feature quantizes a layer (median cut), removes speckles by connected-component area, walks pixel-edge contours, settles staircases, and fits with Douglas-Peucker plus per-run Schneider cubics; Overlapping mode nests regions by hole containment. Prior art for the whole pipeline: Adobe Streamline (1989), AutoTrace (1998), Potrace (Selinger 2003 paper; the GPL code was never read or vendored), Schneider 1990, Douglas-Peucker 1973, Heckbert 1982; vector-tools.md already records the fitter stages as expired prior art. Closest in-force: Adobe US 7636097 B1 ("Methods and apparatus for tracing image data", filed 2006-02-15, Active, expires 2028-02-28), the Live Trace patent. Claim 1 requires ALL of: overlapping display of the raster and the traced image in one interface, configuration selectors for multiple renditions (transparent/outlined views of either), automatic detection of modifications to the source image, automatic re-tracing on such modifications, and instant parameter updates. Patchy traces once on OK into plain shape layers with no link to the source, shows only the traced result in a dialog preview, and never re-traces on source edits, so the re-trace-on-modification and rendition-selector elements are absent (high confidence; the dialog-parameter route is the one this record has used for every other feature). Adobe US 7876932 B1 (centerline tracing via medial axis): Expired - Lifetime, 2025-10-17. Adobe US 11257256 B2 ("Raster image tracing", filed 2020-05-15, to 2040): claim 1 requires two user gesture points and displaying only the Bezier subset between the path points nearest them; whole-image tracing has no such gesture (not practiced). Adobe US 9972073 B2 / US 10521889 ("Enhanced vectorization of raster images", priority 2016-06-22, to 2036): claim 1 requires a UI for LOCALIZED control where the user marks a portion to vectorize with a different characteristic; every Patchy parameter is global (not practiced). Bending Spoons (ex Evernote) US 10212457 / 10743035 / 11395011 ("Coordinated piecewise Bezier vectorization", priority 2015-11-17, to 2036): claim 1 requires tangent vectors at contour points, segmentation points at sharp angles under 90 degrees and at high-curvature spots (over 90 degrees across 20 pixels), and a coordinated global optimization of RMS deviation plus smooth conjugation across adjacent segments; Patchy's runs fit independently with only a corner-angle split (not practiced). STMicro US 7567720 (triangle-mesh merging) is inapplicable. Trademarks: "Image Trace" and "Live Trace" were not found in Adobe's published trademark list; Patchy's command is the descriptive "Trace Image to Shapes" and docs refer to Illustrator only nominatively. Binding boundaries: the "Vector tracing" bullet in legal-constraints.md.

- **Format and copyright**: Adobe's "Photoshop File Formats Specification" (https://www.adobe.com/devnet-apps/photoshop/fileformatashtml/, November 2019 edition, confirmed live July 2026) is a public page with no click-through whose preface states it is provided for third parties to read and write the format; the restrictive 2004-2006 SDK EULA bound only its signatories. File formats are not copyrightable expression (Feist; 17 USC 102(b); Lotus v. Borland; Google v. Oracle; CJEU SAS Institute v. World Programming C-406/10), and Patchy's COM byte-diff method observes a licensed Photoshop's output without decompiling, so the reverse-engineering safe harbors (Sega v. Accolade, Sony v. Connectix, DMCA 1201(f), EU 2009/24/EC art. 5(3)/6) are belt-and-suspenders on top. Rules that keep this clean: no verbatim Adobe spec text in the repo (link it instead), self-authored fixtures only, no PSDC cloud-document support. Enforcement record: no documented Adobe action against any third-party PSD implementation or Photoshop clone (GIMP, Krita, Affinity, Corel, Apple, Photopea all ship PSD support; Photopea has sold a direct in-browser clone since 2013 untouched); Adobe's actual enforcement targets are piracy, DRM circumvention, cybersquatting, and trademark misuse.
- **HEIF-in-WASM boundary reassessed 2026-07-31**: the earlier blanket
  "never vendor libheif" rule conflated a container parser with an HEVC codec.
  Patchy's browser build may distribute libheif under LGPL-3.0-or-later when
  the complete modified source and license are conveyed, Patchy's MIT-licensed
  corresponding application source and build instructions permit relinking,
  and the combined work carries the required notices. The reviewed build
  enables only libheif's Emscripten WebCodecs adapter. That adapter parses HEIF
  and HEVC configuration/NAL framing but delegates picture decoding to the
  browser's `VideoDecoder`; no libde265, x265, FFmpeg decoder, encoder, or
  other software codec backend is linked. The
  [W3C HEVC WebCodecs registration](https://w3c.github.io/webcodecs/hevc_codec_registration.html)
  makes HEVC support optional and defines `hvc1.*`/`hev1.*` configuration, so
  Patchy checks `VideoDecoder.isConfigSupported()` and performs no fallback.
  [Apple documents WebCodecs HEVC in Safari 17.4](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/safari-release-notes/safari-17_4-release-notes);
  [Chromium documents platform HEVC](https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/blink-dev/c/YJ1QijNiHeM/m/HWs-wQBfAAAJ)
  and conditions it on device/OS capabilities. This lowers Patchy's
  codec-distribution exposure but does not establish that browser decoding is
  royalty-free for every distributor or territory. The binding engineering
  rule is therefore narrower than a legal conclusion: never ship the HEVC
  codec, never encode HEIC, retain LGPL compliance material, and obtain IP
  counsel if Patchy's distribution model or codec boundary changes.
