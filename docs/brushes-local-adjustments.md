# Local adjustment brushes: Dodge, Burn, Sponge, Blur, Sharpen

Split from [brushes.md](brushes.md): the toning and detail brushes, their fixed algorithms and the calibration against Photoshop. Their speed paths are in [performance-strokes.md](performance-strokes.md); the binding patent summary is in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md) and the claim record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md).

Dodge, Burn, Sponge, Blur, and Sharpen share one stroke-start snapshot engine in `canvas_widget_brush.cpp`. They use the procedural Size/Soft footprint plus a separate Strength cap. Dodge and Burn offer Shadows/Midtones/Highlights and Protect Tones; Sponge offers Saturate/Desaturate and Vibrance. Settings are application-wide and persist under stable `tools/local*` and `tools/sponge*` keys. Flyouts: Detail (Smudge, Mixer, Blur, Sharpen), Toning (Dodge, Burn, Sponge). A stroke that starts with Alt held paints with the opposite tool (GitHub issue 662): Dodge as Burn, Burn as Dodge, Blur as Sharpen, Sharpen as Blur (`alt_swapped_toning_tool`, canvas_widget_shared; `local_adjustment_operation` reads the press's choice, the undo entry names the tool painted). Sponge and Smudge have no opposite (Smudge keeps its Alt eyedropper). Alt down and up with no stroke running put the operation a press would paint in the status bar. `ui_toning_alt_stroke_paints_opposite_operation`.

The algorithms are fixed and user-directed: Blur applies one alpha-aware 3x3 binomial blur per brush dab; Sharpen moves every channel by 0.2 of the pixel's lightness minus a fixed 21-tap binomial blur of it, per dab (below); Dodge and Burn add a fixed per-tone curve (below), to the lightness as one equal channel offset with Protect Tones, to each channel without; Sponge scales chroma around fixed Rec. 709 luma, optionally easing off already-saturated colors. Nothing ranks pixels, detects edges or regions, samples stroke-start color statistics, searches patches, deconvolves, or adapts the footprint to image content (the claim record is in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md); keep the constraint comment at `CanvasWidget::local_adjustment_brush_segment` in sync). The tools preserve the active layer's alpha and never expand its bounds; selections and palette snapping apply before the write, and layered PSD/PSB round-trips are exact (`ui_local_adjustment_brushes_use_fixed_math_and_round_trip_psd` pins the five algorithms and every output byte). Dodge, Burn and Sponge segments walk only their capsule, skip what a continuing segment cannot change, and fan big segments out over bands; Blur, Sharpen and Smudge fan big dabs out over row bands ([performance-strokes.md](performance-strokes.md), GitHub issue 660).

## Blur and Sharpen

Blur and Sharpen paint per dab (`blur_sharpen_brush_segment`, GitHub issue 660 follow-up), unlike Dodge, Burn and Sponge, which read the stroke-start snapshot under one per-stroke alpha cap. Dabs sit every quarter of the brush diameter (at least 1 px) along the stroke, by distance (`toning_dab_placed_`, `toning_dab_distance_`), so event density does not change the result; the first lands at the press. Each dab copies its rectangle plus the kernel's reach from the current layer, then per covered pixel mixes in the fixed step at Strength times the footprint coverage (and the selection's alpha): Blur the alpha-aware 3x3 binomial blur of each channel; Sharpen adds Strength times coverage times `0.2 (L - blur21(L))` to all three channels alike (then clamps to 0..255), so a pixel keeps its color unless a channel clips, where L is the lightness `(54R + 183G + 19B) / 256` and blur21 the alpha-aware separable 21-tap binomial (`kSharpenRadius` 10, `kSharpenGain`; the across pass runs per copy row, the down pass per band). 32-bit layers work on display-encoded values like the other local tools. Overlapping dabs and repeat passes therefore build up; a pass with a hard brush covers each pixel about four times. Before, both applied the 3x3 step once per stroke from the snapshot under the alpha cap, so a stroke at 50% moved a 64|192 edge only to 80|176 and scrubbing added nothing.

Calibration (November 7, 2026, Photoshop 2026 27.11 on MAPPY through COM, the Dodge and Burn rig below): a 64|192 edge and a uniform 64..192 noise patch, stroked across with a hard 40 px brush at 25% spacing, Strength (Photoshop's `flow`) 25, 50 and 100, one and three passes. A single Blur dab at 100% takes the edge to exactly 96|160 (one [1 2 1]/4 step) and a single Sharpen dab to 40|216 (0.75 of the difference). Modelling a pass as four dabs mixed at Strength fits every Blur edge profile within about 1 level (fitted per-dab amounts 0.23, 0.47 to 0.49 and 0.91 to 0.94 for 25, 50 and 100) and Sharpen within 1 to 3 levels for one pass (more after three passes, where Photoshop clips). One pass at 50%: Blur 102|158 and noise spread 37 to 12; Sharpen 12|245 and noise spread 37 to 81 with Protect Detail off, 44|209 with a halo about 10 px wide and 37 to 53 with it on (`detailBoost`, Photoshop's default). Patchy uses Strength directly as the per-dab amount.

Sharpen first copied Protect Detail off (`v + 0.75 (v - blur3x3)` per channel per dab). On photos that compounds: Seth's portrait turned into magenta and green speckle within a couple of passes, and Photoshop does the same in that mode. Measured on two 512 px crops of the #597 portrait (full size, and quarter size saved as JPEG quality 80; `local-test-fixtures/sharpen-photo`), hard 60 px brush, lines 60 px apart, Strength 50, one, two and five passes, as ratios to the original over the stroked rows: Protect Detail off gives luma detail (a 5x5 high-pass) x1.75, x3.5, x20 and chroma detail x2.6, x6.6, x72 with 31% of pixels newly clipped after five passes (the old Patchy rule reproduces it within 1.6 levels RMS); Protect Detail on gives luma x1.33, x1.78, x4.3 and chroma x2.0, x3.2, x9.3 with 9% clipped (face crop; the JPEG crop x1.31, x1.71, x3.4, clipping 22%). Protect Detail depends on the image, which a fixed rule may not do, so Patchy takes the closest fixed rule: a least-squares search over binomial width and gain for lightness-only per-dab steps put 21 taps and 0.2 within about 5% of Protect Detail on's luma growth at one, two and five passes (0.99 to 1.15 of it) and its single-pass edge within 6 levels RMS, while chroma detail stays at the original's (the channels move together; no pixel of the crops shifts color) and clipping stays below Photoshop's (0.6 and 2% after one pass, 8 and 13% after five, against Photoshop's 0.7 and 3%, 9 and 22%). A 400 px Sharpen move costs about 2.9 ms on 3580x5370 at 4 threads (2.2 ms with the 3x3 rule). `ui_sharpen_keeps_color_and_stays_bounded` (a noisy color patch, five passes: no color shift, no clipping, bounded spread) and `ui_sharpen_photo_keeps_color_over_passes_if_available` (the two crops, writing `test-artifacts/ui_sharpen_photo_<crop>_x<passes>.png`) pin that. `ui_blur_sharpen_brushes_build_up_per_dab` checks the edge and noise after one and three passes and writes `test-artifacts/ui_blur_sharpen_build_up.png`.

## Dodge and Burn ranges

Each Range is a curve of the level change a full-strength stroke gives a tone (`ui/dodge_burn_curves.hpp`: 17 knots at tones 0, 1/16, ..., 1 joined by a Catmull-Rom spline, multiplies and adds only), one set for Protect Tones on (applied to the lightness `(54R + 183G + 19B) / 256`) and one for off (applied per channel). Strength is the stroke's alpha cap, mapped the way Photoshop's Exposure builds up over a stroke's overlapping dabs: `1 - (1 - s)^4` with Protect Tones, `1 - (1 - s)^1.75` without (`dodge_burn::stroke_amount`). Before GitHub issue 661 the ranges were straight ramps (Shadows `1 - L`, Midtones `1 - |2L - 1|`, Highlights `L`), so Shadows still moved mid gray at half weight and Burn on Highlights turned white black.

Calibration (November 7, 2026, Photoshop 2026 27.11 on MAPPY through COM from an interactive scheduled task): a 1024 px 0..255 gray ramp (4 px a level), one band per case stroked once along `PathItem.strokePath` with a hard 16 px brush at 25% spacing, every Range at Exposure 10, 25, 50 and 100, Protect Tones on and off (tool option `useLegacy` is Protect Tones off; Range enums `dodgeS/M/H`, `burnInS/M/H`; Burn is `burnInTool`). The knots are a least-squares fit of every unclipped sample through the Exposure mapping above. Protect Tones on matches Photoshop within 1 level on average (95th percentile 1, worst 3) at all four Exposures; off within 1 level for Midtones and the Dodge Shadows and Burn Highlights curves, but Photoshop's Dodge Highlights and Burn Shadows without Protect Tones compound per dab (a gain of about 1.75 at 100%), so at Exposure 10 to 50 those two differ by up to 18 levels. Facts from the measurement: with Protect Tones, black and white stay put except Burn on Highlights (white to 204 at 100%) and Dodge on Highlights (clips to white from about 70% gray); Midtones Dodge and Burn are mirror images peaking at 35 levels near 55% gray; Shadows peaks near 28% gray (Dodge +19, Burn -37) and is gone by 80%. Without Protect Tones, Dodge on Shadows lifts black to 110 and Burn on Shadows stretches levels below 109 to black. Scratch scripts: the desk card's `661` folder. `ui_dodge_burn_ranges_follow_calibrated_curves` checks the bands at the default Strength and writes `test-artifacts/ui_dodge_burn_ranges.png`.
