# Binding scanning and text recognition constraints

Split from [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md): the binding entries for Divide Scanned Photos, Scan a Book to PDF and text recognition (OCR); the parent indexes each entry and links every claim-level record. Design docs: [import.md](import.md), [book-scan.md](book-scan.md), [ocr.md](ocr.md).

## Scanned-photo division

- **Divide Scanned Photos detects once, on explicit request, on a still image** (August 2026, [record](patent-research.md)). Detection runs only when the user invokes the command or changes a dialog parameter; regions come from fixed deterministic geometry with global parameters and user-editable regions, and extraction is one fixed resample per region (axis crop, rotation, or homography with the expired Zhang aspect recovery). Never add live-camera or per-frame detection, automatic capture or quality-triggered frame selection, ML or content classification, per-region automatic parameter adaptation, or automatic selection between algorithms: those are the claim elements of the active mobile-capture families (Mitek US 9672510 to 2028; Kofax US 9760788 / US 9754163).

## Book scanning

- **Scan a Book to PDF finds pages once per explicit scan, with fixed geometry, and cuts them by rotation only** (November 2026, [record](patent-research-book-scanning.md), [design](book-scan.md)). A scan starts only from the user's Scan button (never motion, page-turn, lid or timer detection: PFU US 9258448, to 2034). Detection runs on that still image or on a dialog parameter change, with global per-book parameters and user-editable boxes; the "scanned-photo division" limits above apply to it too. Pages are cut by rotation resampling only: never line sets, vanishing points, per-page quadrangles or homographies (ABBYY US 10140691, to 2036), so the Divide Scanned Photos perspective mode is never reused here. No neural or learned page segmentation (Adobe US 12705755, to 2044). Tone changes stay global (levels, gray conversion, one B&W threshold): never local brightness changes near the binding (Canon US 8730535, to 2029) or adaptive thresholds. Reading direction is the user's setting and only orders pages: never detect the opening direction to turn one page of a spread against the other, and never put two pages on one PDF page (Brother US 8270041, to 2031). No page-curvature dewarping, 3D or camera-pose models, text-line curve fits or finger removal. The grayscale-capture hint is an offer the user accepts, never an automatic switch. A searchable book PDF (added 2026-11-17, [design](book-scan.md#searchable-pdf)) recognizes text only at export, only when the user ticked the option, with the engine and language the user picked; what it reads becomes PDF text, one run per line, and nothing else: never an input to page detection, cutting, straightening, turning or tone choices, and never stored in the project.

## Text recognition

- **Text recognition runs only on request, with the user's engine and language, and its results only become text** (design rules set 2026-11-16, [record](patent-research-text-recognition.md), [design](ocr.md)). Recognition starts only from a command, a ticked option or a script call; nothing recognizes in the background and no model downloads unasked. The engine, the language or script group and the model are the user's picks from settings, never derived from the image, and no code runs several recognizers to keep the best. Nothing from recognition, and no learned text detector, feeds Divide Scanned Photos, book page detection, cutting, straightening or tone choices: the two entries above stay as they are.
- **The model pipeline finds lines in a network's probability map and crops rectangles** (Adobe US 9576348, to 2035; Baidu US 10762376, to 2039). The map is thresholded at a fixed value and each connected region is one text line with one rotated rectangle; the line is reported as that rectangle grown by a fixed share of its thickness, and cut out with the larger fixed enlargement the recognizer needs. Never binarize the image itself and dilate or merge character shapes to find text (not as a fallback either), never turn on the detector post-processing's mask dilation, never use polygon or free-quadrilateral regions with a perspective warp, and never add a dewarp or perspective-correction model before recognition.
- **Recognized text never touches the pixels under it, and no font is identified** (Adobe US 9576348 / US 9928439; the DeepFont family US 9501724 and continuations; Amazon US 9349202; Canva US 2025/0218083 A1, pending). "Show text and hide original" hides a layer: never erase, inpaint or fill where recognized text was, and never wire a fill, heal or Remove feature to recognition results. The font family is the user's pick; never name, classify or match a typeface from pixels, never render candidate fonts to compare with the image, and never let recognition use a detected font. Size, color and slant come from plain measurement of the line (width fit against the user's font, sampled color, measured slant angle). Bold and serif, sans or monospace estimation are not built: each needs its comparison in the record first (Wipro US 10956730, to 2039; Amazon US 9349202; Adobe US 10127199).
- **Blocks come from the boxes and character counts of two neighbouring lines at a time** (Microsoft US 8565474, to 2031): their centre distance, their overlap along the line, their thickness and their length per character, and whether the two sit side by side on one row (a join that two lines of one row would both make with the same line is not made). Never classify a line as starting or continuing a paragraph, and never use indentation, a short last line, page margins, a page type or any page layout input in the grouping. Alignment is read only inside a multi-line block.
- **Searchable PDFs write each recognized line as one run** (Konica Minolta US 9442899, to 2034). Never split a line into separately placed words or pieces, by any rule, including a TJ array that positions pieces; Scan a Book to PDF, where Patchy itself scans, follows the same rule. Highlights inside a long line are approximate as a result. The PDF viewer's own Find and selection on recognized pages place highlights from the engine's word boxes on screen only ([pdf-viewer-ocr.md](pdf-viewer-ocr.md)): that text is never written to a file.
- **Models and their files** (licenses in [legal-licensing.md](legal-licensing.md)): the downloadable engine is PaddlePaddle's PP-OCRv6 small set, published as Apache-2.0, as one download (decided 2026-11-16). A second model (the National Diet Library recognizer, manga-ocr or any other) needs its license step and a new dated decision first; the National Diet Library layout model (DINOv3 license), YomiToku and EasyOCR's weights are not used.
