# Binding selection constraints

Split from [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md): the binding entries for the selection tools and commands (Quick Select, automatic selection, the Magnetic Lasso, Color Range); the parent indexes each entry and links every claim-level record. Design docs: [selection-tools.md](selection-tools.md), [ai-selection.md](ai-selection.md), [lasso-tools.md](lasso-tools.md), [color-range.md](color-range.md).

## Selection tools

- **Quick Select solves once on mouse release.** Do not add live per-mouse-move classification or a selection preview before November 3, 2029 (Adobe US 8050498). Enhance Edge remains geometric, the solve uses one window per stroke, and input never selects among algorithms automatically (US 10698588 claims toggling graph cut and level-set refinement from the Add/Remove mode plus where the click lands relative to the selection; never switch mechanisms on that pair). `ui_quick_select_stroke_selects_object_and_is_undoable` pins it.
- **Automatic selection (Select Subject, Object Selection, their script calls) is one network solve from one prompt, combined by plain boolean** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-selection.md), [design](ai-selection.md)). Object Selection sends either one box or one positive point per solve, on mouse release only: never a negative point (US 11314982, to 2037), never a box plus points or any two prompt modalities together (US 11568627, to 2037), never a previous mask back into the decoder (SAM `mask_input` stays zero; US 11676279, to 2041), and never a distance-map input. Add and Subtract are selection booleans, not network inputs. Nothing segmented is shown before release, and there is no hover highlight or precomputed per-object masks (US 8050498; Adobe's allowed hover-preview application US 2023/0129341). Select Subject runs one fixed salient model once over the whole image and upsamples once: no network-located crop, second pass, or refinement of sub-regions (US 11244195 / US 12591950, to 2040). Select Background and Remove Background reuse that one result through plain inversion or as a layer mask. No detectors, class labels, text queries, or automatic choice between models (US 11107219, US 11631234, US 11972569, US 12260557); the user picks the command, and Object Selection's Standard or High Quality model by setting and download, never by image content. Zoom-to-box encodes the user's box grown by fixed geometry (`sam_box_region`) once and decodes the box once: never a network-located region, a second pass, or a refinement of a mask. Any SAM-family model must keep SAM's decoder without encoder skip connections (US 12051225 claim 16; SAM 2 and HQ-SAM wait on a further review). No edge-refinement brush or learned matting stage on the result (US 10068361, US 11593948). A new model, prompt type or refinement needs a new review. A single-pass whole-image matting model is reviewed (record, 2026-10-12): allowed as a replacement or a user-chosen alternative, never merged with or fed by the general model; Adobe US 12051225 claim 16 (transformer encoder plus skip-connected decoder producing an alpha matte, to about 2042-09) is an open item against the BiRefNet architecture, so Select Subject, Select Background and Remove Background stay out of releases until it is resolved (`PATCHY_ENABLE_SELECT_SUBJECT` off; Object Selection ships).
- Magnetic Lasso and brush shape-dynamics patents expired, so live snap display is allowed. Magnetic Lasso still constructs its selection region once in `finish_magnetic_lasso()`, unlike Quick Select's prohibited live classification.

## Color Range

- **Color Range selects by each pixel's own color through fixed linear rules** (GitHub issue 709, compared and decided 2026-11-12, [record](patent-research-selection.md#color-range-github-709), [design](color-range.md)). A sampled color is one point in display RGB with one ramp, linear in the Euclidean distance to it (full to half the Fuzziness, none at the Fuzziness); added samples combine by maximum and subtract samples scale the result. The hue bands and tone ranges are fixed sectors and thirds with linear ramps. Adobe US 8385634 (to about 2031-12-28) describes a color model of Gaussian or truncated Gaussian functions built from the selected colors, a soft mask from it, and user input that adds or removes Gaussians. So never, without a new review: a Gaussian or other statistical color model of the samples (means, variances, mixtures, histograms); samples merged, fitted or clustered into foreground and background models; a falloff computed per channel in a luminance and chrominance space; a mask value formed as one model's value minus another's; a position term (a spatial range, localized clusters). The command only makes a selection, once, on OK: it takes no drawn region as its input, applies no adjustment, and a range is never stored on an adjustment, a layer mask or a brush as a live range mask (Adobe US 11223744, to about 2037-12-02: a user-drawn spatial mask and a color or luminance range mask together weighting an adjustment). The canvas takes one sample per press and shows nothing before OK; the mask shows only in the dialog (US 8050498, above). Skin tones, face, sky or subject detection and learned ranges are not built. Decided 2026-11-12: Color Range ships as built, in main and in releases, on the record's comparison (each sample one point, a straight-line falloff on RGB distance, no Gaussian, statistics or clustering of the samples, no position term; a selection made once on OK, with no drawn region and no adjustment). The limits above stay binding for every later change: a Gaussian or statistical color model, sample clustering or localized clusters, or a range mask tied to a drawn region or a local adjustment needs a new review first. The claims of both patents were read through a web fetch that gives short quotes, not from the USPTO images. `color_range_is_deterministic_and_tokens_are_stable` pins the rule's bytes.
