# Binding selection constraints

Split from [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md): the binding entries for the selection tools and commands (Quick Select, automatic selection, the Magnetic Lasso, Color Range) and for the Color Replacement tool, a brush that shares Color Range's records; the parent indexes each entry and links every claim-level record. Design docs: [selection-tools.md](selection-tools.md), [ai-selection.md](ai-selection.md), [lasso-tools.md](lasso-tools.md), [color-range.md](color-range.md), [color-replacement.md](color-replacement.md).

## Selection tools

- **Quick Select solves once on mouse release.** Do not add live per-mouse-move classification or a selection preview before November 3, 2029 (Adobe US 8050498). Enhance Edge remains geometric, the solve uses one window per stroke, and input never selects among algorithms automatically (US 10698588 claims toggling graph cut and level-set refinement from the Add/Remove mode plus where the click lands relative to the selection; never switch mechanisms on that pair). `ui_quick_select_stroke_selects_object_and_is_undoable` pins it.
- **Automatic selection (Select Subject, Object Selection, their script calls) is one network solve from one prompt, combined by plain boolean** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-selection.md), [design](ai-selection.md)). Object Selection sends either one box or one positive point per solve, on mouse release only: never a negative point (US 11314982, to 2037), never a box plus points or any two prompt modalities together (US 11568627, to 2037), never a previous mask back into the decoder (SAM `mask_input` stays zero; US 11676279, to 2041), and never a distance-map input. Add and Subtract are selection booleans, not network inputs. Nothing segmented is shown before release, and there is no hover highlight or precomputed per-object masks (US 8050498; Adobe's allowed hover-preview application US 2023/0129341). Select Subject runs one fixed salient model once over the whole image and upsamples once: no network-located crop, second pass, or refinement of sub-regions (US 11244195 / US 12591950, to 2040). Select Background and Remove Background reuse that one result through plain inversion or as a layer mask. No detectors, class labels, text queries, or automatic choice between models (US 11107219, US 11631234, US 11972569, US 12260557); the user picks the command, and Object Selection's Standard or High Quality model by setting and download, never by image content. Zoom-to-box encodes the user's box grown by fixed geometry (`sam_box_region`) once and decodes the box once: never a network-located region, a second pass, or a refinement of a mask. Any SAM-family model must keep SAM's decoder without encoder skip connections (US 12051225 claim 16; SAM 2 and HQ-SAM wait on a further review). No edge-refinement brush or learned matting stage on the result (US 10068361, US 11593948). A new model, prompt type or refinement needs a new review. A single-pass whole-image matting model is reviewed (record, 2026-10-12): allowed as a replacement or a user-chosen alternative, never merged with or fed by the general model; Adobe US 12051225 claim 16 (transformer encoder plus skip-connected decoder producing an alpha matte, to about 2042-09) is an open item against the BiRefNet architecture, so Select Subject, Select Background and Remove Background stay out of releases until it is resolved (`PATCHY_ENABLE_SELECT_SUBJECT` off; Object Selection ships).
- Magnetic Lasso and brush shape-dynamics patents expired, so live snap display is allowed. Magnetic Lasso still constructs its selection region once in `finish_magnetic_lasso()`, unlike Quick Select's prohibited live classification.

## Color Range

- **Color Range selects by each pixel's own color through fixed linear rules** (GitHub issue 709, compared and decided 2026-11-12, [record](patent-research-selection.md#color-range-github-709), [design](color-range.md)). A sampled color is one point in display RGB with one ramp, linear in the Euclidean distance to it (full to half the Fuzziness, none at the Fuzziness); added samples combine by maximum and subtract samples scale the result. The hue bands and tone ranges are fixed sectors and thirds with linear ramps. Adobe US 8385634 (to about 2031-12-28) describes a color model of Gaussian or truncated Gaussian functions built from the selected colors, a soft mask from it, and user input that adds or removes Gaussians. So never, without a new review: a Gaussian or other statistical color model of the samples (means, variances, mixtures, histograms); samples merged, fitted or clustered into foreground and background models; a falloff computed per channel in a luminance and chrominance space; a mask value formed as one model's value minus another's; a position term (a spatial range, localized clusters). The command only makes a selection, once, on OK: it takes no drawn region as its input, applies no adjustment, and a range is never stored on an adjustment, a layer mask or a brush as a live range mask (Adobe US 11223744, to about 2037-12-02: a user-drawn spatial mask and a color or luminance range mask together weighting an adjustment; the Color Replacement tool, a brush that tests one sampled color per dab and repaints at once, has its own entry below). The canvas takes one sample per press and shows nothing before OK; the mask shows only in the dialog (US 8050498, above). Skin tones, face, sky or subject detection and learned ranges are not built. Decided 2026-11-12: Color Range ships as built, in main and in releases, on the record's comparison (each sample one point, a straight-line falloff on RGB distance, no Gaussian, statistics or clustering of the samples, no position term; a selection made once on OK, with no drawn region and no adjustment). The limits above stay binding for every later change: a Gaussian or statistical color model, sample clustering or localized clusters, or a range mask tied to a drawn region or a local adjustment needs a new review first. The claims of both patents were read through a web fetch that gives short quotes, not from the USPTO images. `color_range_is_deterministic_and_tokens_are_stable` pins the rule's bytes.

## Color Replacement tool

- **The Color Replacement tool is a brush whose dabs test each pixel against one sampled color and repaint at once, the form published before 2017** (GitHub issue 720, compared and decided 2026-11-13, [record](patent-research-color-replacement.md), [design](color-replacement.md)). Each dab compares the stroke-start pixels under the tip with one point in display RGB by the Magic Wand's tolerance metric (a hard limit, plus a straight-line fringe a quarter of that limit wide when Anti-alias is on), optionally keeps only the matching pixels joined to the dab's center, and paints the foreground color over them with the Hue, Saturation, Color or Luminosity blend math. Claim 1 of Adobe US 11223744 (filed 2017-10-17; to about 2037-12-02) recites a spatial mask from a user drawing around a region, a range mask for pixels whose value of a user-specified parameter is within a range, a request to adjust a parameter, and the adjustment weighted by the two masks together; its other claims add a Lab point or area model, a Gaussian width or 3D smoothing function, histogram or range normalization and stored mask files. A brush footprint times a color tolerance test that weights a color change has a counterpart for most elements of claim 1, and no design limit separates the tool from that claim. What differs is that tools of this form were published long before the filing date: Photoshop 5.5's Background Eraser (1999; Sampling Continuous, Once and Background Swatch, Limits Discontiguous, Contiguous and Find Edges, Tolerance), Photoshop CS's Color Replacement tool (2003) and Paint Shop Pro's Color Replacer (1998). Decided 2026-11-13: the tool is built and ships as built, in main and in releases, and for that patent it rests on those earlier publications (E1 to E3 of the record), the same kind of decision as the Crop ones of 2026-11-11 ([legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md#crop-interaction)). It stays in the published form. So never, without a new review: a Lab or other luminance and chrominance point or area model; a Gaussian or other smoothing function around the sample; histogram, luminance or range normalization before the comparison; a spatial mask or a range mask kept in the document, the layer or a file; a replacement whose color, range or amount can be adjusted after the dab is painted (a live or non-destructive local adjustment); a range slider or parameter (luminance, depth) that masks any adjustment other than this direct repaint. Adobe US 8218860 (filed 2008-08-28; to about 2031-05-11) recites a histogram of the image's hue values, a prominent hue range found from it and its replacement by another range; the tool forms no histogram and no statistic of the image, so never choose the color to replace from the image's content (a hue histogram, a prominent or dominant color, a peak search): the user's pointer or the background swatch names it. The local adjustment brushes keep their own limit (Dodge, Burn, Sponge, Blur and Sharpen never measure a color or infer a boundary, [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)); this tool shares their stroke plumbing only. `color_replace_tolerance_is_the_magic_wand_metric` and `color_replace_overlapping_dabs_do_not_compound` pin the match and the at-once repaint. Watch items in the record: the earlier publications are listed from the products' histories, not checked against copies; no class sweep was run. Re-check 2031-05-11 and 2037-12-02.
