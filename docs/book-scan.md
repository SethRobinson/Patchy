# Scan a Book to PDF

File > Import > Scan a Book to PDF (`fileImportScanBookAction`, `file.import_scan_book_pdf`) turns a bound book into an image-page PDF whose pages all share one size, scanning a spread per key press. It is a desktop-only menu entry, `QAction::NoRole`. Continuing a saved book happens in the wizard's setup step; there is no separate Resume command (`file.import_resume_book_scan` was never released and is not registered). Binding legal boundary: "Book scanning" in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md#book-scanning) (record: [patent-research-book-scanning.md](patent-research-book-scanning.md)); scanner backends: [import-scanner.md](import-scanner.md).

Related documents:

- [book-scan-scanners.md](book-scan-scanners.md): following the scanner while the window is open: scanners switched on and off, the scanner's own Scan button (Windows), the scanner log.

## Flow

The step bar across the top (`BookScanStepBar`, painted from theme roles) numbers the four steps, checks the done ones and fills the current one; its accessible name is the step's title.

1. Setup: a Book group (title, author for PDF `/Info`, project folder, the folder note `bookScanFolderStatus`, free space) beside a Scanner group (scanner `bookScan/deviceId`, "Use the system scan dialog for each scan", which switches to the dialog session). For a new book the guide card `bookScanSetupGuide` below has Covers (`bookScanCoverStyleCombo`: Both covers in one scan, or One cover at a time for a book too large to lie open; `bookScan/coverStyle`, fixed for a book once its cover is scanned), `BookPlacementPicture` (the open book's back cover, spine and front cover on the glass, or two numbered panes, front cover down then back cover down) and three numbered steps, all following Covers; text sizes are in the page's themed sheet, since the application sheet overrides `setFont` on labels. Scan Cover and Continue This Book wear the primary style only while enabled. The folder is the last one used, exactly (`bookScan/lastFolder`; first time `<Documents>/Patchy Book Scans`); the wizard never renames or dates it. Whenever the folder field changes the setup step reads the folder's `book.json`: with a book it shows its title and scan count, fills Title and Author, selects its scanner, and swaps Scan Cover and Skip Cover for Continue This Book (scanning, review when finished, or the cover step when nothing past the cover was scanned) and Start Over... (after a confirmation, deletes only the files that manifest lists and keeps the folder and anything else in it, such as an exported PDF). Scan Cover scans the open book's outside, or the front cover alone, at 300 dpi Color, whole bed (nearest offered resolution at or above 300); Skip Cover measures from the pages and leaves the covers out; it still shows the cover step, with Top of the book (no outline, tilt, book area or Include covers).
2. Cover: the upright cover scan with the outline and two spine lines (drag edges, lines, or the whole box; Tilt spin for the angle, -90 to 90 degrees here and in the scanning step). Top of the book points Up/Right/Down/Left sets `up_turns` (lossless quarter turns applied to every scan) and re-detects; the last choice starts every new book (`bookScan/upTurns`, with a cover scan or Skip Cover, so its first detection runs on the turned scan), and a continued book keeps the turn in its book.json. Reading direction, capture resolution and mode, scan area (Book area: the outline's bounds in scanner coordinates plus 15 mm, clamped to the bed; or Whole glass), Two pages / One page per scan, Include covers. Two pages per scan is always the default: a cover scan cannot see past the glass, so an outline that with 0.1 inch on each side does not fit the reported bed only shows a notice offering One page per scan. The preview labels the two outer parts Back cover and Front cover (swapped for right to left). The spine lines move freely and may cross; on release they are reordered left to right; a press on both picks the one the drag moves toward. A half turn of Top of the book mirrors the outline and spine lines; a quarter turn detects again. One cover at a time (`BookCoverStyle::Separate`): the editor shows one cover's outline, no spine lines; after the front cover the hint asks for the back and Scan Back Cover (`bookScanBackCoverButton`, `start_back_cover_scan`) scans it to `scans/back-cover.png`; Show (`bookScanCoverSideCombo`) picks the cover the editor, Tilt and Rescan Cover act on. Each cover is its own PDF page and the cover page size is one cover's outline (`book_cover_part`, `book_cover_page_size`); the bed-fit notice takes the open book as two covers wide; the pages scan the whole glass (no book area: a closed book does not show where the open one lies), and the disabled Scan area box shows Whole glass without changing `bookScan/scanArea`. Pages can be scanned without the back cover, which is then left out.
3. Scanning: the instruction names the next pages ("Turn to pages 3 and 4", or "One page per scan: lay page 3"). Scan (default button, also Space) scans with the fixed settings; each scan is detected once (spread outline and gutter, or one page outline) unless its boxes are taken over ([Divider, binding margin and detection](#divider-binding-margin-and-detection)) and shown with its editable boxes; Pages (Two / One page per scan: changing it detects every scan so far again in the new mode and drops page edits, Use choices and any custom page order), Use (Both / Left only / Right only, hidden for one page per scan), Top of the book (always shown, the book's `up_turns`: without a cover scan it is the only place to see that the pages lie sideways and fix it; a change says "Every scan of this book was turned."), Tilt, Binding margin (two pages per scan only), Detect the pages on each scan, The scanner's Scan button starts a scan (only for a scanner that declares one: a press then does what Scan does, [book-scan-scanners.md](book-scan-scanners.md#the-scanners-own-scan-button)), Turn This Scan 180 Degrees, Rescan Last (written beside the old file, then moved onto its name, so a failed rescan keeps the old scan), Cancel Scan (direct sessions; a scanner stops only at its next chance, so the click greys the button out as "Cancelling...", sets the status and opens the window-modal "Cancelling Scan" notice `bookScanCancellingDialog` with a busy bar and Hide, all undone when the scanner reports back; a cancelled scan that ends with the scanner lost is not retried), Finished. Resolution and Mode (direct sessions) are the next scans' capture settings and can change between scans (a few pages in color, a plate at 600 dpi); each scan keeps its own `dpi` and `mode`, and pages are measured in inches, so mixed resolutions share one page size. With an earlier scan selected they show that scan's settings and only set what Rescan This Scan uses (a note says what it was scanned at); Back to Next Pages shows the book's again. A rescan detects again (at another resolution too, falling back on the previous scan's box rescaled) unless it keeps its boxes. The strip below lists every page so far with its number, the front and back covers included (one "Cover" item when the covers are left out of the PDF, or Front cover and Back cover for covers scanned one at a time). Clicking a page selects its scan for editing: the box editor shows it with its Use, Tilt and Turn 180 options, Rescan Last becomes Rescan This Scan, and the instruction names the scan and the pages Back to Next Pages returns to. Clicking a cover shows the cover's outline and spine lines with Tilt (Use and Turn 180 hidden) and Rescan Cover, which keeps the book's settings, recomputes the book area and keeps the cover selected. A rescan keeps the scan's file name, place, Use choice and any custom page order. Scan (or Space) still scans the next pages and selects the newest scan. Changing Top of the book with pages scanned turns them all: a half turn keeps every box (`half_turned_spread`/`half_turned_cover`, and Left/Right Use choices and custom-order sides swap with the pages), a quarter turn detects every scan again. The box editor reorders lines on release only for the cover's two spine lines; a spread's single gutter line keeps where it is dropped. Every edit is saved at once. A click on a strip page only moves the selection and swaps the box editor's image (the strip is rebuilt only when its pages or thumbnails change). A driver's BMP is converted to PNG and deleted after its reader closes (Windows cannot delete an open file). A page box reaching the scan edge shows the "may reach past the scanned area" warning. After the first spread of a Color capture with no colored pages, a hint offers Switch to Grayscale (the user decides). Optional beep (`bookScan/beep`).
4. Review: thumbnails in page order (drag to reorder: the selected pages move to the gap under the drop, drawn as a bar; Delete, Turn 180 Degrees, Insert Blank Page, Edit Scan or a double-click: the scanning step with that page's scan or the cover selected; Back, first in the row, returns to scanning), page size (Measured median, Largest, From the cover, Custom, A4, A5, US Letter, 6 x 9 in, 5.5 x 8.5 in; units follow the locale's measurement system), Binding margin (the scanning step's setting again, beside the preview that shows its result), output Color / Grayscale / Black and white with a global threshold and a live preview, Selected pages (Same as the book, Color, Grayscale, Black and white: the selected pages' own output, such as color plates in a black and white book; the label under such a thumbnail names it), resolution (as scanned, or 600 to 150 dpi), image quality (`pdf_image_quality_presets`). Export PDF..., or Open Book Folder (shows the project folder in the file manager; the wizard stays open). After an interactive export the user may delete the project: only the files the manifest lists, then the two folders if empty (never a recursive delete).

## Divider, binding margin and detection

- **The divider decides the cut of both pages.** A page's inner edge is the gutter line, and its box runs outward from there by the uniform width (`uniform_spread_page_box`): moving the divider moves both pages of the spread, at every page size policy. A page takes pixels only from its own part of the outline, from the line to the outline's outer edge (`uniform_spread_page_cut`, `BookPageCut`): a box that reaches past the outer edge is white there, never the lid or the glass. A side wider than the uniform page loses its outer end (with Measured (median), the wider side of an off-center divider); Largest keeps every page whole. The pages' place therefore follows the divider: a divider detected a little differently from scan to scan shifts the page by as much, and with detection off every scan is cut at the same place.
- **Binding margin** (`BookProject::binding_margin`, inches; `bookScanBindingMarginSpin` in the scanning step, `bookScanReviewBindingMarginSpin` in the review step, in mm or inches by the locale, 0 to 2 inches, default 0): a strip that wide on each side of every spread's divider belongs to neither page. It is one value for the book, moves the cut limit and is left out of the measured page width (`spread_page_width`); the box editor shades the strip beside the line. Geometry only: nothing near the binding is brightened or otherwise changed in tone ([legal entry](legal-constraints-scanning.md#book-scanning)). Hidden for One page per scan, which has no divider. The last value starts the next book (`bookScan/bindingMargin`).
- **Detect the pages on each scan** (`bookScanDetectCheck`, `BookProject::detect_each_scan`, on for every new book and not remembered between books: a new book lies elsewhere and its first scan has no boxes to take over). Off: a new scan takes the previous scan's layout as it is (outline, divider, tilt, or the page box in One page per scan), and a rescan keeps its own; boxes moved by hand lead the scans after them. The first scan of a book is detected even with it off.
- **A rescan keeps boxes the user moved** (`user_edited`), with detection on or off; the status line says so. An unedited scan is detected again when detection is on.
- **Taking boxes over** (`carried_scan_layout`): the same place on the glass, rescaled for another resolution, turned for a scan that had its own 180 degree turn. A scan of another size (another scan area) cannot take boxes over and is detected.
- Turn This Scan 180 Degrees turns the boxes with the scan (`half_turned_spread`) when they were moved by hand or detection is off, and detects otherwise.
- Changing Pages per scan, or a quarter turn of Top of the book, detects every scan again whatever the option says: the old boxes do not describe the new pages.
- Scripts need nothing new: `app.buildBookPdf` renders through `render_book_page`, so it writes the pages the wizard shows.

## Scan copies

The box editor, the review preview and the thumbnails never read a full scan on a click. When a scan arrives (or a resumed book's copy is missing or older than its scan), the full scan is read once and two copies are written to `thumbs/`: `thumbs/<scan file name>` (PNG at 36 dpi, thumbnails) and `thumbs/<base name>-view.jpg` (JPEG, longest side 1600 px, the box editor's image and the review preview at 60 dpi). The last 12 view copies stay in memory. Box coordinates stay in full-scan pixels; the copies only scale them. The export and the grayscale hint read the full scans. Deleting a project removes both copies of each scan.

Settings keys (permanent): `bookScan/lastFolder`, `bookScan/deviceId`, `bookScan/dpi`, `bookScan/colorMode` (`color`/`gray`), `bookScan/scanArea` (`book`/`glass`), `bookScan/readingDirection` (`ltr`/`rtl`), `bookScan/coverStyle` (`spread`/`separate`), `bookScan/includeCovers` (saved only from a book with a cover: Skip Cover's unchecked, disabled box is no choice), `bookScan/beep`, `bookScan/bindingMargin` (inches), `bookScan/upTurns` (0 to 3, the last Top of the book; missing: 0), `bookScan/scannerButton` (the scanner's own Scan button starts a scan; missing: on).

## Geometry rules

- Every scan is turned upright first (`up_turns` plus a scan's own `extra_turns`), so left and right are always the spread's halves.
- Spread detection: the Divide Scanned Photos background model and min-area rectangle (largest region). A detection smaller than half the previous spread (or a tenth of the image without one) is distrusted and the previous spread kept, for white pages on a white lid; the user corrects the box.
- Gutter: inside the straightened spread, 512 columns by 192 rows, each column's 80th-percentile luminance (text lines are dark marks on white paper and keep a white percentile; the fold's shadow darkens every row), smoothed over 9 columns, darkest in the central 30%, the middle of the run within a quarter of the contrast. Under 6 luminance levels of contrast the spread splits at its middle.
- One uniform page size for the whole PDF; each page box is that size, the spread's angle, vertically centered, anchored at the page's inner edge: the gutter line less the binding margin, with the box running outward to the fore-edge side. A spread's page is limited along its width to its own part of the outline, from that inner edge to the outline's outer edge: a source pixel whose center lies past either limit is left out. Covers and one-page scans are centered. Extraction is rotation resampling only (bilinear, area-averaged supersampling when shrinking, white beyond the scan and beyond the limit).
- A spread page's measured width is its side of the gutter line less the binding margin (`measured_page_sizes`).
- Output: fixed Rec. 601 gray weights, global black/white points, one global threshold. Reading direction only orders pages (right page first for right to left; the front cover is the right half of a left-to-right cover scan).

## book.json

Format id `patchy-book-scan`, version 1, written through `write_file_bytes_atomically` after every scan and edit. Keys and tokens are permanent: `title`, `author`, `device`, `capture` (`dpi`, `mode` `color`/`gray`, `bookArea`, `areaInches`), `upTurns`, `direction` (`ltr`/`rtl`), `mode` (`two-pages`/`one-page`), `includeCovers`, `finished`, `bindingMargin` (inches, written only above 0; missing: 0), `detectEachScan` (written only when false; missing: true), `cover` (`file`, `dpi`, `outline` {`cx`,`cy`,`w`,`h`,`angle`}, `spineLeft`, `spineRight`, `edited`; the front cover alone for `separate`), `coverStyle` (`spread`/`separate`; missing: `spread`), optional `backCover` (`file`, `dpi`, `outline`, `edited`; read only for `separate`), `scans` [{`file`, `dpi`, `mode` `color`/`gray` (missing: the book's capture mode), `turns`, `spread`, `gutter`, `edited`, `use` `both`/`left`/`right`}], optional `pages` [{`kind` `scan`/`blank`/`front-cover`/`back-cover`, `scan`, `side` `left`/`right`, `turns`, optional `output` `color`/`gray`/`bw`}], `output` (`sizePolicy` `median`/`largest`/`cover`/`custom`, `customSize`, `mode` `color`/`gray`/`bw`, `blackPoint`, `whitePoint`, `threshold`, `dpi`, `quality`, and `searchable`, written only when true). Scan files are relative paths inside the folder; a manifest naming an absolute path, a drive or `..` is refused. Scans are stored as lossless PNG in `scans/` (`cover.png`, `back-cover.png`, `spread-0001.png` or `page-0001.png`).

## PDF

`write_book_pdf` streams pages through `pdf::ImageWriter` ([pdf.md](pdf.md)): every page the uniform size in points, image over the whole page, rendered and encoded on up to 4 workers and written in order, `BookScanCache` holding at most 4 upright scans. Each page uses its own output when it has one (`page_output_options`). Color and Grayscale pages go through `pdf_detail::encode_page_image` (gray pages are one-component); Black and White pages are one bit per pixel, Flate-compressed (`/BitsPerComponent 1`). Cancel removes the partial file and keeps an existing destination. Scripts: `app.buildBookPdf(folder, path, options)` ([scripting-guide.md](../scripts/bundled/scripting-guide.md)).

## Searchable PDF

The review step's "Make the PDF searchable (recognize text)" (`bookScanSearchableCheck`, off by default, `BookProject::output_searchable`, `output.searchable` in book.json) with the shared engine and language picker under it (`OcrEnginePicker`, prefix `bookScanOcr`, shown while ticked; [ocr.md](ocr.md#dialogs)). Binding entries: "Book scanning" and "Text recognition" in [legal-constraints-scanning.md](legal-constraints-scanning.md).

- **Recognition runs only at export, and its text only becomes PDF text.** `write_book_pdf(project, path, progress, BookPdfRecognition{engine, language})`: each page that is not blank is cut (`render_book_page` with `apply_output` false), read once, and only then converted for output, so the engine reads the scan's own tones even for a Black and White page, and the image written is the one an export without recognition writes. The lines go out through `pdf_text_lines(result, image size, page size)` as one run per line ([pdf-searchable-text.md](pdf-searchable-text.md)). Nothing that was read is kept: book.json holds the choice only, and recognition never feeds page detection, cutting, straightening or tone choices.
- **One recognizer for the book**, opened off the GUI thread; the encode workers take turns at it (`PageReader::mutex`). While a page is being read the progress callback is called about every 15 ms with unchanged numbers, so the window stays alive and Cancel is seen; Cancel, or a page that fails, requests `ocr::Cancel`, so the reads in flight stop at once. An engine failure fails the export with the engine's words ("The text on a page could not be recognized: ...").
- **No usable engine is an error, not a PDF without text.** With the check ticked and no engine that can run with the picks, Export stops and says so; the picker's note and its Download button say what to do. The picks are stored (`ocr/engine`, `ocr/language`) when an export starts.
- **Scripts.** `app.buildBookPdf` options `searchable` (default: the project's saved choice), `ocrEngine`, `ocrLanguage` (default: the stored picks, then the first available engine); it throws when no engine can run.
