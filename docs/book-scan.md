# Scan a Book to PDF

File > Import > Scan a Book to PDF (`fileImportScanBookAction`, `file.import_scan_book_pdf`) turns a bound book into an image-page PDF whose pages all share one size, scanning a spread per key press. It is a desktop-only menu entry, `QAction::NoRole`. Continuing a saved book happens in the wizard's setup step; there is no separate Resume command (`file.import_resume_book_scan` was never released and is not registered). Binding legal boundary: "Book scanning" in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md#book-scanning) (record: [patent-research-book-scanning.md](patent-research-book-scanning.md)); scanner backends: [import-scanner.md](import-scanner.md).

## Files

- `src/core/book_scan.{hpp,cpp}` (Qt-free): `OrientedBox` geometry, `detect_book_outline` (the Divide Scanned Photos detector, largest region), `detect_gutter_fraction`, `detect_spread`, uniform sizing (`median_page_size`, `largest_page_size`, `cover_page_size`), `uniform_spread_page_box`/`centered_page_box`, `extract_book_page`, `apply_book_output`, the grayscale hint, `book_spread_fits_bed`, `unturned_rect`. Tests: `book_scan_*` (patchy_core_tests).
- `src/ui/scanner_session.{hpp,cpp}` and `scanner_session_win.cpp`: repeated scans with fixed settings ([import-scanner.md](import-scanner.md#scanner-sessions)).
- `src/ui/book_scan_project.{hpp,cpp}`: the project model, `book.json`, page order, page sizing and `render_book_page`; `src/ui/book_pdf_export.{hpp,cpp}`: `write_book_pdf`.
- `src/ui/book_scan_dialog.{hpp,cpp}`: the wizard (`bookScanDialog`, non-modal through `run_non_modal_dialog`) and `BookScanPreview`, its box editor. The MainWindow entry point lives in `main_window_files.cpp`.

## Flow

1. Setup: title, author (PDF `/Info`), project folder, scanner (`bookScan/deviceId`), "Use the system scan dialog for each scan" (switches to the dialog session). The folder is the last one used, exactly (`bookScan/lastFolder`; first time `<Documents>/Patchy Book Scans`); the wizard never renames or dates it. Whenever the folder field changes the setup step reads the folder's `book.json`: with a book it shows its title and scan count, fills Title and Author, selects its scanner, and swaps Scan Cover and Skip Cover for Continue This Book (scanning, review when finished, or the cover step when nothing past the cover was scanned) and Start Over... (after a confirmation, deletes only the files that manifest lists and keeps the folder and anything else in it, such as an exported PDF). Scan Cover scans the open book's outside at 300 dpi Color, whole bed (nearest offered resolution at or above 300); Skip Cover measures from the pages and leaves the covers out.
2. Cover: the upright cover scan with the outline and two spine lines (drag edges, lines, or the whole box; Tilt spin for the angle). Top of the book points Up/Right/Down/Left sets `up_turns` (lossless quarter turns applied to every scan) and re-detects. Reading direction, capture resolution and mode, scan area (Book area: the outline's bounds in scanner coordinates plus 15 mm, clamped to the bed; or Whole glass), Two pages / One page per scan, Include covers. Two pages per scan is always the default: a cover scan cannot see past the glass, so an outline that with 0.1 inch on each side does not fit the reported bed only shows a notice offering One page per scan. The preview labels the two outer parts Back cover and Front cover (swapped for right to left). The spine lines move freely and may cross; on release they are reordered left to right; a press on both picks the one the drag moves toward. A half turn of Top of the book mirrors the outline and spine lines; a quarter turn detects again.
3. Scanning: the instruction names the next pages ("Turn to pages 3 and 4", or "One page per scan: lay page 3"). Scan (default button, also Space) scans with the fixed settings; each scan is detected once (spread outline and gutter, or one page outline) and shown with its editable boxes; Pages (Two / One page per scan: changing it detects every scan so far again in the new mode and drops page edits, Use choices and any custom page order), Use (Both / Left only / Right only, hidden for one page per scan), Tilt, Turn This Scan 180 Degrees, Rescan Last (written beside the old file, then moved onto its name, so a failed rescan keeps the old scan), Cancel Scan (direct sessions), Finished. Resolution and Mode (direct sessions) are the next scans' capture settings and can change between scans (a few pages in color, a plate at 600 dpi); each scan keeps its own `dpi` and `mode`, and pages are measured in inches, so mixed resolutions share one page size. With an earlier scan selected they show that scan's settings and only set what Rescan This Scan uses (a note says what it was scanned at); Back to Next Pages shows the book's again. A rescan at another resolution detects again, falling back on the previous scan's box rescaled. The strip below lists every page so far with its number, the front and back covers included (one "Cover" item when the covers are left out of the PDF). Clicking a page selects its scan for editing: the box editor shows it with its Use, Tilt and Turn 180 options, Rescan Last becomes Rescan This Scan, and the instruction names the scan and the pages Back to Next Pages returns to. Clicking a cover shows the cover's outline and spine lines with Tilt and Top of the book (Use and Turn 180 hidden) and Rescan Cover, which keeps the book's settings, recomputes the book area and keeps the cover selected. A rescan keeps the scan's file name, place, Use choice and any custom page order. Scan (or Space) still scans the next pages and selects the newest scan. Changing Top of the book with pages scanned turns them all: a half turn keeps every box (`half_turned_spread`/`half_turned_cover`, and Left/Right Use choices and custom-order sides swap with the pages), a quarter turn detects every scan again. The box editor reorders lines on release only for the cover's two spine lines; a spread's single gutter line keeps where it is dropped. Every edit is saved at once. A click on a strip page only moves the selection and swaps the box editor's image (the strip is rebuilt only when its pages or thumbnails change). A driver's BMP is converted to PNG and deleted after its reader closes (Windows cannot delete an open file). A page box reaching the scan edge shows the "may reach past the scanned area" warning. After the first spread of a Color capture with no colored pages, a hint offers Switch to Grayscale (the user decides). Optional beep (`bookScan/beep`).
4. Review: thumbnails in page order (drag to reorder: the selected pages move to the gap under the drop, drawn as a bar; Delete, Turn 180 Degrees, Insert Blank Page, Edit Scan or a double-click: the scanning step with that page's scan or the cover selected, Scan More Pages), page size (Measured median, Largest, From the cover, Custom, A4, A5, US Letter, 6 x 9 in, 5.5 x 8.5 in; units follow the locale's measurement system), output Color / Grayscale / Black and white with a global threshold and a live preview, Selected pages (Same as the book, Color, Grayscale, Black and white: the selected pages' own output, such as color plates in a black and white book; the label under such a thumbnail names it), resolution (as scanned, or 600 to 150 dpi), image quality (`pdf_image_quality_presets`). Export PDF..., or Open Book Folder (shows the project folder in the file manager; the wizard stays open). After an interactive export the user may delete the project: only the files the manifest lists, then the two folders if empty (never a recursive delete).

## Scan copies

The box editor, the review preview and the thumbnails never read a full scan on a click. When a scan arrives (or a resumed book's copy is missing or older than its scan), the full scan is read once and two copies are written to `thumbs/`: `thumbs/<scan file name>` (PNG at 36 dpi, thumbnails) and `thumbs/<base name>-view.jpg` (JPEG, longest side 1600 px, the box editor's image and the review preview at 60 dpi). The last 12 view copies stay in memory. Box coordinates stay in full-scan pixels; the copies only scale them. The export and the grayscale hint read the full scans. Deleting a project removes both copies of each scan.

## Scanners turning on and off

The scanner list and everything that depends on it follow a scanner being switched on or off, plugged in or unplugged, while the window is open; nothing listens before the window opens or after it closes, and nothing polls.

- Windows: the WIA session registers for `WIA_EVENT_DEVICE_CONNECTED` and `WIA_EVENT_DEVICE_DISCONNECTED` (`IWiaDevMgr2::RegisterEventCallbackInterface` on its worker thread) and, as a second trigger, watches `WM_DEVICECHANGE`/`DBT_DEVNODES_CHANGED` through a native event filter. A WIA event waits 400 ms, a device-node change 1.5 s (WIA catches up after the device node, and that message comes for every device), then the list is read again on the worker thread. It emits `devices_changed(list)` when the list differs or a WIA event came (a scanner switched off and on within the wait lists the same, but its handle is dead). `EnumDeviceInfo(WIA_DEVINFO_ENUM_LOCAL)` already leaves out an installed scanner that is off (checked on Windows 11 with a USB scanner: `WIA_DEVINFO_ENUM_ALL` lists it).
- The dialog on `devices_changed`: fills the list again, keeping the selection (past the setup step, the book's scanner); Scan Cover and Skip Cover are disabled with a tooltip while the list is empty; closes the open scanner (`ScannerSession::close`, or after the scan when one is running), so the next scan opens it again and reads its resolutions and bed again. Past the setup step, while the book's scanner is missing the status line says it is off and the cover step's resolution and mode are disabled; scans open the book's scanner, never another listed one. A reopen past the setup step that finds other resolutions refills the resolution list and moves the capture resolution to the nearest offered one (status line says so). Picking another scanner in the list, and Refresh, also close the open one.
- A scan that fails with a lost-device error (`ScanResult::device_lost`: `WIA_ERROR_OFFLINE`, `WIA_ERROR_DEVICE_COMMUNICATION`, `WIA_S_NO_DEVICE_AVAILABLE`, `ERROR_DEVICE_NOT_CONNECTED`, RPC disconnects) closes the scanner, opens it again and repeats the scan once; if it cannot be opened the error says to switch it on.
- macOS and Linux scan through the system dialog session, whose list is one fixed entry: the ImageKit browser updates itself, and the Linux dialog enumerates SANE and gphoto2 on a worker thread each time it opens. SANE has no hotplug notice and `sane_get_devices` can take seconds with network backends, so nothing re-enumerates on its own there. File > Import > From Scanner, Photocopy and Scan and Divide Photos open the system dialog each time and keep no device state.

Settings keys (permanent): `bookScan/lastFolder`, `bookScan/deviceId`, `bookScan/dpi`, `bookScan/colorMode` (`color`/`gray`), `bookScan/scanArea` (`book`/`glass`), `bookScan/readingDirection` (`ltr`/`rtl`), `bookScan/includeCovers`, `bookScan/beep`.

## Geometry rules

- Every scan is turned upright first (`up_turns` plus a scan's own `extra_turns`), so left and right are always the spread's halves.
- Spread detection: the Divide Scanned Photos background model and min-area rectangle (largest region). A detection smaller than half the previous spread (or a tenth of the image without one) is distrusted and the previous spread kept, for white pages on a white lid; the user corrects the box.
- Gutter: inside the straightened spread, 512 columns by 192 rows, each column's 80th-percentile luminance (text lines are dark marks on white paper and keep a white percentile; the fold's shadow darkens every row), smoothed over 9 columns, darkest in the central 30%, the middle of the run within a quarter of the contrast. Under 6 luminance levels of contrast the spread splits at its middle.
- One uniform page size for the whole PDF; each page box is that size, the spread's angle, vertically centered, anchored at the page's fore-edge (the gutter edge bends under the binding). Covers and one-page scans are centered. Extraction is rotation resampling only (bilinear, area-averaged supersampling when shrinking, white beyond the scan).
- Output: fixed Rec. 601 gray weights, global black/white points, one global threshold. Reading direction only orders pages (right page first for right to left; the front cover is the right half of a left-to-right cover scan).

## book.json

Format id `patchy-book-scan`, version 1, written through `write_file_bytes_atomically` after every scan and edit. Keys and tokens are permanent: `title`, `author`, `device`, `capture` (`dpi`, `mode` `color`/`gray`, `bookArea`, `areaInches`), `upTurns`, `direction` (`ltr`/`rtl`), `mode` (`two-pages`/`one-page`), `includeCovers`, `finished`, `cover` (`file`, `dpi`, `outline` {`cx`,`cy`,`w`,`h`,`angle`}, `spineLeft`, `spineRight`, `edited`), `scans` [{`file`, `dpi`, `mode` `color`/`gray` (missing: the book's capture mode), `turns`, `spread`, `gutter`, `edited`, `use` `both`/`left`/`right`}], optional `pages` [{`kind` `scan`/`blank`/`front-cover`/`back-cover`, `scan`, `side` `left`/`right`, `turns`, optional `output` `color`/`gray`/`bw`}], `output` (`sizePolicy` `median`/`largest`/`cover`/`custom`, `customSize`, `mode` `color`/`gray`/`bw`, `blackPoint`, `whitePoint`, `threshold`, `dpi`, `quality`). Scan files are relative paths inside the folder; a manifest naming an absolute path, a drive or `..` is refused. Scans are stored as lossless PNG in `scans/` (`cover.png`, `spread-0001.png` or `page-0001.png`).

## PDF

`write_book_pdf` streams pages through `pdf::ImageWriter` ([pdf.md](pdf.md)): every page the uniform size in points, image over the whole page, rendered and encoded on up to 4 workers and written in order, `BookScanCache` holding at most 4 upright scans. Each page uses its own output when it has one (`page_output_options`). Color and Grayscale pages go through `pdf_detail::encode_page_image` (gray pages are one-component); Black and White pages are one bit per pixel, Flate-compressed (`/BitsPerComponent 1`). Cancel removes the partial file and keeps an existing destination. Scripts: `app.buildBookPdf(folder, path, options)` ([scripting-guide.md](../scripts/bundled/scripting-guide.md)).
