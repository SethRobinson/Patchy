# Patent research: content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting

Claim-level record for the content-driven object-removal boundary in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md) ("Content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting"). Companion to [patent-research.md](patent-research.md), split out for that file's size cap. Engineering research from the public claim text on Google Patents, not legal advice; doctrine-of-equivalents caution applies to every judgment below.

## Why the review happened (2026-09-22)

GitHub issue #23 asked for a lightweight, non-AI Remove Object and linked cheind/inpaint (Criminisi exemplar fill plus PatchMatch search). The first Remove Object shipped as the selection form of Spot Healing: a shape-derived mirror of the nearest neighbouring region, which on a wide selection of a beam pasted the wall above it. The blanket rule "no content-aware fill or patch search until the PatchMatch family expires" was then read against the claims themselves so that what the claims do not cover is no longer forbidden by the repository.

## Patents read, claim elements, and what they do NOT cover

- **US 8285055 B1, Adobe, "Determining correspondence between image regions"** (the PatchMatch patent; priority 2008-09-10, Active, expires 2031-04-09). Every independent claim requires ITERATIVE evaluation of a patch mapping with two phases: a propagation phase that picks the best of the current mapping and mappings obtained from NEARBY pixels' mappings, and a perturbation phase that tests a mapping obtained by perturbing the chosen one (the random search). An exhaustive scan that scores every candidate offset in a window independently, with no offset field, no propagation from neighbours, and no perturbation, has neither phase and does not read on it. Prior art context: Efros-Leung 1999 non-parametric sampling and Criminisi 2003 are exhaustive-search methods that predate the priority date.
- **US 8340463 B1, Adobe, "Candidate pruning for patch transforms"** (priority 2008-08-29, Active, expires 2031-10-24). Every independent claim requires pre-computing pruned candidate-patch lists for a regular grid of overlapping patches, "patch loops" of mutually compatible neighbours stored as active labels, and belief-propagation reconstruction. No candidate pruning, no loops, no message passing: not practiced.
- **US 8355592 B1, Adobe, "Generating a modified image with semantic constraint"** (priority 2009-05-06, Active, expires 2031-08-12). Claim 1 requires iterations of a patch-based optimization under a user constraint on the location or orientation of an identified feature; claim 8 adds coarse-to-fine resizing, nearest-neighbor-field computation, model fitting and projection; claim 16 is the reshuffling variant. No feature constraints, no model fitting, no reshuffling: not practiced.
- **US 9396530 B2, Adobe, "Low memory content aware image modification"** (priority 2014-07-24, Active, expires 2034-10-15). Every independent claim requires computing a patch coordinate map at a DOWNSAMPLED resolution, upsampling that map, and placing fill content at the original resolution from the upsampled coordinates. This is why the cleared design searches at the working resolution only and never builds a coarse offset map to refine or upsample.
- **US 10825148 B2, Adobe, "Boundary-aware object removal and content fill"** (priority 2018-11-29, Active, to 2039). Every independent claim requires a trained model (conditional GAN) that completes object boundaries, per-object masks at boundary intersections, and separate fills per mask. A user-drawn selection with one fill: not practiced.
- **US 9697595 B2 / US 10467739 B2, Adobe, "Content aware fill based on similar images"** (Active). Sources come from OTHER images; the cleared design samples only the document being edited.
- **US 6987520 B2, Microsoft, "Image region filling by exemplar-based inpainting"** (Criminisi, Perez, Toyama; priority 2003-02-24, Expired - Fee Related, expiration 2023-03-30). The classic method: fill front, priority = confidence times isophote strength, exhaustive best-match search, copy the tile, update the front. Expired, so its express teaching is free prior art, and it is the algorithm family the cleared boundary describes.

## The cleared design

Implemented 2026-09-22 in `src/core/exemplar_inpaint.cpp` as Edit > Remove Object's only menu method; the shape-derived mirror stays as its automatic fallback and as the script API's `nearestEdge` method (its menu entry was removed 2026-09-23). Design record in [healing.md](healing.md).

Fill the hole from its boundary inward in a fixed deterministic priority order (Criminisi's confidence times data term, or a simpler fixed order); for each target patch, scan every candidate source patch in a bounded window of the same image at the working resolution, score with an integer distance metric (L1 or SSD over the known pixels, fixed tie-breaks; the variation pick below is the one place randomness enters, through splitmix64), copy the best match (or, for a variation, one of the near-best matches of that same scan), update the front. Each patch's search is independent of every other patch's chosen offset. The shipped metric adds a fixed per-pixel penalty on the source offset (a geometric preference for nearby sources), and the shipped tone match is the expired-patent membrane on the low-pass band (frequency separation), not gradient-domain compositing. Not in the first implementation without their own check: patch rotation, scale, or mirror adaptation; gradient-domain color adaptation; a user-painted sampling region (Photoshop's 2018+ Content-Aware Fill workspace has unreviewed Adobe filings); anything that seeds, propagates, perturbs, prunes, or upsamples offsets.

Unchanged neighbours of this boundary: US 9058699 (gradient-domain compositing, to 2029) still forbids compositing source gradients or building a guidance field; US 8050498 (live classify-and-display, to 2029-11-03) still forbids live per-move previews; the classic-healing carve-out for Healing Brush, Spot Healing, Remove Object's shape-derived mode, and the Patch tool is unchanged (see [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)).

## Claim check 2026-09-24: variations (near-best random pick) and the Remove Object dialog

GitHub issue #23's follow-up (c-sanchez): PhotoLine's object removal gives a different result per click, and rerolling until one is clean is the value. Two additions were checked against the same claims.

- **Variation N (`ExemplarInpaintOptions::attempt` > 0, the dialog's Reroll).** For each target patch the unchanged exhaustive scan finds the best distance D; a second full scan of the same windows against the fixed threshold D + max(D / 8, 8 per known target pixel) counts the admissible candidates, and the copy comes from the k-th of them in scan order, k a splitmix64 draw seeded from (attempt, target cell). This is Efros and Leung 1999 ("Texture synthesis by non-parametric sampling"), which picks uniformly among the candidates within a fixed factor of the best distance: prior art from before every priority date here. Against US 8285055: its independent claims need iterative propagation from neighbouring pixels' mappings plus a perturbation phase; here no offset is ever perturbed (the admissible set comes from scoring every candidate in the windows, not from moving an offset), no mapping is read from a neighbour, and nothing iterates. Against US 8340463: its claims need candidate lists PRECOMPUTED for a grid of patches, kept as active labels, and reconstructed by belief propagation; here the admissible set is computed per target from that target's own fresh scan, used once, and discarded, and no message passing exists. Against US 9396530: the pick happens at the working resolution with no coordinate map. The engineering rule that keeps it there: no candidate list outlives its target, seeds a neighbour, or narrows a later search, and the attempt-0 output stays byte-identical (`exemplar_inpaint_restores_periodic_stripes_deterministically`).
- **The Remove Object dialog (Reroll, Tone match, Edge feather).** US 8050498 (Adobe, live classify-and-display, to Nov 3, 2029) claims classifying pixels under the pointer and displaying the result as the input moves. The dialog runs a fill only on an explicit Reroll click, on a slider release, or on a settled slider/spin value (a coalescing timer that skips while the slider is held), never per slider move or pointer move; each run is a complete fill of the original snapshot whose finished result replaces the previous attempt inside one history entry (pushed on OK, none on Cancel). Nothing is classified live and nothing is displayed per move. The tone match keeps the expired-membrane form (Dirichlet interpolation of the hole's own low-pass edge values; its normalization moved to hole cells only for quality, not a legal change) and the feather is a plain gaussian of the coverage mask.
