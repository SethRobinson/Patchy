# Drag move coalescing

Split from [interactive-previews.md](interactive-previews.md): the rule every new drag follows, which canvas and widget drags apply only the newest pointer move of an event-loop pass, how presses, releases and keys flush the recorded move, the tests that pin it, the November 2026 audit of every drag and the live Wayland timings.

## Rule for new drags

Wayland delivers every pointer motion (the AppImage's Qt 6.8.3 hands all of them to Patchy; X11, Windows, macOS and the Flatpak's Qt 6.11 merge them), so a drag whose move handler costs more than about a millisecond queues moves on a fast mouse and falls seconds behind on a slow machine. This was fixed one drag at a time in GitHub #207, #314, #443, #444, #478, #597, #660, #677, #678, #700 and #705; every new drag follows this rule from the start.

- **When.** A drag must coalesce when its move does more than move an overlay: a recomposite, a preview render, a layer re-raster, a text re-layout, a selection rebuild, an edge search, a view change that re-lays out the canvas, or anything that grows with the document, the layer or the story. Measure the move with `patchy_perf_tests dragaudit` (below); over about 0.5 ms on a fast desktop it coalesces.
- **Canvas drags.** Add the drag's state to `coalescing_drag_active` (canvas_widget.hpp) and classify its `mouseMoveEvent` branch in `ui_canvas_drag_branches_follow_the_wayland_drag_rule` (tests/ui/drag_coalescing_tests.cpp), which fails on any top-level branch nobody listed. Nothing else is needed: `defer_coalesced_drag_move` records the move, the pass end replays the newest through `mouseMoveEvent`. A drag that tracks hover moves (the Magnetic Lasso) coalesces the same way; the filter does not look at the buttons.
- **Widget drags.** A pressed `QAbstractSlider` coalesces by itself (`ensure_slider_drag_coalescing`), scrub fields through `ScrubGesture` ([ui-conventions-dialogs.md](ui-conventions-dialogs.md#scrub-move-coalescing)). Any other widget whose drag does real work calls `coalesce_widget_drag_moves(widget)` (dialog_utils.hpp): its moves with a button held go through the same app-wide filter. A drag an event filter handles instead (the Type tool's box handles, whose moves MainWindow's filter takes on the handle or the canvas) calls `defer_widget_drag_move(widget, event)` at the top of its move handling, returns when it is true, and calls `flush_widget_drag_move()` before it handles the release.
- **Latest only, or every point.** A positional drag (a handle, a box, a move, a pan, a marquee) applies only the newest move; that is what the other platforms already deliver. A drag that builds a path from its points keeps every one: `defer_coalesced_drag_move` records each into `pending_stroke_positions_` and the replay hands them to the stroke as folded samples (`coalesced_stroke_positions`), so the bytes equal the moves applied one by one. Every paint stroke does this (`coalescing_retouch_stroke` is `painting_`); a new path-collecting drag reads the replayed positions the same way. A drag that reads its delta from the last applied position (panning: `last_mouse_position_`, the scrubby zoom: `zoom_drag_last_pos_`) stays correct, since a recorded move updates neither.
- **Flush points.** Presses, releases, keys and focus loss apply the recorded move first (`flush_pending_drag_move`; the widget filter for presses, releases, double clicks and keys), so a drop, a Shift constraint, Escape or an anchor click acts where the pointer is. A drag that ends any other way (a timer, a prompt) calls `flush_pending_drag_move` before it reads the position; `cancel_pointer_gestures` drops the record.
- **Only window-system moves coalesce**, so tests that send moves one by one see each applied.
- **The test.** Turn coalescing on for posted moves (`set_drag_move_coalescing_for_testing(DragMoveCoalescing::AllMoves)`, or `set_slider_drag_coalescing_all_moves_for_testing(true)` for widgets), post 30 moves at once, process events three times and check 29 coalesced (`drag_moves_coalesced`, `slider_drag_moves_coalesced_for_testing`) and the drag stands at the 30th, or where the same moves sent one by one leave it. Then send one move (applied, opens a pass) and one more (recorded), release, and check the result sits at the recorded move and the drag is one undo step. A stroke compares queued and sent bytes. Patterns: `ui_gradient_fill_handle_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`, `ui_path_transform_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`, `ui_text_box_resize_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`, `ui_view_and_selection_drags_coalesce_queued_moves`, `ui_mixer_and_pattern_stamp_strokes_coalesce_queued_moves_and_keep_bytes`.
- **Measuring on Linux.** `patchy_perf_tests canvasdrag_live` with `scripts/dev/wayland-popups/live-canvas-drag.sh <shape|gradient|pathtransform> <on|off> [pace] [hogs]` on the headless sway rig ([wayland.md](wayland.md)): `NATIVE=1` runs the aqt Qt 6.8.3 build (`remote-build.ps1 -Target linux -NativeQt`), which takes every move, and `hogs 3` holds the app to a quarter of a core. The Flatpak's Qt 6.11 merges moves and hides the lag.

## The November 2026 audit

`patchy_perf_tests dragaudit` times one move of every canvas drag, sent one by one so nothing coalesces, on a 3600x5400 noisy photo with a pentagon shape layer at fit (14% zoom), brushes at 300 px, and the repaint after it; `burst` lines post 60 moves at once and time the drain with the coalescing off and on (`PATCHY_PERF_DRAGAUDIT_CASES=name,...` picks cases). BRAIN (i9-14900HX, 32 threads), November 2026:

| Drag (now coalesced) | One move | 60 queued, every move applied | 60 queued, coalesced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Path Select transform box (Ctrl+T), moving the pentagon | 373 ms | 22.1 s | 0.83 s |
| Pattern Stamp, 300 px | 40 ms | 1.90 s | 1.58 s (every point kept) |
| Type tool box handle, 40 sentences | 20 ms | 1.61 s | 0.06 s |
| Mixer Brush, 300 px | 16 ms | 0.99 s | 0.98 s (every point kept) |
| Magnetic Lasso trace (hover) | 8.6 ms (max 18) | 1.39 s | 0.10 s |
| Scrubby zoom | 1.9 ms | 115 ms | 35 ms |
| Rotate View | 1.6 ms | 98 ms | 34 ms |
| Panning | 1.6 ms | 88 ms | 21 ms |
| Ellipse Marquee | 1.1 ms (max 3.0) | 511 ms | 67 ms |

A stroke keeps every point, so its dabs still cost what they cost (Brush's own batch, GitHub #677, is what made Brush cheap); coalescing it saves the per-event work and the repaints between. The transform box's live fill re-rasterizes the layer at full resolution, so even coalesced it shows about three steps a second on this document: a cheaper preview for it (a proxy, or a translate-only shift like Path Select's) is the open follow-up.

On the sway rig (glados, `live-canvas-drag.sh pathtransform`, 2554 moves in 1.0 s, Qt 6.8.3 `NATIVE=1`): coalescing off, the release was handled 216 s late at full speed and not within 180 s on a quarter core (`hogs 3`); on, 0.23 s late at full speed and 1.0 s on a quarter core.

Measured and left alone, each move only moving an overlay (median per move): rectangle marquee 0.08 ms, marquee resize handles 0.08, moving a selection outline 0.07, Lasso 0.06, Polygon Lasso rubber band 0.2, Quick Selection 0.06 (the classification runs at release), Red Eye 0.03, Patch 0.04, Spot Healing 0.7 at 300 px and 0.2 at 900 px (a footprint stamp; the heal runs at release), a new gradient 0.2 in Pixels and in Fill Layer mode (the preview is drawn in the repaint, 12 to 13 ms), drawing a shape 0.2, Pen handle pulls 0.17, a text box drag 0.26, Ruler 0.23, a guide 0.28. Left alone by reading, not measured: Object Selection (a rectangle until the release), artboard and crop drags (a rectangle; Crop: jobs 2040, 2042), Warp handles (rendered once per painted frame, GitHub #298), the Color panel field and hue slider (one color change), the Curves graph and the gradient editor's stops (they feed the throttled `CoalescedPreviewEmitter`, about 30 previews a second), the Navigator box (a pan) and Layers panel drags (Qt drag and drop). Brush, Eraser, Clone and the toning brushes already coalesced (Brush 15 ms and Clone 7 ms a move at 300 px).

## How it works

Wayland delivers every pointer motion uncompressed (X11, Windows and macOS merge them), and each Move-tool or Free Transform drag move recomposites its preview, so a fast pointer queued seconds of moves and the canvas repainted about once a second (GitHub #207, #443, #444; the Path Select twin is #314). `defer_coalesced_drag_move` (canvas_widget_events.cpp) applies the first move of an event-loop pass at once and only records later ones while `moving_layer_` or `dragging_transform_` holds; a queued call at the pass end applies the latest recorded position and opens the next pass. Presses, releases and key events call `flush_pending_drag_move` first, so a drop, Shift constraint or Escape acts where the pointer really is; `cancel_pointer_gestures` drops the record. Only spontaneous (window-system) moves coalesce, so tests that send moves one by one see each applied; `set_drag_move_coalescing_for_testing` lets posted moves coalesce. A held pick (`color_picking_`, GitHub #478), Path and Direct Select drags (#678, [vector-commands.md](vector-commands.md)) Gradient Fill handles (#705, [gradients-fill-handles.md](gradients-fill-handles.md)) and the drags the [audit](#the-november-2026-audit) added (Path Select's transform box, marquee drags, the Magnetic Lasso's trace, panning, Rotate View, the scrubby zoom) coalesce the same way, and so does every paint stroke (Clone, Healing, Smudge, toning, Brush, Eraser, Mixer Brush, Pattern Stamp; GitHub #597, #660, #677), keeping every recorded position as a folded sample instead of only the latest ([performance-strokes.md](performance-strokes.md)); the Type tool's box handles coalesce through `defer_widget_drag_move`; `coalescing_drag_active` names the drags ([pick-tool.md](pick-tool.md#drag-cost)). Scrubs: [ui-conventions-dialogs.md](ui-conventions-dialogs.md#scrub-move-coalescing). Counted in `drag_moves_coalesced`; pinned by `ui_move_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`, `ui_free_transform_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`, `ui_pick_drag_coalesces_queued_moves` and the two drags' own tests; `patchy_perf_tests textdrag` times a 120-move burst both ways. `textdrag_live` (`live-drag.sh`) and `canvasdrag_live` (`live-canvas-drag.sh`, in `scripts/dev/wayland-popups/`) time a real Wayland drag on the headless sway rig (move queueing delay, repaint lag and gaps against the moves' compositor timestamps). Qt 6.11 (the Flatpak) merges moves there, so it keeps up either way; Qt 6.8.3 (the AppImage, `NATIVE=1`) delivers every move and reproduces the lag on a quarter core; on an i7-1360P laptop a 120-move burst on a pixel layer takes 187 ms applied one by one and 20 ms coalesced (October 2026).

## Path Select and Gradient Fill handles (GitHub #678, #705)

Both drags joined `coalescing_drag_active` in November 2026 (`path_edit_drag_moves_points`: Path Select and Direct Select point, handle and whole-shape drags, a shape tool's Ctrl drag and the Pen's Ctrl latch; `gradient_handle_drag_`). Path Select already queued one apply per event-loop pass (#314); the canvas coalescing also skips the rest of `mouseMoveEvent` for a recorded move, and the replayed move feeds that queued apply. A Path Select step also refreshes its region on the UI thread even past half the canvas (`document_changed_drag_region`; [vector-commands.md](vector-commands.md)). Pinned by `ui_path_select_drag_coalesces_moves_and_keeps_the_fill_current` and `ui_gradient_fill_handle_drag_coalesces_queued_moves`.

`patchy_perf_tests canvasdrag_live` (`PATCHY_PERF_CANVASDRAG_KIND=shape|gradient`) with `scripts/dev/wayland-popups/live-canvas-drag.sh <shape|gradient> <on|off> [pace] [hogs]` drags on the sway rig: a Path Select drag of a pentagon over a 3600x5400 noisy photo (its corner past the canvas edge, like the reporter's), or a 1280x720 Gradient Fill's end handle. It also reports `stale_paints` (repaints while the composite was older than the document) and `lag_px` (the dragged point's distance from the pointer at each repaint). Measured on glados (24 threads) with 2554 moves in 3.4 s, `hogs 3` pinning the app to a quarter of a core:

| Case | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gradient handle, Qt 6.8.3, quarter core | the release handled 102 s late, 3 repaints | 22 ms late, 29 repaints (116 ms apart) |
| Gradient handle, Qt 6.8.3, full speed | 15 ms late, 636 repaints | 15 ms late, 372 repaints |
| Gradient handle, Qt 6.11, quarter core | 374 ms late, 19 repaints (191 ms apart) | 26 ms late, 26 repaints (104 ms apart) |
| Path Select, Qt 6.8.3, full speed | 56 of 73 repaints showed a stale fill | 0 of 472 stale |
| Path Select, Qt 6.8.3, quarter core | 5 of 40 stale, release 155 ms late | 0 of 58 stale, release 74 ms late |
| Path Select, Qt 6.11, full speed | 27 of 149 stale | 0 of 484 stale |

Qt 6.11 (the Flatpak) merges motion before Patchy sees it (1351 of the 3000 moves sent at full speed, 39 on a quarter core), so its lag stayed mild; the AppImage's Qt 6.8.3 delivers all of them.
