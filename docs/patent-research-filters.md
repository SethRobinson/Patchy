# Patent research record: filters and Smart Objects

Split from [patent-research.md](patent-research.md); each entry carries its date. The binding summary is [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md) "Filters and Smart Objects"; the review of October 20, 2026 is [patent-research-audit.md](patent-research-audit.md).

- **Architecture reviewed**: no in-force Adobe patent was found covering embedded source documents rendered into a layer, re-editable parameterized filter stacks stored with a layer, per-filter blend mode/opacity, the shared filter mask, or a gallery-style preview UI. Claim-level sweep over Google Patents and FreePatentsOnline full text (assignees Adobe and Macromedia): "smart filter" appears in zero Adobe patent specifications, "smart object" in zero Adobe claims, "filter gallery" nowhere; the CS2 (2005, Smart Objects) and CS3 (2007, Smart Filters) filing windows are expired or in their final term-adjustment months, and those windows turned up nothing on point. The Gaussian Blur, High Pass, Median, and Dust & Scratches implementations are classic pre-1999 published techniques with no in-force coverage found.
- **Plastic Wrap reviewed with a narrow original renderer (2026-07-15)**: the sweep found no in-force patent on the classic image effect described in Adobe's [filter effects reference](https://helpx.adobe.com/au/photoshop/using/filter-effects-reference.html). Patchy's implementation is one fixed deterministic local height-field formula: integer alpha-weighted luminance, edge-clamped box smoothing, local gradients, and a constant upper-left relief/specular direction. It does not infer materials or lighting, classify content, search or synthesize patches, or pick an algorithm from the image. The renderer preserves bounds and alpha; the native Photoshop `PlsW` descriptor and settings round-trip independently from Patchy's original pixel treatment. Keep this fixed-method boundary unless a new review covers a broader implementation.
- **Classic Unsharp Mask and Motion Blur stay fixed and user-directed (implemented 2026-07-15)**: Unsharp Mask applies one radius-selected low-pass, user-entered Amount, and user-entered Threshold uniformly over the layer. It never ranks pixels by sharpness, estimates blur, chooses a threshold, searches similar patches, deconvolves, or adapts its kernel by content. Motion Blur applies one straight line kernel from the user's Angle and Distance; it does not estimate camera shake, track objects, use optical flow, vary the path per pixel, or expose Blur Gallery widgets. Those omissions keep these classic operations outside the active adaptive-sharpening, patch-based sharpening, deblurring, tracking, and Blur Gallery families reviewed for this work. Do not turn either native Smart Filter into an automatic, content-adaptive, deconvolution, or multi-widget effect without a new patent review.
- **Auto Tone, Auto Contrast, and Auto Color use fixed-constant textbook methods (implemented 2026-08-01)**: percentile-clip histogram stretching (per-channel, or through one merged histogram for the composite variant) and a neutral-midtone gamma snap are decades-old published techniques: contrast stretching and histogram-based enhancement are standard in Gonzalez & Woods' Digital Image Processing editions since the 1970s, Photoshop has shipped Auto Levels since the 1990s, GIMP's Stretch Contrast / White Balance and ImageMagick's contrast-stretch implement the same percentile clip, and the neutral-midtone assumption is Buchsbaum's 1980 gray-world hypothesis. Patchy's kernel (`src/filters/auto_levels_math.{hpp,cpp}`) keeps every parameter a compile-time constant (0.1% clip per end, 128 midtone target), derives only whole-image 256-bin histograms and channel means (no spatial or sliding-window histograms, so the Surface Blur / integral-histogram families below are structurally inapplicable), never selects between algorithms by content, and reuses the levels transfer already shipped. No fresh assignee sweep was run for this feature; it was deliberately constrained inside the fixed/user-directed boundary pattern this record already established, with the binding line in legal-constraints.md. The former watch item US 2022/0207115 A1 granted as US 11914681 B2 (2024-02-27) with claims on negotiating a commonly supported feature between chained editing operations and nothing on content-derived parameters, so that caveat is closed ([patent-research-audit.md](patent-research-audit.md), 2026-10-20).
- **Surface Blur required a redesign; implemented 2026-07-14 with bit-identical output.** Adobe US 8594445 ("Fast bilateral filtering using rectangular regions", filed 2005-11-29, Active, adjusted expiration 2032-05-30 per Google Patents, 11.5-year maintenance fee paid May 2025) claims a bilateral filter whose spatial component is a 2-D box, computed by constructing a histogram of the window's pixel values and applying a range filter to that histogram centered at the center pixel's current value; the original implementation (sliding coarse/fine window histograms plus the triangle range weight) had every element of claim 1 and the sliding reuse of dependent claim 2. Adobe US 7920741 ("integral histogram convolution", filed 2007-05-31, Active, adjusted expiration 2030-02-02) additionally claims the merge-column-histograms-then-slide-along-the-scanline structure itself, which the shared Median/Dust & Scratches machinery also had. The shipped redesign removes every histogram from Surface Blur (direct per-pixel accumulation of the same integer triangle-weighted sums up to `kSurfaceBlurDirectMaximumRadius` = 8 px; per-intensity-level box sums above it, the linear-shift-invariant decomposition published by Durand & Dorsey 2002) and rebuilt Median/Dust & Scratches on ONE window histogram updated a single pixel value at a time along a serpentine traversal (Huang 1979 prior art; no column histograms). Outputs stayed byte-identical (`smart_filter_*` pixel tests unchanged); `surface_blur_direct_and_level_paths_match_reference` pins both new paths against the brute-force reference across the radius cutoff. NEVER reintroduce merged or slid window histograms in these filters, and never compute Surface Blur through ANY value histogram, before the respective expirations (2030-02-02 and ~2032-05-30; the US 8594445 figure could shorten to 2029-02-01 if a terminal disclaimer to its CIP child US 7783121 applies). Also examined, none describing the shipped design: US 7783121 (value-range-limited histograms for HDR), US 7889923 (sparse merge skipping), US 8315473 (fractional-radius border regions), US 8655097 (brush-local bilateral with automatic tolerance), US 7675524 (enclosed-block associative convolution), US 10902569 (unique-value list pruning), US 7983511 (noise-space detail classification).
- **Tilt-Shift Blur's algorithm is clear of Adobe patents**: every independent claim in the CS6 Blur Gallery family requires an element Patchy lacks. US 8831371 needs two or more blur-pattern instances iteratively combined in one mask; US 9019310 needs field-blur pins feeding a per-pixel blur-RADIUS mask with per-pixel variable kernels; US 8693776 needs a user-variable selection-edge bleed; US 8824793 needs bokeh boost thresholds; US 8891942 is video moving-object tracking; the depth-map, motion-path, and deblurring families are facially inapplicable. Patchy's single linear pattern, 0..1 mix mask, and fixed tent-blur level blending sit outside all of them; Photoshop's on-canvas widgets appear only in specifications and dependent claims. No Adobe design patent on the Blur Gallery controls was found (all 88 Adobe "display screen" design patents reviewed). Imported Photoshop Blur Gallery smart filters stay preservation-only and preview-locked in Patchy, which implements none of those methods.
- **Lens Blur and Iris Blur use narrow, explicit geometry (implemented 2026-07-15)**: Lens Blur follows Photoshop's documented Depth Map Source: None use case with one aperture chosen only from Radius, Blades, Blade Curvature, and Rotation. It performs one fixed premultiplied-alpha convolution and never infers depth, detects or boosts highlights, classifies content, or varies its kernel per pixel. Iris Blur computes one fixed round aperture blur, then blends it with the original through one ellipse from explicit Center, Angle, Width, Height, and Focus values. It has exactly one pattern, one mask, and no on-canvas iris-boundary widget. These omissions keep it outside US 8831371's independent requirement for two or more blur-pattern instances iteratively combined in a mask, US 9019310's field-blur pins and per-pixel radius kernels, US 8693776's selection bleed, and US 8824793's bokeh threshold boost. The layered-depth Lens Blur patent US 12561768 (was US 2025/0104196; to 2044-05-23) is also avoided: Patchy has no learned depth, focal matte, splatting, occlusion correction, or content-derived settings. Do not add depth maps or inference, multiple pins, combined patterns, a Photoshop-style iris gizmo, per-pixel variable-radius kernels, bokeh thresholding, highlight detection, or `blurbTransform` authoring without a new review. Adobe's current behavior references are [Lens Blur](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/effects-filters/blur-sharpen-filters/create-depth-of-field-with-lens-blur.html) and [Blur Gallery](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/blur-gallery.html); the key active family is [US 8831371](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8831371).
- **Tilt-Shift preview overlay was redesigned 2026-07-14 around a non-Adobe patent**: Apple US 8971623 ("Overlaid user interface tools for applying effects to image", priority 2012-03-06, Active, expires 2032-12-26) claims receiving a selected image location, overlaying a UI item comprising a first and second line flanking that location, each dividing the image into an area containing the location and one not, and applying the effect to the areas not containing it; dependent claims add parallel lines and name tilt-shift. The original overlay (two image-spanning solid focus lines plus two dashed full-blur lines around the center handle) had each of those elements. The overlay then marked each boundary with a short draggable grip bar (26 px half-length on the axis through the center handle) and, since 2026-10-20, with nothing at all: the focus and transition handle dots, the center crosshair and the rotation line through the center are the whole overlay, while hit-testing and symmetric width editing are unchanged. `ui_filter_gallery_tilt_shift_overlay_has_no_boundary_marks` pins it; never reintroduce a line or bar along a boundary before 2033. Re-read 2026-10-20 ([patent-research-audit.md](patent-research-audit.md)): claim 1 requires lines that divide the image but never says they span it, so the grip bars differ from the claim on one reading of "divides the image" and not on the other; the no-boundary-marks alternative was chosen on 2026-10-20 (implemented in the following commit). Published earlier work describing on-image band interfaces predates the priority date (Instagram 2011, Snapseed 2011, TiltShift Generator 2009, tiltshiftmaker ~2008). A systematic non-Adobe UI sweep was not performed; this patent surfaced incidentally, so give any new on-canvas gizmo its own check rather than assuming Adobe-only sweeps suffice.
- **Other active Adobe patents** examined, none describing the current smart filter feature set, with the adjacent feature that would trigger a re-read: US 8924851 (web-dev "web image objects" re-executing accumulated instructions, to 2032; re-read before any web-export non-destructive asset feature), US 8655112 (one-click merge-visible-layers + effect files + paintable mask on the merged layer, to 2032; Patchy's smart filters attach to ONE existing layer and must never auto-merge), US 9058699 and its parent US 8380005 (a lower-resolution preview of a composite holding an object extracted from another image, then a higher-resolution refined render; to 2029-02-02 and 2031-12-23; read 2026-10-20 in [patent-research-audit.md](patent-research-audit.md): the reduced-resolution Move and Free Transform previews have that structure; decided 2026-10-20: keep them, with the published earlier work recorded in [patent-research-preview.md](patent-research-preview.md); re-check at those dates), US 8170380 (import/export keep-appearance vs keep-editable dual-state UI), US 7973950 (print-history reapply), US 11070717 (live-camera filter recommendation), US 11393077 + US 11763430 (GAN-trained dust/scratch removal, to ~2040; the classic median+threshold filter is outside the claims), US 11335049 (cloud component sync), US 12530821 (neural compositing in non-destructive workflows, granted Jan 2026; re-read before integrating generative output into smart-filter stacks).
