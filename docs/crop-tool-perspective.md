# Perspective Crop

Read this before changing the Perspective Crop tool, its options bar, or `perspective_crop_document` (GitHub issue 328). The Crop tool it shares a flyout with is in [crop-tool.md](crop-tool.md).

The Crop flyout's second member (`tools.perspective_crop`, `CanvasTool::PerspectiveCrop`, no default key; Shift+C cycles the flyout). It rectifies a skewed area (a photographed page or sign) onto a rectangle. Session code in canvas_widget_perspective_crop.cpp; commit in `MainWindow::commit_perspective_crop_quad` (main_window_layer_ops.cpp); core in `perspective_crop_document` (document_geometry.cpp).

- **Session.** Unlike Crop, picking the tool frames nothing. The first press decides: a drag lays out a box (its four corners), a click places the first corner and three more clicks place the rest (a dashed rubber band follows the pointer). A fourth click that would fold the quad is refused with "The corners must form a convex shape". New corners are stored in output order: the one nearest the top left first, then clockwise on screen (`upright_quad`), so click order and direction never mirror or turn the result. On the finished quad the press goes through Free Transform's quad hit zones (`quad_handle_at`, shared with the distorted Free Transform box; [move-tool.md](move-tool.md) EdgesAndCorners rules, no rotate stem): a corner moves on its own (the Distort gesture cursor), a mid-edge handle or the edge line moves that side's two corners together (resize cursor by the edge's on-screen direction), the inside moves the whole quad, and the reach just outside a corner rotates all four corners about the quad's center (the corner rotate cursor; Shift snaps the turn to 15 degrees). A corner or edge drag that would fold the quad keeps the last convex shape (Free Transform's rule). Drags never reorder the corners, so the output's top is always corners 0 and 1: rotating the quad turns the result. The Turn button (`perspectiveCropTurnButton`, `turn_perspective_crop_top`) moves the top to the next side without moving a corner (the left side becomes the top, a quarter turn clockwise; for a page shot sideways). Enter or the check button commits, Esc or the X clears the corners. A tool switch, edit lock or document swap cancel (never commit), like Crop; `perspective_crop_session_pending` joins the recovery busy check.
- **Overlay.** The Crop shield options (color, opacity, Show Cropped Area off hides it) shade outside the quad; the outline and handles use the Crop box inks (unthemed: they sit on artwork). Show Grid (`tools/perspectiveCropShowGrid`, default on) draws the rectified grid through `QTransform::squareToQuad`, about one line per 40 widget pixels of the longest side (2 to 12 divisions), dark-under-light like the crop guides. The up marker (`draw_perspective_crop_up_marker`, GitHub issue 328 follow-up) shows which edge becomes the result's top: that edge drawn as a 2 px light stroke over a 4 px dark one, plus an arrow (a short stem and a filled head, 10 to 26 px out) beyond its middle pointing away from the quad's center, light filled with a dark outline, the marching ants' light-over-dark pairing so it reads on dark and light artwork. Mid-edge handles join the corner squares once the quad is finished.
- **Options bar.** W and H (`UnitIntSpinBox`, pixel native with unit tokens at the document PPI like Crop's Size fields; 0 shows blank) set the result size, blank taking `perspective_crop_auto_size` (the mean of the top and bottom edge lengths by the mean of the left and right ones); a swap button sits between them. Resolution (pixels per inch, blank keeps the document's) only changes the document's print resolution. Front Image fills all three from the active document, Clear empties them (Photoshop's two buttons). None of the three persist. Groups ([options-bar.md](options-bar.md) rules): W, swap, H | Resolution | Front Image, Clear | Turn | Show Grid | Apply, Cancel (`ui_perspective_crop_options_bar_groups_its_controls`, one row at 1366 px).
- **Commit (`perspective_crop_document`).** The result rect maps onto the quad through `homography_from_rect_to_quad` (core/warp_mesh.hpp). Every pixel layer, mask and channel resamples through the rotated crop's sampler (`sample_mapped_crop_pixels`, shared with `sample_rotated_crop_pixels`: bilinear with clamped edges, 16/32-bit layers in their own float domain, nearest across a depth change), so it is deterministic and works at every depth; no area averaging on the far side, Free Transform's perspective rule. A rect quad the size of its output copies pixels exactly. Layer bounds are the projected bounding box clipped to the new canvas (a layer reaching across the quad's horizon takes the whole canvas). Fill rules are the rotated crop's: a "Background" layer is rebuilt canvas-sized with the background color under area the quad takes from beyond the canvas, other layers get transparency there (opaque 8-bit layers gain alpha). Non-pixel layers: a parallelogram quad is affine, so text transforms, smart-object placements and vector data ride it exactly like the other crops (type stays live); a true perspective quad rasterizes text layers (`clear_layer_text_metadata`, now in core; Free Transform refuses perspective on type for the same reason), gives smart objects a perspective placement (`map_smart_object_placement`, Free Transform's rule) and maps shape layers, vector masks and paths through `transform_layer_vector_data_projective`/`transform_vector_path_projective`. Documents with artboards, Smart Filters or unparsed smart objects are refused as for Crop. One undo step ("Perspective Crop").
- **Scripts.** `doc.perspectiveCrop(corners, {width, height, resolution})`: four `[x, y]` or `{x, y}` corners in output order, the commit's rules with white under the Background (the 2-arg `crop` convention).
