## Selection tools

- **Quick Select solves once on mouse release.** Do not add live per-mouse-move classification or a selection preview before November 3, 2029 (Adobe US 8050498). Enhance Edge remains geometric, the solve uses one window per stroke, and input never selects among algorithms automatically (US 10698588 claims toggling graph cut and level-set refinement from the Add/Remove mode plus where the click lands relative to the selection; never switch mechanisms on that pair). `ui_quick_select_stroke_selects_object_and_is_undoable` enforces the no-mid-drag behavior.
- **Automatic selection (Select Subject, Object Selection, their script calls) is one network solve from one prompt, combined by plain boolean** (October 2026, record in [patent-research-selection.md](patent-research-selection.md), design in [ai-selection.md](ai-selection.md)). Object Selection sends either one box or one positive point per solve, on mouse release only: never a negative point (US 11314982, to 2037), never a box plus points or any two prompt modalities together (US 11568627, to 2037), never a previous mask back into the decoder (SAM `mask_input` stays zero; US 11676279, to 2041), and never a distance-map input. Add and Subtract are selection booleans, not network inputs. Nothing segmented is shown before release, and there is no hover highlight or precomputed per-object masks (US 8050498; Adobe's allowed hover-preview application US 2023/0129341). Select Subject runs one fixed salient model once over the whole image and upsamples once: no network-located crop, second pass, or refinement of sub-regions (US 11244195 / US 12591950, to 2040). Select Background and Remove Background reuse that one result through plain inversion or as a layer mask. No detectors, class labels, text queries, or automatic choice between models (US 11107219, US 11631234, US 11972569, US 12260557); the user picks the command, and Object Selection's Standard or High Quality model by setting and download, never by image content. Zoom-to-box encodes the user's box grown by fixed geometry (`sam_box_region`) once and decodes the box once: never a network-located region, a second pass, or a refinement of a mask. Any SAM-family model must keep SAM's decoder without encoder skip connections (US 12051225 claim 16; SAM 2 and HQ-SAM wait on a further review). No edge-refinement brush or learned matting stage on the result (US 10068361, US 11593948). Changing a model, adding a prompt type, or adding refinement needs a new review. A single-pass whole-image matting model is reviewed in the record (2026-10-12): allowed as a replacement or a user-chosen alternative, never merged with or fed by the general model; Adobe US 12051225 claim 16 (transformer encoder plus skip-connected decoder producing an alpha matte, to about 2042-09) is an open item against the BiRefNet architecture, so Select Subject, Select Background and Remove Background stay out of releases until it is resolved (`PATCHY_ENABLE_SELECT_SUBJECT` off; Object Selection ships).
- Magnetic Lasso and brush shape-dynamics patents expired, so live snap display is allowed. Magnetic Lasso still constructs its selection region once in `finish_magnetic_lasso()`, separate from Quick Select's prohibited live region classification.

## Filters and Smart Objects

- Smart Objects, Smart Filters and Filter Gallery architecture are allowed subject to the boundaries below.
- **Surface Blur may not compute through a value histogram.** Median and Dust & Scratches keep exactly one window histogram updated one pixel value at a time; never introduce column, merged or slid histograms before the expirations (US 8594445, US 7920741). The calibrated outputs and constraint comments in `smart_filter_renderer.cpp` are binding.
- **Tilt-Shift Blur draws no boundary marks.** Its overlay is the center crosshair, the rotation line and the four handle dots (decided 2026-10-20; US 8971623, audit item 3). Never add a line or bar along a band edge before 2033, and keep every mark off the image perimeter. Pinned by `ui_filter_gallery_tilt_shift_overlay_has_no_boundary_marks`; the algorithm itself is clear.
- **Lens Blur and Iris Blur remain fixed and geometric.** Lens Blur is one explicitly selected aperture convolution with no depth inference, classification, highlight detection, or per-pixel kernel. Iris Blur computes one round blur and blends it through one explicit ellipse. Do not add multiple pins, combined blur patterns, Photoshop-style iris boundary widgets, variable-radius kernels, bokeh thresholds, learned depth/focal mattes, splatting or native `blurbTransform` authoring without a new review (US 8831371, US 9019310, US 8824793, US 12561768). Imported Blur Gallery stays preview-locked; Lens/Iris stay raster-only.
- **Liquify remains manual, generic, and raster-only.** Never add face detection, landmarks, automatic face regions, face-feature controls or deformation derived from a detected face without a new review (US 9646195). Do not invent or partially author Photoshop's native Liquify Smart Filter descriptor. Ordinary layers save the raster result, Smart Objects require rasterization, imported unsupported stacks stay byte-preserved.
- **Plastic Wrap keeps its fixed local height-field formula:** integer alpha-weighted luminance, box smoothing, gradient, and relief. Do not add material or lighting inference, classification, patch search or synthesis, or content-driven algorithm selection. Its PSD surface is the native three-integer `PlsW` descriptor; Patchy's renderer preserves bounds and alpha without claiming Photoshop-identical pixels.
- **Unsharp Mask and Motion Blur remain fixed and user-directed.** Unsharp Mask uses one radius-selected low-pass plus explicit Amount and Threshold. Motion Blur uses one straight-line kernel from explicit Angle and Distance. Do not add ranking, inferred blur, automatic thresholds, patch search, deconvolution, shake estimation, tracking, optical flow, content-adaptive kernels, per-pixel paths or Blur Gallery-style widgets without a new review.
- **The auto adjustments (Auto Tone, Auto Contrast, Auto Color) use only whole-image tonal statistics with fixed constants** (August 2026): one 256-bin histogram per channel (merged for Auto Contrast) plus per-channel means, a fixed 0.1% clip per end, and the fixed 128 neutral midtone target, applied through the shared levels transfer. No spatial or sliding-window histograms, no content classification, no automatic selection between algorithms, and no learned or content-adaptive parameters; any of those needs a new review. The kernel and constants live in `src/filters/auto_levels_math.{hpp,cpp}`; this bullet is the binding boundary its constraint comment references. Prior-art record: [patent-research.md](patent-research.md).
- **Content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting** (claim-level review 2026-09-22, record in [patent-research-inpainting.md](patent-research-inpainting.md)). Classic exemplar-based inpainting is ALLOWED: fill the hole from its boundary inward in a fixed, deterministic priority order and, for each target patch, choose the best source patch by an EXHAUSTIVE scan of a bounded search window in the same image at the working resolution (integer distance metric, fixed tie-breaks, or Efros-Leung's own epsilon selection among the near-best candidates of that same scan) (expired Criminisi and Efros-Leung prior art). Content inspection for source choice is permitted in that form (`core/exemplar_inpaint.cpp`). Forbidden while the cited patents are active: (1) any nearest-neighbor-field search whose candidates come from neighbouring patches' current offsets (propagation) or from perturbing an offset (random search), iterated (Adobe US 8285055, to 2031-04-09; every independent claim needs both phases), so each target patch's search is a plain scan independent of other patches' chosen offsets; (2) precomputed pruned candidate lists, "patch loops", or belief-propagation reconstruction over patch labels (US 8340463, to 2031-10-24); (3) reshuffling, retargeting, or patch optimization under user feature or orientation constraints with model fitting and projection (US 8355592, to 2031-08-12); (4) computing a patch coordinate map at a downsampled resolution and upsampling it to fill at full resolution (US 9396530, to 2034-10-15): no coarse-to-fine offset maps; (5) machine-learned object boundaries or per-object masks (US 10825148, to 2039) and sources from other images (US 9697595 / US 10467739). The implementation stays in that plain form: no patch rotation, scale, or mirror adaptation, no gradient-domain color adaptation, and no user-painted sampling region without a new check (Photoshop's 2018+ Content-Aware Fill workspace has unreviewed Adobe filings). Its distance metric may carry a fixed geometric term (a per-pixel penalty on the source offset), and its tone match is the recorded healing membrane applied to the low-pass band (frequency separation): Dirichlet interpolation of boundary values, never composited gradients. Its only randomness is the variation pick (`ExemplarInpaintOptions::attempt` > 0, the Remove Object dialog's Reroll; claim check dated 2026-09-24 in the record): a splitmix64 draw among the candidates within a fixed slack of the best distance, recomputed per target from a fresh exhaustive scan of the same windows and dropped after the copy, never stored, seeded from another patch, perturbed, or propagated. Keep it that way: no candidate list may outlive its target, seed a neighbour, or narrow a later search. Also unchanged: no ML/GAN dust-and-scratch removal while the US 11393077 family is active; re-read US 12530821 before integrating generative output into Smart Filter stacks; never create a one-click merge-visible plus effects plus masked-copy operation without re-reading US 8655112. Classic user-directed healing remains allowed.
- **Spot Healing, Remove Object, and the Patch tool stay inside the classic-healing carve-out** (August and September 2026, record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). All blend with the healing membrane that expired US 6587592 itself taught (`core/heal_membrane.cpp`): destination-minus-source offsets from the region BOUNDARY interpolated harmonically across the interior by a fixed deterministic relaxation, then added to the source texture. Never composite source gradients or build a Poisson guidance field (engineering boundary; audit record). Sources stay user-directed or shape-derived: Spot Healing uses one coherent rigid mapping chosen from the stroke footprint's shape alone (`core/spot_heal.cpp`, fixed candidate order, geometry-only validity scores, pixel content never read); Remove Object is the same mapping chosen from the SELECTION's shape, and its only additional input is the user's repeat count (`attempt`), which indexes the geometrically valid candidates in their fixed order; the count never derives from pixel content and no candidate is ever scored by content; the Patch tool's only source is the user's drag offset, previewed as a raw translated blit with the region built geometrically once on release. Content-driven source selection may be added to them only as the exhaustive exemplar search the bullet above allows, and no live per-move classification or healing preview may be added while US 8050498 (live classify-and-display, to Nov 3, 2029) is active. The Remove Object dialog (September 2026) obeys this: a fill runs only on an explicit Reroll click, a slider release, or a settled slider/spin value (one run per discrete change, coalesced), never per slider move or pointer move, and the canvas shows the finished fill of the last run, not a live classification of input. Any on-canvas gizmo beyond the drag outline with its unprocessed content ghost and the plain context menu needs its own check.

## G'MIC engine

Design: [gmic.md](gmic.md); record: [patent-research-gmic.md](patent-research-gmic.md).

- **The vendored interpreter is built without its patch-based algorithms.** The patch correspondence search (`_matchpatch` in `CImg.h`: PatchMatch propagation plus random search, the two phases of Adobe US 8285055, to 2031-04-09) and patch-based inpainting (`inpaint_patch` in `gmic.cpp`) are compiled out with `patchy_gmic_no_patch_algorithms`, not merely hidden. Never build the engine without that definition; `gmic_engine_leaves_out_programs_and_patch_algorithms` pins it. A filter that would need them at some setting fails there with "Not available in this build". Non-patch `inpaint` (neighbour averaging, no patch search) and shared diffusion/morphology helpers stay: offered filters use them internally for hole filling, color interpolation and gradients. The excluded `fx_inpaint*` filter wrappers remain blocked.
- **Filters are offered only from `patchy_filters_allowlist.txt`.** Left out by name (`NAMED` in `scripts/dev/gmic/build_allowlist.py`): every Inpaint filter; patch-based texture synthesis, smoothing and style transfer; Seamcarve; interactive segmentation and colorization, including the lineart colorizers; neural-network filters; Blend [Seamless] (gradient-domain compositing); Blur [Motion] (a blur kernel shaped by a user-drawn point path with weight decay; Adobe US 9723204 claims 1 and 12, to 2035); Blur [Depth-of-Field] (a per-pixel blur level around a focus point; Adobe US 9019310 claim 1, to 2033); and Custom Code. Every other filter that runs in this build is offered. The named list is by rule; the family triage is in the record; adding a filter in those areas needs the same review as a native feature.
- **Point handles are plain position markers** (`CanvasWidget::set_preview_point_handles`): one per point the filter declares, never added or removed by Patchy, no per-marker amount or dial, nothing drawn around them (no extent, ring, boundary or region), and each only sets a number the dialog also shows. G'MIC's own preview commands (which draw guides, ellipses and the motion spline) are never run. Blur [Radial] and Blur [Angular] show their centre this way: no independent claim of the Lens/Iris family above (US 8831371, US 9019310, US 8824793) is met by a marker alone, and none distinguishes a marker from a number field. `report_points()` gives Blur [Motion] and Blur [Depth-of-Field] no marker even if they were ever offered.
- **Enforce exclusions inside the interpreter.** `gmic_command_policy.hpp` replaces restricted apply, preview, private-helper and algorithm definitions with refusal stubs at registration; built-in patch `denoise` is refused at dispatch. This covers nested formula `run()`, aliases, copied bodies and redefinitions. Keep the core refusal tests and UI unchanged-layer test in [gmic.md](gmic.md). It does not clear user-written algorithms.
- **No external programs, no network, no other G'MIC code.** The engine is built with `gmic_noexec` and `cimg_no_system_calls` and without curl. Community filters and online updates are never loaded (mixed licensing, mutable after review).
- **Licensing.** Only the CeCILL-C sources are used. Nothing comes from G'MIC-Qt (GPL 3), and FFTW (GPL) is never linked; the engine's FFT is pocketfft (BSD-3-Clause).

## Brush behavior

- **Flow and Airbrush remain flat-stamp operations.** They may repeat the current 2D stamp at fixed spatial/time cadences and cap accumulated paint at Opacity. Do not add particles, a 3D spray cone, stylus-pose projection, velocity-dependent deposition, bristle or fluid simulation, wet/dirty paint, or bidirectional transfer without re-reading US 8854342 and the US 9600907/10489937 family.
- **Brush Texture is one static world-anchored grayscale mask.** Pressure, velocity, direction, rotation, and stylus pose may not select, compose, or weight texture channels while US 10902645 and US 8896579 are active.
- **Color Dynamics** only interpolates the two selected colors and applies direct HSV offsets. It may not sample the canvas, mix paint, or build a 3D RGB Bezier path (US 10924633).
- **Wet Edges** keeps one maximum-coverage union mask per stroke with no canvas-color feedback, paint store, fluid, pigment, paper, drying, or pickup simulation.
- **Dual Brush** remains one fixed secondary computed mask, never a component graph or hierarchy.
- **Mixer Brush** runs LIMITED continuous pickup, reviewed in the 2026-08-14 claim re-review (record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). The pickup store is ONE running premultiplied-RGBA average fed only by per-dab footprint samples of the canvas. It must NEVER be seeded from or blended with the foreground/deposit color: the foreground enters only a transient per-dab linear interpolation whose result is never written back (Microsoft US 7768525 claim 14, to 2028-03-07; kept as is, decided 2026-10-20, re-check then). Still prohibited: per-pixel/per-bristle brush state or any spatial pickup texture, paint-amount/fill channels (US 8749572), erodible height-map tips or persisted worn state (US 10217253), non-linear curvature-parameterized color mixing (US 10924633 B1), region-sweep loading modes or multi-cell brush models (US 8654143), and fluid/drying/cleaning simulation beyond the calibrated Wet-0 linear dry-out. ABR input-driven texture depth imports as a static depth with a notice.
- **Pattern Stamp** performs a direct lookup into one static tile. It may not inspect the destination for illumination, gradients, shading, or reflections; adapt or synthesize the pattern from content; or assemble runtime texture channels driven by pressure, direction, velocity, or orientation (US 9864922 and US 10902645).

## Vector tracing

- **Trace Image to Shapes traces once, on explicit request, into static shape layers** (August 2026, record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md), design in [image-trace.md](image-trace.md)). The traced group has no link back to its source layer: never add a live tracing object that re-traces when the source pixels change, never re-trace automatically after the dialog closes, and never add overlay or outline renditions of the source in the preview or on the canvas before 2028-02-28 (Adobe US 7636097). Parameters stay global for the whole layer: no per-region, brush-painted, or localized tracing parameters (Adobe US 9972073 / US 10521889, to 2036). No gesture-driven path extraction between two user-picked points on detected edges (Adobe US 11257256, to 2040). The fitter stays the per-run Schneider fit with independent runs: no coordinated global least-squares smoothness solve across adjacent segments, no tangent-vector construction with angle/curvature thresholds placing segmentation points (Bending Spoons US 10212457 family, to 2036).

## Scanned-photo division

- **Divide Scanned Photos detects once, on explicit request, on a still image** (August 2026, research record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). Detection runs only when the user invokes the command or changes a dialog parameter; regions come from fixed deterministic geometry with global parameters and user-editable regions, and extraction is one fixed resample per region (axis crop, rotation, or homography with the expired Zhang aspect recovery). Never add live-camera or per-frame detection, automatic capture or quality-triggered frame selection, ML or content classification, per-region automatic parameter adaptation, or automatic selection between algorithms: those are the claim elements of the active mobile-capture families (Mitek US 9672510 to 2028; Kofax US 9760788 / US 9754163).

## Alignment guides and Align/Distribute

- **Alignment snapping snaps edges and centers only, never spacing** (September 2026, record in [patent-research-alignment.md](patent-research-alignment.md), design in [alignment.md](alignment.md)). While a layer is dragged, Patchy may snap its edges and centers to other layers' opaque bounds, the selection, guides, the grid, and the canvas, and draw a line through the matched edge or center. It must never position a dragged layer equidistant between other layers or at a neighbouring pair's spacing, and must never show spacing or distance measurements during a drag (Adobe US 10782861, to 2036). Every edge and center of every moving layer is a snap source regardless of where the drag was grabbed (Adobe US 9250786, to 2034). The snap search stays a plain linear comparison of edge coordinates against a tolerance, with no cursor guide line, reference-point distance index, or sorted candidate structure (Adobe US 9442623, to 2033). Targets are layer bounds and user-drawn geometry only, never objects detected inside a layer's pixels (Adobe US 11562488, to 2041). Keep the one-shot Align and Distribute commands out of the drag path.

## Artboards

- **No artboard is chosen from edit history** (October 2026, record in [patent-research-artboards.md](patent-research-artboards.md)): a new artboard's size and place come from the user's explicit input or from the artboard whose add button was clicked, never from the most recently edited artboard (Adobe US 8667406, to 2031-02-04). Angular snapping, cross-artboard change propagation and content reflow need a new review.

## Adjustment layers

- **Black & White mixes only the user's six weights** (October 2026, record in [patent-research-adjustments.md](patent-research-adjustments.md)). Never derive the weights or the gray from image content, such as an Auto button (Adobe US 7706606, to 2029), and never drive the weights from one control along a path (Apple US 9092893, to 2032), without a new review.
- **Color Lookup applies only the table in the user's own file or a LUT file the user picks** (October 2026, record in [patent-research-adjustments.md](patent-research-adjustments.md)). Patchy reads the LUT text a `clrL` block keeps (`.cube`, `.3dl`, `.look`) and its embedded ICC device-link or abstract profile, and applies the table unmodified by fixed interpolation. A LUT file the user picks, in those formats only, is embedded in the user's document as Photoshop embeds it. Never ship, copy, recreate, fetch, or list by name the looks Photoshop or any third party bundles. An unedited `clrL` block is written back byte for byte. Never add, without a new review: LUT generation from image content, learned looks, look matching between images, table editing, or LUT export.

## Soft proof and gamut warning

- **Proof Colors and Gamut Warning stay the generic ICC view** (October 2026, record in [patent-research-proofing.md](patent-research-proofing.md), design in [cmyk.md](cmyk.md)). One fixed transform through the user's chosen CMYK profile and rendering intent, and one flat fill for out-of-gamut colors, both from Little CMS, applied to the whole canvas view on the user's menu choice. Never add, without a new review: a view correction for a measured monitor, paper white or viewing condition beyond the ICC intents; a proof chosen or adjusted per region or from image content; automatic editing of pixels into gamut; learned gamut mapping. The record is a prior-art note, not a claim-level review.

## Format, copyright, trademarks, and assets

- **Open from Clipboard** makes only an unsaved in-memory raster document ([clipboard.md](clipboard.md)); never extend it to automatic clipboard-to-file/cloud storage or shortcut-bound destinations.
- PSD interoperability comes from observed output of a licensed Photoshop, never decompilation. Never copy verbatim Adobe specification text into the repository; link to it. Use self-authored fixtures only and do not support the undocumented PSDC cloud-document format.
- "Smart Object(s)", "Smart Filter(s)", "Filter Gallery", and "PSD" are not Adobe trademarks in the reviewed record. PHOTOSHOP is registered and policed. Use referential language such as "compatible with Adobe Photoshop"; never put Adobe marks in Patchy's name, icons or branding, and keep README.md's Trademark Notice.
- Bundled art and textures must never come from AI image generation. Acceptable sources: code generation, or real photography/human artwork under a commercially compatible license, preferably CC0. Every third-party asset needs a `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md` entry and ships losslessly. Poly Haven textures are human-made.
- HEIC/HEIF decoding must use a platform or browser-provided HEVC decoder. Native builds keep their platform-codec paths; Linux dlopens the distro's libheif and its HEVC plug-in at run time and ships nothing (never bundle libheif in the AppImage). The browser build may statically link the vendored libheif container parser and its WebCodecs adapter, but every software HEVC codec backend and HEVC encoder must remain disabled. Never vendor or link libde265, x265 or another HEVC decoder/encoder, and never add HEIC encoding. The WASM CMake option block, the runtime `VideoDecoder.isConfigSupported()` check, and the distribution obligations in `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md` are binding. See [heif.md](heif.md).
- JPEG XR decoding and encoding must use Windows' in-box Imaging Component codec. Never vendor or link jxrlib, libjxr, or another JPEG XR codec, and never add a JPEG XR path to a non-Windows build, without a new review. Patchy ships no codec, so no JPEG XR patent claim applies to the binary. Friendlier than HEVC (Open Specification Promise, 2006-2009 priorities) but never claim-reviewed, so no licence to vendor a codec. Design: [jxr.md](jxr.md); wiring: [file-formats.md](file-formats.md).
- AVIF may ship in-process AV1 codecs (decided October 16, 2026; record in [patent-research-formats.md](patent-research-formats.md)): libavif 1.4.2 over dav1d 1.5.4 (decode) and libaom 3.15.2 (encode), BSD-2; libaom's AOM Patent License 1.0 text is in `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md`. Conditions: no other AV1 encoder (SVT-AV1, rav1e) or AV2/avm code without a new review; pinned, unmodified releases with LICENSE files. Design: [avif.md](avif.md).
- JPEG 2000 (ISO/IEC 15444-1), OpenEXR and Radiance HDR may ship in-process codecs (October 2026 read; record in [patent-research-formats.md](patent-research-formats.md)): OpenJPEG (BSD-2) for JPEG 2000 Part 1 only, tinyexr (BSD-3) for OpenEXR, Patchy's own RGBE for HDR. Conditions: Part 1 only (no Part 2 extensions, no HTJ2K beyond what OpenJPEG's decoder carries, no JPIP), library sources only with LICENSE files beside them and `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md` entries, no deep or multi-part EXR. Upgrades need a new licence read.
- JPEG XL may vendor libjxl (October 2026 engineering read; record in [patent-research-formats.md](patent-research-formats.md)). Conditions, all binding: (1) a pinned upstream release with no algorithmic changes, since Google's `PATENTS` grant covers only "this implementation" and excludes claims a modification causes; build and symbol-namespace changes are fine, and the libjxl `LICENSE` and `PATENTS` text plus Highway (BSD-3 election), Brotli and skcms/lcms2 notices go in `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md`. (2) Libraries only: no tools, plug-ins, or system codec discovery. (3) The encoder runs with patches and dots off (`JXL_ENC_FRAME_SETTING_PATCHES` and `_DOTS` = 0), and Patchy must not enable any path that classifies image regions as photographic or not to pick lossy versus lossless coding per region (Cloudinary US 12,243,274, to about 2043, outside Google's grant). (4) Never restructure the ANS decoder so symbol output is conditional on a state sufficiency test or the state update is deferred to the next iteration, and never add a per-stream flush/re-initialize flag (Microsoft US 11,234,023 and its pending continuation US 2022/0109891). (5) A libjxl upgrade that changes `PATENTS`, or a newly exposed encoder feature, needs a new review. Use "JPEG XL" only as the format name; no JPEG committee logos.
- Shipping dependencies must allow commercial distribution. GPL tools may be used for development or fixture generation only when not linked into or shipped with Patchy.
- No Photoshop SDK code or Adobe-created assets may enter this repository or a Patchy binary. The only exception is the self-authored regression material in `test-fixtures/psd/`, which is not Adobe artwork. The classic filter plug-in ABI that the legacy `.8bf` host speaks (`src/plugins/host/legacy_filter_abi.hpp`, `src/plugins/pipl.cpp`) is Patchy's own declaration written from the public plug-in documentation and from the observed behaviour of real plug-ins (September 2026): structure layouts, selector numbers and callback signatures are interoperability facts, but no SDK header, sample, resource template or documentation text may be copied into it, and every later addition follows the same rule. The `.8bf` files under `local-test-fixtures/photoshop-plugins/` (fetched by `scripts/fetch-test-plugins`) are third-party binaries used only as test data, never committed, linked into or shipped with Patchy.
