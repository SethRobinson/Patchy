# Binding legal and licensing constraints

Read this file before changing any feature named below or introducing an adjacent implementation. The claim-level research record lives in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md) and, for content-aware fill, [patent-research-inpainting.md](patent-research-inpainting.md); detailed compatibility records live in [smart-objects.md](smart-objects.md), [selection-tools.md](selection-tools.md), [liquify.md](liquify.md), [brushes.md](brushes.md), [vector-tools.md](vector-tools.md), [image-trace.md](image-trace.md), [file-formats.md](file-formats.md), and [af-format.md](af-format.md). These constraints are binding even when a broader feature appears technically straightforward.

## Selection tools

- **Quick Select solves once on mouse release.** Do not add live per-mouse-move classification or a selection preview before November 3, 2029 (Adobe US 8050498). Enhance Edge remains geometric, the solve uses one window per stroke, and input never selects among algorithms automatically. `ui_quick_select_stroke_selects_object_and_is_undoable` enforces the no-mid-drag behavior.
- Magnetic Lasso and brush shape-dynamics patents are expired, so live snap display is allowed. Magnetic Lasso still constructs its selection region once in `finish_magnetic_lasso()`, clearly separate from Quick Select's prohibited live region classification.

## Filters and Smart Objects

- Smart Objects, Smart Filters, and Filter Gallery architecture are cleared subject to the design boundaries below.
- **Surface Blur may not compute through a value histogram.** Median and Dust & Scratches keep exactly one window histogram updated one pixel value at a time; never introduce column, merged, or slid histograms before the relevant expirations (US 8594445 and US 7920741). The calibrated outputs and constraint comments in `smart_filter_renderer.cpp` are binding.
- **Tilt-Shift Blur uses short grip bars.** Never restore lines spanning or dividing the image before 2033 (Apple US 8971623). The algorithm itself is clear.
- **Lens Blur and Iris Blur remain fixed and geometric.** Lens Blur is one explicitly selected aperture convolution with no depth inference, classification, highlight detection, or per-pixel kernel. Iris Blur computes one round blur and blends it through one explicit ellipse. Do not add multiple pins, combined blur patterns, Photoshop-style iris boundary widgets, variable-radius kernels, bokeh thresholds, learned depth/focal mattes, splatting, or native `blurbTransform` authoring without a new review (US 8831371, US 9019310, US 8824793, US 2025/0104196). Imported Blur Gallery stacks remain byte-preserved and preview-locked; Patchy's Lens/Iris filters remain raster-only.
- **Liquify remains manual, generic, and raster-only.** Never add face detection, landmarks, automatic face regions, face-feature controls, or deformation derived from a detected face without a new review (US 9646195). Do not invent or partially author Photoshop's native Liquify Smart Filter descriptor. Ordinary layers save the raster result, Smart Objects require rasterization, and imported unsupported stacks remain byte-preserved.
- **Plastic Wrap keeps its fixed local height-field formula:** integer alpha-weighted luminance, box smoothing, gradient, and relief. Do not add material or lighting inference, classification, patch search or synthesis, or content-driven algorithm selection. Its PSD surface is the native three-integer `PlsW` descriptor; Patchy's renderer preserves bounds and alpha without claiming Photoshop-identical pixels.
- **Unsharp Mask and Motion Blur remain fixed and user-directed.** Unsharp Mask uses one radius-selected low-pass plus explicit Amount and Threshold. Motion Blur uses one straight-line kernel from explicit Angle and Distance. Do not add ranking, inferred blur, automatic thresholds, patch search, deconvolution, shake estimation, tracking, optical flow, content-adaptive kernels, per-pixel paths, or Blur Gallery-style widgets without a new review.
- **The auto adjustments (Auto Tone, Auto Contrast, Auto Color) use only whole-image tonal statistics with fixed constants** (August 2026): one 256-bin histogram per channel (merged for Auto Contrast) plus per-channel means, a fixed 0.1% clip per end, and the fixed 128 neutral midtone target, applied through the shared levels transfer. No spatial or sliding-window histograms, no content classification, no automatic selection between algorithms, and no learned or content-adaptive parameters; any of those needs a new review. The kernel and constants live in `src/filters/auto_levels_math.{hpp,cpp}`; this bullet is the binding boundary its constraint comment references. Prior-art record: [patent-research.md](patent-research.md).
- **Content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting** (claim-level review 2026-09-22, record in [patent-research-inpainting.md](patent-research-inpainting.md)). Classic exemplar-based inpainting is ALLOWED: fill the hole from its boundary inward in a fixed, deterministic priority order and, for each target patch, choose the best source patch by an EXHAUSTIVE scan of a bounded search window in the same image at the working resolution (integer distance metric, fixed tie-breaks, or Efros-Leung's own epsilon selection among the near-best candidates of that same scan), the expired Criminisi teaching (Microsoft US 6987520, expired 2023-03-30; Efros-Leung 1999 prior art). Content inspection for source choice is permitted in that form (implemented as Remove Object's default in `core/exemplar_inpaint.cpp`, whose header carries the constraint comment). Forbidden while the cited patents are active: (1) any nearest-neighbor-field search whose candidates come from neighbouring patches' current offsets (propagation) or from perturbing an offset (random search), iterated (Adobe US 8285055, to 2031-04-09; every independent claim needs both phases), so each target patch's search is a plain scan independent of other patches' chosen offsets; (2) precomputed pruned candidate lists, "patch loops", or belief-propagation reconstruction over patch labels (US 8340463, to 2031-10-24); (3) reshuffling, retargeting, or patch optimization under user feature or orientation constraints with model fitting and projection (US 8355592, to 2031-08-12); (4) computing a patch coordinate map at a downsampled resolution and upsampling it to fill at full resolution (US 9396530, to 2034-10-15): no coarse-to-fine offset maps; (5) machine-learned object boundaries or per-object masks (US 10825148, to 2039) and sources from other images (US 9697595 / US 10467739). The implementation stays in that plain form: no patch rotation, scale, or mirror adaptation, no gradient-domain color adaptation, and no user-painted sampling region without a new check (Photoshop's 2018+ Content-Aware Fill workspace has unreviewed Adobe filings). Its distance metric may carry a fixed geometric term (a per-pixel penalty on the source offset), and its tone match is the cleared healing membrane applied to the low-pass band (frequency separation): Dirichlet interpolation of boundary values, never composited gradients. Its only randomness is the variation pick (`ExemplarInpaintOptions::attempt` > 0, the Remove Object dialog's Reroll; claim check dated 2026-09-24 in the record): a splitmix64 draw among the candidates within a fixed slack of the best distance, recomputed per target from a fresh exhaustive scan of the same windows and dropped after the copy, never stored, seeded from another patch, perturbed, or propagated. That is Efros-Leung 1999 prior art, not US 8285055's perturbation phase (no offset is perturbed and nothing iterates) and not US 8340463's precomputed pruned candidate lists (nothing is precomputed or kept). Keep it that way: no candidate list may outlive its target, seed a neighbour, or narrow a later search. Also unchanged: no ML/GAN dust-and-scratch removal while the US 11393077 family is active; re-read US 12530821 before integrating generative output into Smart Filter stacks; never create a one-click merge-visible plus effects plus masked-copy operation without re-reading US 8655112. Classic user-directed healing remains allowed.
- **Spot Healing, Remove Object, and the Patch tool stay inside the classic-healing carve-out** (August 2026, Remove Object added September 2026, research record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). All blend with the healing membrane that expired US 6587592 itself taught (`core/heal_membrane.cpp`): destination-minus-source offsets from the region BOUNDARY interpolated harmonically across the interior by a fixed deterministic relaxation, then added to the source texture. That is cleared prior art, and it is not gradient-domain compositing: no source gradients are composited and no Poisson guidance field exists, which is what US 9058699 (to 2029) claims - never composite source gradients or build a guidance field. Sources stay user-directed or shape-derived: Spot Healing uses one coherent rigid mapping chosen from the stroke footprint's shape alone (`core/spot_heal.cpp`, fixed candidate order, geometry-only validity scores, pixel content never read); Remove Object (Edit menu, canvas context menu, Patch Enter, `layer.removeObject`) is the same mapping chosen from the SELECTION's shape, and its only additional input is the user's repeat count (`attempt`), which indexes the geometrically valid candidates in their fixed order - the count never derives from pixel content and no candidate is ever scored by content; the Patch tool's only source is the user's drag offset, previewed as a raw translated blit with the region built geometrically once on release. Their source choice is shape-derived or user-directed by design; content-driven source selection may be added to them only as the exhaustive exemplar search the bullet above clears (never PatchMatch-style propagation or perturbation of offsets, never reshuffling), and no live per-move classification or healing preview may be added while US 8050498 (live classify-and-display, to Nov 3, 2029) is active. The Remove Object dialog (September 2026) obeys this: a fill runs only on an explicit Reroll click, a slider release, or a settled slider/spin value (one run per discrete change, coalesced), never per slider move or pointer move, and the canvas shows the finished fill of the last run, not a live classification of input. The drag gizmo (translated outline plus unprocessed content ghost) is decades-old generic drag-and-drop prior art, and the right-click context menu is a plain QMenu of existing commands; a new on-canvas gizmo beyond those needs its own check.

## Brush behavior

- **Flow and Airbrush remain flat-stamp operations.** They may repeat the current 2D stamp at fixed spatial/time cadences and cap accumulated paint at Opacity. Do not add particles, a 3D spray cone, stylus-pose projection, velocity-dependent deposition, bristle or fluid simulation, wet/dirty paint, or bidirectional transfer without re-reading US 8854342 and the US 9600907/10489937 family.
- **Brush Texture is one static world-anchored grayscale mask.** Pressure, velocity, direction, rotation, and stylus pose may not select, compose, or weight texture channels while US 10902645 and US 8896579 are active.
- **Color Dynamics** only interpolates the two selected colors and applies direct HSV offsets. It may not sample the canvas, mix paint, or build a 3D RGB Bezier path (US 10924633).
- **Wet Edges** keeps one maximum-coverage union mask per stroke with no canvas-color feedback, paint store, fluid, pigment, paper, drying, or pickup simulation.
- **Dual Brush** remains one fixed secondary computed mask, never a component graph or hierarchy.
- **Mixer Brush** runs LIMITED continuous pickup, cleared by the 2026-08-14 claim re-review (record and element analysis in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). The pickup store is ONE running premultiplied-RGBA average fed only by per-dab footprint samples of the canvas. It must NEVER be seeded from or blended with the foreground/deposit color: the foreground enters only a transient per-dab linear interpolation whose result is never written back (the US 7768525 claim-14 rule; that patent is Microsoft's, binding until 2028-03-07, doctrine-of-equivalents caution). Still prohibited: per-pixel/per-bristle brush state or any spatial pickup texture, paint-amount/fill channels (US 8749572), erodible height-map tips or persisted worn state (US 10217253), non-linear curvature-parameterized color mixing (US 10924633 B1), region-sweep loading modes or multi-cell brush models (US 8654143), and fluid/drying/cleaning simulation beyond the calibrated Wet-0 linear dry-out. ABR input-driven texture depth imports as a static depth with a notice.
- **Pattern Stamp** performs a direct lookup into one static tile. It may not inspect the destination for illumination, gradients, shading, or reflections; adapt or synthesize the pattern from content; or assemble runtime texture channels driven by pressure, direction, velocity, or orientation (US 9864922 and US 10902645).

## Vector tracing

- **Trace Image to Shapes traces once, on explicit request, into static shape layers** (August 2026, claim record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md), design in [image-trace.md](image-trace.md)). The traced group has no link back to its source layer: never add a live tracing object that re-traces when the source pixels change, never re-trace automatically after the dialog closes, and never add overlay or outline renditions of the source in the preview or on the canvas before 2028-02-28 (Adobe US 7636097, whose claim 1 combines overlapping source/trace display with rendition selectors and automatic re-tracing on source modification). Parameters stay global for the whole layer: no per-region, brush-painted, or localized tracing parameters (Adobe US 9972073 / US 10521889, to 2036). No gesture-driven path extraction between two user-picked points on detected edges (Adobe US 11257256, to 2040). The fitter stays the per-run Schneider fit with independent runs: no coordinated global least-squares smoothness solve across adjacent segments, no tangent-vector construction with angle/curvature thresholds placing segmentation points (Bending Spoons US 10212457 family, to 2036). Centerline (stroke) tracing is clear to build (Adobe US 7876932 expired 2025-10-17) but is not implemented.

## Scanned-photo division

- **Divide Scanned Photos detects once, on explicit request, on a still image** (August 2026, research record in [patent-research.md](patent-research.md)). Detection runs only when the user invokes the command or changes a dialog parameter; regions come from fixed deterministic geometry (border-seeded flood fill, component labeling, minimum-area rectangles, optional quad fit) with global parameters and user-editable regions, and extraction is one fixed resample per region (axis crop, rotation, or homography with the expired Zhang aspect recovery). Never add live-camera or per-frame detection, automatic capture or quality-triggered frame selection, ML or content classification, per-region automatic parameter adaptation, or automatic selection between algorithms: those are the claim elements of the active mobile-capture families (Mitek US 9672510 to 2028; Kofax US 9760788 / US 9754163).

## Alignment guides and Align/Distribute

- **Alignment snapping snaps edges and centers only, never spacing** (September 2026, research record in [patent-research-alignment.md](patent-research-alignment.md), design in [alignment.md](alignment.md)). While a layer is dragged, Patchy may snap its edges and centers to other layers' opaque bounds, the selection, guides, the grid, and the canvas, and draw a line through the matched edge or center. It must never position a dragged layer equidistant between other layers or at a neighbouring pair's spacing, and must never show spacing or distance measurements during a drag (Adobe US 10782861, to 2036). Every edge and center of every moving layer is a snap source regardless of where the drag was grabbed (Adobe US 9250786, to 2034: grab-point-dependent source features). The snap search stays a plain linear comparison of edge coordinates against a tolerance, with no cursor guide line, reference-point distance index, or sorted candidate structure (Adobe US 9442623, to 2033). Targets are layer bounds and user-drawn geometry only, never objects detected inside a layer's pixels (Adobe US 11562488, to 2041). Static Align and Distribute commands are user-invoked one-shot layout with no reposition input and stay outside these claims; keep them out of the drag path.

## Format, copyright, trademarks, and assets

- PSD interoperability is based on observed output from a licensed Photoshop, never decompilation. Do not copy verbatim Adobe specification text into the repository; link to it. Use self-authored fixtures only and do not support the undocumented PSDC cloud-document format.
- "Smart Object(s)", "Smart Filter(s)", "Filter Gallery", and "PSD" are not Adobe trademarks in the reviewed record. PHOTOSHOP is registered and policed. Use referential language such as "compatible with Adobe Photoshop"; never use Adobe marks in Patchy's product name, icons, or branding, and retain README.md's Trademark Notice.
- Bundled art and textures must never come from AI image generation. Acceptable sources are code generation or real photography/human artwork under a commercially compatible license, preferably CC0. Every third-party asset needs a `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md` entry and must ship losslessly. Poly Haven textures are human-made.
- HEIC/HEIF decoding must use a platform or browser-provided HEVC decoder. Native builds
  keep their existing platform-codec paths. The browser build may statically link the
  vendored libheif container parser and its WebCodecs adapter, but every software HEVC
  codec backend and HEVC encoder must remain disabled. Never vendor or link libde265, x265, or
  another HEVC decoder/encoder, and do not add HEIC encoding. The WASM CMake option block,
  the runtime `VideoDecoder.isConfigSupported()` check, and the distribution obligations
  recorded in `NOTICE-THIRD-PARTY.md` are binding parts of this boundary. See
  [file-formats.md](file-formats.md) for the platform and browser design.
- JPEG XR decoding and encoding must use the in-box Windows Imaging Component codec. Never
  vendor or link jxrlib, libjxr, or another JPEG XR codec, and never add a JPEG XR path to a
  non-Windows build, without a new review. The boundary is cheap to hold and worth holding:
  Patchy ships no codec, so no JPEG XR patent reads on the binary at all. The underlying
  position is friendlier than HEVC's (Microsoft placed the JPEG XR patents under the Open
  Specification Promise and released the reference implementation under BSD-2-Clause, and
  the 2006-2009 priority dates are at or past term), but that reasoning has not been through
  a claim-level review, so it is not a licence to vendor a codec. See [jxr.md](jxr.md) for
  the design and [file-formats.md](file-formats.md) for the wiring.
- Shipping dependencies must permit commercial distribution. GPL tools may be used locally for development or fixture generation only when they are not linked into or distributed with Patchy.
- No Photoshop SDK code or Adobe-created assets may enter this repository or a Patchy binary. The only exception is the self-authored regression material in `test-fixtures/psd/`, which is not Adobe artwork.
