## Selection tools

- **Quick Select solves once on mouse release.** Do not add live per-mouse-move classification or a selection preview before November 3, 2029 (Adobe US 8050498). Enhance Edge remains geometric, the solve uses one window per stroke, and input never selects among algorithms automatically (US 10698588 claims toggling graph cut and level-set refinement from the Add/Remove mode plus where the click lands relative to the selection; never switch mechanisms on that pair). `ui_quick_select_stroke_selects_object_and_is_undoable` pins it.
- **Automatic selection (Select Subject, Object Selection, their script calls) is one network solve from one prompt, combined by plain boolean** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-selection.md), [design](ai-selection.md)). Object Selection sends either one box or one positive point per solve, on mouse release only: never a negative point (US 11314982, to 2037), never a box plus points or any two prompt modalities together (US 11568627, to 2037), never a previous mask back into the decoder (SAM `mask_input` stays zero; US 11676279, to 2041), and never a distance-map input. Add and Subtract are selection booleans, not network inputs. Nothing segmented is shown before release, and there is no hover highlight or precomputed per-object masks (US 8050498; Adobe's allowed hover-preview application US 2023/0129341). Select Subject runs one fixed salient model once over the whole image and upsamples once: no network-located crop, second pass, or refinement of sub-regions (US 11244195 / US 12591950, to 2040). Select Background and Remove Background reuse that one result through plain inversion or as a layer mask. No detectors, class labels, text queries, or automatic choice between models (US 11107219, US 11631234, US 11972569, US 12260557); the user picks the command, and Object Selection's Standard or High Quality model by setting and download, never by image content. Zoom-to-box encodes the user's box grown by fixed geometry (`sam_box_region`) once and decodes the box once: never a network-located region, a second pass, or a refinement of a mask. Any SAM-family model must keep SAM's decoder without encoder skip connections (US 12051225 claim 16; SAM 2 and HQ-SAM wait on a further review). No edge-refinement brush or learned matting stage on the result (US 10068361, US 11593948). A new model, prompt type or refinement needs a new review. A single-pass whole-image matting model is reviewed (record, 2026-10-12): allowed as a replacement or a user-chosen alternative, never merged with or fed by the general model; Adobe US 12051225 claim 16 (transformer encoder plus skip-connected decoder producing an alpha matte, to about 2042-09) is an open item against the BiRefNet architecture, so Select Subject, Select Background and Remove Background stay out of releases until it is resolved (`PATCHY_ENABLE_SELECT_SUBJECT` off; Object Selection ships).
- Magnetic Lasso and brush shape-dynamics patents expired, so live snap display is allowed. Magnetic Lasso still constructs its selection region once in `finish_magnetic_lasso()`, unlike Quick Select's prohibited live classification.

## Brush behavior

- **Flow and Airbrush remain flat-stamp operations.** They may repeat the current 2D stamp at fixed spatial/time cadences and cap accumulated paint at Opacity. Do not add particles, a 3D spray cone, stylus-pose projection, velocity-dependent deposition, bristle or fluid simulation, wet/dirty paint, or bidirectional transfer without re-reading US 8854342 and the US 9600907/10489937 family.
- **Brush Texture is one static world-anchored grayscale mask.** Pressure, velocity, direction, rotation, and stylus pose may not select, compose, or weight texture channels while US 10902645 and US 8896579 are active.
- **Color Dynamics** only interpolates the two selected colors and applies direct HSV offsets. It may not sample the canvas, mix paint, or build a 3D RGB Bezier path (US 10924633).
- **Wet Edges** keeps one maximum-coverage union mask per stroke with no canvas-color feedback, paint store, fluid, pigment, paper, drying, or pickup simulation.
- **Dual Brush** remains one fixed secondary computed mask, never a component graph or hierarchy.
- **Mixer Brush** runs LIMITED continuous pickup (claim re-review 2026-08-14, [record](patent-research.md)). The pickup store is ONE running premultiplied-RGBA average fed only by per-dab footprint samples of the canvas. It must NEVER be seeded from or blended with the foreground/deposit color: the foreground enters only a transient per-dab linear interpolation whose result is never written back (Microsoft US 7768525 claim 14, to 2028-03-07; re-check then). Still prohibited: per-pixel/per-bristle brush state or any spatial pickup texture, paint-amount/fill channels (US 8749572), erodible height-map tips or persisted worn state (US 10217253), non-linear curvature-parameterized color mixing (US 10924633 B1), region-sweep loading modes or multi-cell brush models (US 8654143), and fluid/drying/cleaning simulation beyond the calibrated Wet-0 linear dry-out. ABR input-driven texture depth imports as a static depth with a notice.
- **Pattern Stamp** performs a direct lookup into one static tile. It may not inspect the destination for illumination, gradients, shading, or reflections; adapt or synthesize the pattern from content; or assemble runtime texture channels driven by pressure, direction, velocity, or orientation (US 9864922 and US 10902645).

## Vector tracing

- **Trace Image to Shapes traces once, on explicit request, into static shape layers** (August 2026, [record](patent-research.md), [design](image-trace.md)). The traced group has no link back to its source layer: never add a live tracing object that re-traces when the source pixels change, never re-trace automatically after the dialog closes, and never add overlay or outline renditions of the source in the preview or on the canvas before 2028-02-28 (Adobe US 7636097). Parameters stay global for the whole layer: no per-region, brush-painted, or localized tracing parameters (Adobe US 9972073 / US 10521889, to 2036). No gesture-driven path extraction between two user-picked points on detected edges (Adobe US 11257256, to 2040). The fitter stays the per-run Schneider fit with independent runs: no coordinated global least-squares smoothness solve across adjacent segments, no tangent-vector construction with angle/curvature thresholds placing segmentation points (Bending Spoons US 10212457 family, to 2036).

## Scanned-photo division

- **Divide Scanned Photos detects once, on explicit request, on a still image** (August 2026, [record](patent-research.md)). Detection runs only when the user invokes the command or changes a dialog parameter; regions come from fixed deterministic geometry with global parameters and user-editable regions, and extraction is one fixed resample per region (axis crop, rotation, or homography with the expired Zhang aspect recovery). Never add live-camera or per-frame detection, automatic capture or quality-triggered frame selection, ML or content classification, per-region automatic parameter adaptation, or automatic selection between algorithms: those are the claim elements of the active mobile-capture families (Mitek US 9672510 to 2028; Kofax US 9760788 / US 9754163).

## Book scanning

- **Scan a Book to PDF finds pages once per explicit scan, with fixed geometry, and cuts them by rotation only** (November 2026, [record](patent-research-book-scanning.md), [design](book-scan.md)). A scan starts only from the user's Scan button (never motion, page-turn, lid or timer detection: PFU US 9258448, to 2034). Detection runs on that still image or on a dialog parameter change, with global per-book parameters and user-editable boxes; the "scanned-photo division" limits above apply to it too. Pages are cut by rotation resampling only: never line sets, vanishing points, per-page quadrangles or homographies (ABBYY US 10140691, to 2036), so the Divide Scanned Photos perspective mode is never reused here. No neural or learned page segmentation (Adobe US 12705755, to 2044). Tone changes stay global (levels, gray conversion, one B&W threshold): never local brightness changes near the binding (Canon US 8730535, to 2029) or adaptive thresholds. Reading direction is the user's setting and only orders pages: never detect the opening direction to turn one page of a spread against the other, and never put two pages on one PDF page (Brother US 8270041, to 2031). No page-curvature dewarping, 3D or camera-pose models, text-line curve fits or finger removal. The grayscale-capture hint is an offer the user accepts, never an automatic switch.

## Alignment guides and Align/Distribute

- **Alignment snapping snaps edges and centers only, never spacing** (September 2026, [record](patent-research-alignment.md), [design](alignment.md)). While a layer is dragged, Patchy may snap its edges and centers to other layers' opaque bounds, the selection, guides, the grid, and the canvas, and draw a line through the matched edge or center. It must never position a dragged layer equidistant between other layers or at a neighbouring pair's spacing, and must never show spacing or distance measurements during a drag (Adobe US 10782861, to 2036). Every edge and center of every moving layer is a snap source regardless of where the drag was grabbed (Adobe US 9250786, to 2034). A whole-shape drag (Path Select, Ctrl in a shape tool; GitHub issue 695, 2026-11-11) runs the same scan on the bounds of the moved shapes under the same rules: every edge and the center of each moved shape, never features chosen from the grab point. The snap search stays a plain linear comparison of edge coordinates against a tolerance, with no cursor guide line, reference-point distance index, or sorted candidate structure (Adobe US 9442623, to 2033). Targets are layer bounds and user-drawn geometry only, never objects detected inside a layer's pixels (Adobe US 11562488, to 2041). Keep the one-shot Align and Distribute commands out of the drag path.

## Artboards

- **No artboard is chosen from edit history** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-artboards.md)): a new artboard's size and place come from the user's explicit input or from the artboard whose add button was clicked, never from the most recently edited artboard (Adobe US 8667406, to 2031-02-04). Angular snapping, cross-artboard change propagation and content reflow need a new review.

## Adjustment layers

- **Black & White mixes only the user's six weights** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-adjustments.md)). Never derive the weights or the gray from image content, such as an Auto button (Adobe US 7706606, to 2029), and never drive the weights from one control along a path (Apple US 9092893, to 2032), without a new review.
- **Vibrance's gain stays a fixed function of each pixel's own values** (claim comparison of 2026-11-02, [record](patent-research-adjustments.md)). Until 2032-03-22 (Apple US 8638338; continuation US 9639965 to 2028-09-29), never a slider that gives colors outside a protected color region the plain saturation amount and colors inside it an amount scaled by distance from reference colors (skin tones or any other), and never a nearest-reference-color search, without a new review. Hue/Saturation's band ranges keep Photoshop's pre-2007 range-and-falloff form. GEGL's `gegl:vibrance` (one chroma offset for every pixel) stays inside this boundary.
- **Color Lookup applies only the table in the user's own file or a LUT file the user picks** (October 2026, [record](patent-research-adjustments.md)). Patchy reads the LUT text a `clrL` block keeps (`.cube`, `.3dl`, `.look`) and its embedded ICC device-link or abstract profile, and applies the table unmodified by fixed interpolation. A LUT file the user picks, in those formats only, is embedded in the user's document as Photoshop embeds it. Never ship, copy, recreate, fetch, or list by name the looks Photoshop or any third party bundles. An unedited `clrL` block is written back byte for byte. Never add, without a new review: LUT generation from image content, learned looks, look matching between images, table editing, or LUT export.

## Soft proof and gamut warning

- **Proof Colors and Gamut Warning stay the generic ICC view** (October 2026, record linked above, [design](cmyk.md)). One fixed transform through the user's chosen CMYK profile and rendering intent, and one flat fill for out-of-gamut colors, both from Little CMS, applied to the whole canvas view on the user's menu choice. Never add, without a new review: a view correction for a measured monitor, paper white or viewing condition beyond the ICC intents; a proof chosen or adjusted per region or from image content; automatic editing of pixels into gamut; learned gamut mapping.

## Prompt-driven image generation

- **OpenAI Image Tools results are plain pixel layers from the user's own prompt** (November 2026, [record](patent-research-generative.md), [design](openai-images.md)). Create sends the user's prompt and optional reference images with no mask and no selection input. Edit and Remove (re-checked 2026-11-06) send the visible image around the user's selection and `background: opaque`; Remove adds a mask from that selection only and a fixed instruction, and no reference images; Edit adds a markup overlay that only outlines that selection, the user's prompt with fixed instructions appended, and the user's reference images if any (Seth's decision, 2026-11-06, US 12573004; never a mask with references, never a mask and markup together); the result layer's mask is the selection captured at the click, grown and feathered only by the user's Edge blend amount. Never: detect an object in a result to make a mask (US 12462456); describe the document's elements to the service (US 12530821); generate or complete a prompt from image content or metadata (US 12579606); offer Expand sizes from a list of the model's output aspect ratios (US 12626423); crop and extend in one Expand, or rotate the image against the new canvas (US 2025/0124626); pick a command or mode from the mask's shape (US 12608858); keep a generative layer that stores its prompt, mask, seeds or variants for regeneration. Open decision for a later change, with the claim elements involved in the record (Edit and Remove as built take the cautious side): a transparent background in a selection-masked request (US 12462456). Decided (Seth, 2026-11-06): Edit's markup request may carry reference images (US 12573004 claims 1 and 11; the encoders and model run on OpenAI's servers); a mask still never goes with references. Expand (built 2026-11-06) takes the new canvas only from the user's per-side pixel fields, only grows it, sends the frame with no mask, and adds a layer over the original that is opaque to a depth set by the user's Blend amount and then fades to clear over a fixed width (a feather, with the same per-channel mean tone offset as Edit); the original layer stays untouched underneath. Animate (built 2026-11-06) sends a prompt, optionally one image and never a mask, and opens the returned GIF's frames as plain layers (Full color, 2026-11-07: the frames as one PNG sheet cut into equal panels; see the record); it never runs generation over the frames of an existing animation (US 12573124). Describe (built 2026-11-06) shows a text model's description of the current image as text only: never copied into a prompt or sent with an image request (US 12579606), never a comparison of versions or the edit history (US 12579831). Seams (2026-11-06): only a mask dilation and feather set by the user's Edge blend amount and a per-channel mean tone offset; no learned border refiner (US 2026/0253186) and no gradient-domain blending without a new review. Animate's fixed sprite-sheet wrapper is constant text, never generated by a language model (AU 2023206192). Variations stay in the dialog, never in the document. Changing any tab needs a design re-check against the record.

## Shape drag points

- **A live Polygon and a live Line show only the two points of their drawing drag** (GitHub issue 690, decided 2026-11-10, [record](patent-research-shape-handles.md), [design](vector-tools-shape-handles.md#drag-points-on-a-live-polygon-and-line)). A polygon's center and first vertex and a line's two ends, drawn as two discs: the forms AutoCAD's grips (1992), the expired US 5517578 (1993) and Inkscape 0.38 (2004) published. The vertex sets radius and angle as the drawing drag does; the center only moves the shape. Never add, without a new check: a point or handle that edits a corner radius (Adobe US 10249067, to 2036-02-23: per-point radii that adjust their neighbours), a side count or a star inset; a line, stem, arc, band or readout drawn with the points; snapping of the center point to guides, the grid or other layers (Adobe US 9250786, to 2034-03-17, [alignment record](patent-research-alignment.md)); points on Rectangle, Ellipse or Custom Shape, which keep the eight-handle box. Watch item: the Google Patents class queries the scan could not finish; repeat them before the points gain any behaviour.

## Crop interaction

- **Move Image Under Crop Box is built within these limits** (GitHub issue 699, claim comparison and decision of 2026-11-11, [record](patent-research-crop.md), [design](crop-tool.md#move-image-under-crop-box)). The mode is off by default; with it on, the crop box keeps its place on screen and the view pans and turns behind it. Apple's 2011 family (six US patents and NL 2009172, all in force) describes a crop box that keeps its place on screen while the image pans, zooms and turns behind it. In either mode the Crop tool keeps these limits: a rotation of the box, the view or the image never changes the zoom or the box size, and nothing tests whether a canvas edge shows inside the box (US 9158455, to 2033-09-21; US 9767535, to 2031-07-12; BlackBerry US 9201566, to 2033); no zoom follows a box change by itself, during the drag or after it, and a re-centering only pans (US 10546366, to 2031-07-12; ByteDance US 12008684, to 2041); one overlay, the user's choice, for every gesture, never a finer grid for turning than for moving (US 10762601, to 2031-07-12); the outside of the box is drawn the same way during a gesture and after it (Brother US 11699416, to 2035-10-12); no snapping to preset ratios (Google US 10101891, to 2036), no horizon detection and no rotation dial (Apple US 9569078 family, to 2033). The mode itself is what claims 14 and 15 of US 11532072 and claims 4 to 7 of US 11120525 (both to 2031-07-12) and NL 2009172 (to 2032-07-11) add to a ratio list and a translucent shield, both of which Patchy already has. No design limit separates the mode from those claims; what differs is that Adobe's Lightroom 1.0 guide described it in February 2007, four years before the family's priority date. Decided 2026-11-11: the mode is built, and for those three it rests on the earlier publications E1 to E4 of the record, the same kind of decision as the composite-preview one of 2026-10-20 ("Interactive previews" above). `ui_crop_move_image_keeps_the_recorded_limits` fails when the zoom, box-size, one-overlay, same-outside or no-snapping limit is broken, and `ui_crop_move_image_recenters_by_pan_after_handle_drag` when the re-centering does more than pan. Re-check 2031-07-12, 2032-07-11 and 2033-09-21.
- **The Crop Straighten line is built within these limits** (GitHub issue 702, compared and decided 2026-11-11, [record](patent-research-crop.md#straighten-by-a-drawn-line-github-702), [design](crop-tool-straighten.md)). Sony JP 4640470 B2 (Japan; in force per its register, read 2026-11-11, to 2028-08-18) claims a line derived from the user's drag and the displayed image's tilt corrected from it against a reference line; the gesture has every element, and so does the Ruler's Straighten Image as it ships. What differs is that Lightroom 1.0's guide (February 2007) and Photoshop's measure line with Rotate Canvas > Arbitrary (since 5.5) described it before the 2008-08-18 filing. Decided 2026-11-11: built, resting for that patent on those publications (E5, E6). It keeps the limits above and turns the box once, on release, never while the line is drawn. Re-check 2026-12-10 (fee) and 2028-08-18.
