# Layer-effect render calibration vs Photoshop

Calibration record for rendering layer effects (Blend If, Satin, Stroke, shadows and glows, interior effects, bevel details beyond the write rules). Read this before touching src/render/layer_compositor.hpp effect passes or layer_style_mask_ops.cpp. Descriptor/write rules stay in [ps-compat.md](ps-compat.md).
Conventions: "PS" = Adobe Photoshop 2026/27.8, the installed ground truth; every rule is pinned by PS COM captures unless noted. Fixtures named `photoshop-*` live in `test-fixtures/psd/`; `local-test-fixtures/` is machine-local. The COM workflow lives in [ps-compat.md](ps-compat.md).

## Per-effect calibration notes

- **Layer Style dialog**: footer `Preview` is transient and always opens enabled (off restores the original canvas while edits accumulate; Accept returns the edits regardless). `Show Effects` is the persisted `masterFXSwitch` (`LayerStyle::effects_visible`).
- **Blend If rendering**: the four active Gray/R/G/B gates multiply, and This Layer multiplies Underlying Layer. A split black range `[a,b]` retains byte v as `(v-a+1)/(b-a+1)` for `a <= v < b`; joined `a=b` is a hard cutoff; the white side mirrors the inclusive feather, zero immediately above `white_high`. Composite Gray = integer weights `299R + 590G + 111B`, rounded at 1/1000. Underlying coverage = `(1-da) + da*gate` (transparency always passes). Effects do not inherit the base-pixel gate; adjustments evaluate This Layer on the adjusted output and Underlying on the pre-adjustment backdrop; a nondefault folder range evaluates its isolated group against the outside backdrop; Blend If on a clipping base does not shrink clipping coverage. Fixtures `photoshop-blend-if-4b-roundtrip.psd`/`-render.bmp` (within 2/255).
- **Satin rendering**: from the post-mask alpha matte A: offset `v = (round(max(1,d) cos a), round(-max(1,d) sin a))`, signed difference `A(x+v) - A(x-v)`, Size blur, absolute value. Size 0 skips blur; Size n uses the exact separable tent kernel `(N-|k|)/N^2`, `N = max(2, n)`. Invert applies `1-q` after the Linear contour. The Satin color/mode folds into the layer's straight RGB before compositing, so alpha, masks, opacity, and backdrop apply once.
- **Stroke layer effect**: an exact-Euclidean **distance band** from the binary contour ({alpha > 0}), not a dilation; coverage formula in `stroke_alpha_mask` (src/render/layer_compositor.hpp), contour 0.5 px past the last pixel center, 1 px AA ramp, sum combine (not max) so the band stays seamless over AA contour pixels. Bands: Outside = size out, Inside = size in, **Center = size/2 each way**. Runs match PS run-for-run at every position, sizes 5-20 (`photoshop-stroke-positions.psd`). EDT: `exact_squared_distance_transform` (Felzenszwalb-Huttenlocher, deterministic). **Layer mask**: by default the mask reshapes the contour like pixel transparency where fully black (shape = {alpha > 0 AND mask > 0}) and the band lands on mask-hidden ground at FULL strength (`photoshop-stroke-masked.psd`); with 'lmgm' the contour ignores the mask and the OUTPUT is hidden instead (via Action Manager, 'lmgm' rides INSIDE the Lefx descriptor as `layerMaskAsGlobalMask`; a bare `setd` on the layer wipes the effects). Fractional mask values deliberately do NOT fold into shape or coverage: PS composites them with its content-knockout model, Patchy keeps raw-pixel coverage. Same family, known limitation: on uniform semi-transparent fills PS knocks content out under Inside/Center strokes; Patchy over-composites (tinted wash); exact knockout needs a per-layer compositing buffer. Do not "fix" by binarizing the inside field at {alpha < 1}: that breaks the PS-exact AA-edge band positions. Opaque content matches PS essentially exactly. **Overprint band knockout** (distinct from the fill-alpha limitation, which stays divergent): with FrFX `overprint` OFF (PS default) the band knocks the layer's own content out at FULL band coverage regardless of stroke opacity, and the stroke blends against the layers below with its own mode; interior effects vanish under the band; clipped members are knocked out with their base; an opaque Multiply stroke multiplies the BACKDROP; at reduced opacity the band is `lerp(backdrop, blend(stroke, backdrop), opacity)`; layer opacity scales the whole plane. Overprint ON blends the stroke over the layer's own content. Renderer: per-pixel base-content factor (`StrokeKnockoutPlane`/`stroke_knockout_factor`, layer_compositor.hpp) applied by the base pass, interior effects, and satin/vector-restamp paths; Normal strokes use the compensated divisor `(1-C)/(1-s)` (exact; no-op for opaque solid strokes), non-Normal plain `(1-C)`. The knockout applies at **0% stroke opacity** too: gate only the draw on opacity, never the knockout. Fixture `photoshop-stroke-overprint.psd/bmp`.
- **Drop shadow and outer glow share ONE soft-effect pipeline** (`prepare_photoshop_soft_effect_mask`, layer_style_mask_ops.cpp): (1) Spread expands the matte by the INTEGER radius `lround(spread% * size)` as a GRAYSCALE dilation (alpha x area-sampled disc; radii above 8 fall back to the binarizing chamfer band, which overshoots AA text ~20%); (2) the remaining size blurs with Satin's tent kernel, `N = max(2, lround(size)) - spread_radius` (NOT a triple-box). Spread 100 or size 0 = hard band with 1 px AA ramp; sizes 1-2 still blur with N=2. Never reimplement spread as a post-blur gain: saturating a blur's tail exposes the kernel support as per-glyph boxes.
- **Effect planes composite with burn-mode alpha FOLDED into the color**: LinearBurn and ColorBurn scale the effect color toward WHITE by its alpha and blend at full coverage (a 50% black linearBurn shadow subtracts an absolute `255 * mask * opacity`; the plain `lerp(b, blend(b, color), alpha)` model is refuted). **ColorDodge folds toward BLACK** (`color * alpha`). Normal, Multiply, Darken keep the plain model; source-affine modes are identical either way; other non-affine light modes are unprobed on the plain model. `composite_effect_color` (layer_compositor.hpp) folds every effect draw; layer PIXEL compositing keeps the standard model.
- **"Layer Knocks Out Drop Shadow" (DrSh `layerConceals`, default ON)**: the layer's transparency shape multiplies its own shadow by `(1 - matte)`, regardless of fill opacity (fill-0 shows no shadow inside), master opacity, or a Stroke Overprint knockout; OFF shows the shadow through proportionally. Invisible under opaque content. `LayerDropShadow::layer_conceals`; the pinned 12-item DrSh shape already carried the key. Fixture `photoshop-shadow-conceals.psd/bmp`.
- **Interior effect stacking order**: bottom-to-top overlays (pattern, gradient, color), then satin, then inner glow, then inner shadow, so interior effects blend against the OVERLAY-APPLIED content. **Interior overlays fold into the layer's straight RGB inside the base pass** (`prepare_interior_overlays`/`fold_interior_overlays`, layer_compositor.hpp), like Satin, so alpha, mask, stroke knockout, Fill/layer Opacity, and backdrop apply once; a 100% Normal overlay therefore hides the layer's pixels outright (a per-overlay destination pass leaks `1 - opacity` of the layer color and over-composites semi-transparent interiors), and Satin sits ABOVE the overlays. Three cases keep the legacy destination passes (the fold cannot express them) and still show the layer-opacity leak: Blend If (PS does not gate effects with it), Fill Opacity (scales pixels but not effects), and the shape-layer fill/stroke split (folding would tint the stroke); closing them needs the per-layer compositing buffer.
- **Interior soft-mask falloff** (Precise-only; the earlier choke-only calibration kept a historical box blur at choke 0): `prepare_photoshop_interior_soft_mask` (layer_style_mask_ops.cpp) is the exact interior mirror of the exterior pipeline: the INVERSE matte dilates by `lround(choke% * size)` (`dilate_layer_style_mask_by_radius`: exact disc through radius 8, chamfer above), then tent-blurs with `N = max(2, lround(size)) - choke_radius` (choke 100 or size 0 = hard band). Byte-matched, thin-bar overlaps and dot corners included. Unpinned: an exactly-half shadow offset (cos60 x 5 = 2.5) rounds DOWN in PS where lround rounds up; fixtures use angle 90.
- **Inner glow Technique/Range** (`photoshop-inner-glow{,-range}.psd/bmp`, `photoshop-inner-shadow.psd/bmp`): Edge source = interior falloff, then the Range gain `min(1, blur * 100/Inpr)` applied AFTER the blur. **Center source = the exact complement of the gained Edge field**, `1 - min(1, edge * gain)`. A descriptor omitting `Inpr` renders at Range 100 in BOTH glows (never fall back to 50; legacy Patchy files always wrote an explicit 50). The regenerated IrGl is PS's 12-item shape with `GlwT` between `Opct` and `Ckmt` plus stored `Inpr` (`LayerInnerGlow::technique/range`). Technique "Precise" keeps the historical triple-box falloff verbatim (approximate; Range not applied). Residuals: choke radii above 8 chamfer; two-edge overlaps differ ~1/255 (PS byte-rounds between tent passes).
- **Outer glow "Softer"** (`photoshop-outer-glow{,-range}.psd/bmp`): spread dilation, tent blur `N = max(2, lround(size)) - spread_radius`, then the Range gain `min(1, blur * 100/Inpr)`. **Range's UI default is 50, so real files render TWICE the raw blur**; an Action-Manager-authored glow omitting `Inpr` gets 100, which is why naive probes miss it. `prepare_outer_glow_softer_mask`; `GlwT`/`Inpr` parsed, modeled (`LayerOuterGlow::technique/range`), and written (PS round-trips them). "Precise" keeps the legacy distance-ramp render (approximate; Range not applied). Residuals: spread radii above 8 chamfer; spread-100 corner arcs differ ~1 px.
