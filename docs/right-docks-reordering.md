# Panel reordering and tab groups

Split from [right-docks.md](right-docks.md): dragging sections and tabs within the right panel column, the drag ghost, drop targets and indicators, and how tab groups form, merge and dissolve.

## Reordering

Dragging a title row, or a group's tab bar, up or down moves that section. `PanelColumn` filters the title rows and the tab bars (a press on the label reaches the row; the collapse button keeps its press, and a press on a tab still picks it). Past `startDragDistance` the drag starts: a closed-hand override cursor, a ghost, a drop indicator, and an application event filter so Escape or any other press cancels. Nothing floats, nothing leaves the window, `QDrag` is not used, and `QTabBar::setMovable` stays off: the column owns the gesture (`ui_panel_title_drag_reorders_sections`, `ui_panel_tab_drag_reorders_tabs`, `ui_panel_drag_onto_header_merges_into_tabs`).
- **What moves.** A drag from a tab carries that tab, whichever way it goes (so the first tab can be pulled out below its own group); a drag from a title row, or from the tab bar beside the tabs (the bar spans the section for this), carries the section. Offscreen tests press clear of the window's right edge: the custom window chrome takes a press there as a resize. A press still picks the tab first, so a dragged tab is in front.
- **Ghost.** `panelDragGhost`, a child of the window (never a window, transparent to the mouse), paints what the drag carries at half opacity under the pointer, held where it was pressed, so the drop line, the outline and the panels show through it. Its own style sheet makes its background transparent: the theme gives every plain `QWidget` an opaque `@window_bg`, which would paint under the copy (`ui_panel_drag_onto_header_merges_into_tabs` checks the alpha). It carries the tab, or the bar the section was picked up by (its title row, or the tabs of its tab bar), grabbed when the drag starts.
- **Drop targets** (`drop_at`). Beside the column (over the canvas, say) nothing shows and a release changes nothing: the panel stays where it was. Above or below the column, in line with it, a drop lands at its top or bottom, the only way above a group whose tab bar starts at the very top (its bar covers the edge band). A tab over its own bar, or within 6 px above or below it, only changes places. Otherwise a section's header (its tab bar and title row, `collapsed_height`) takes the panel in, except a band at its top and bottom edges (a quarter of the header, 3 to 8 px), which drops between sections; below an expanded section's header a drop lands above or below its middle. A drop onto its own header, or a section dropped where it is, does nothing and shows nothing.
- **Indicators.** Between sections: the Layers list's insertion line (`panelDropIndicator`, `@drop_indicator_bg`) on the divider. Onto a header: `panelMergeIndicator` outlines that header, plus an upright insertion line at the tab edge when the pointer is over its tab bar (before the first shown tab whose middle lies past the pointer, mirrored right to left).
- **Moves.** A section dropped between sections moves with `move_section_index` through `QSplitter::insertWidget`, keeping its height, and emits `section_order_changed`. A tab dropped along its own bar moves with `move_tab_index`, which moves the `pages` entry, the `QStackedWidget` page and the `QTabBar` tab together, so tab, stack and page indexes stay equal everywhere the code assumes it; it keeps the front page and the focus. A drop beside the tab's own place, or with only closed tabs between, changes nothing. The tab bar still gets the release, so it clears its pressed tab.
- **Groups.** A panel dropped on another section's header joins it as a tab (`merge_panel`, at the tab slot or after the last tab) and comes to the front; a section dropped there brings all its tabs, its front one in front (`merge_section`). The section joined keeps its collapse state. A tab dropped between sections becomes a section of its own there, with the group's collapse state (`separate_panel`). A group left with one panel becomes a plain section again (`dissolve_group`: its page takes the group's splitter slot through `QSplitter::replaceWidget`, the container is deleted later); a single section that gains a tab turns into a group in place (`make_group`). A page leaving its own section drops the min and max heights that section had (a collapsed one is pinned to its title row). Regrouping reparents pages and can delete the dragged tab bar, so a drop queues it (`Qt::QueuedConnection`) rather than doing it inside the dragged widget's event. `finish_regroup` refreshes the fill section, every section's state and the sizes, then emits `tab_groups_changed` and `section_order_changed`; the window refits its floors on it.
- **Names.** Sections are named by their first panel's id (`section_order`, `set_section_order`, `move_section`; any member's id names its section), so a group is named by its first tab. `tab_groups`, `set_tab_groups`, `set_tab_order`, `move_tab`, `merge_panel`, `merge_section` and `separate_panel` do the same as the drags without a mouse. The header menu has no grouping commands yet.
