# Release History

Older Patchy release notes are collected here. The most recent release
remains in [README.md](README.md#whats-new).

## 1.34 - November 13, 2026

- Select: Select > Color Range selects by sampled colors (click the image; Shift adds, Alt subtracts), a hue band, or the highlights, midtones or shadows, with a Fuzziness soft edge, Invert and a mask preview ([issue 709](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/709))
- Shapes: Shape Appearance is a window that stays open while you work; it follows the selected shape or fill layer and applies each change at once as one undo step, and its Show handles box puts the handles of a gradient fill or a gradient stroke on the canvas with any tool ([issue 694](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/694)); a new Solid Color, Gradient or Pattern fill layer spans the canvas when a shape layer is selected ([issue 710](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/710))
- Brushes: Left and Right turn a brush tip that has a direction (a sampled tip, the Square, or a flattened Round brush) by a degree, Shift by 15, and the pointer outline turns with it; Rotate Brush Left and Rotate Brush Right can take other keys in Preferences > Hotkeys ([issue 708](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/708))
- Layers panel: the filter's Text entry finds layers made with the Type tool and text layers from PSD files, fill layers list with adjustments under Fill or Adjustment, and Linked and Has Smart Filters join the properties ([issue 671](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/671)); in a narrow panel, Opacity and Fill drop their labels before the blend mode row wraps ([issue 693](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/693))
- Also: a tool group's letter selects the tool the group last used ([issue 711](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/711)); Help > About Patchy shows an Update button when its check finds a newer version; the PSD compatibility report quotes layer names and no longer lists 16-bit layers as uneditable ([issue 712](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/712))

## 1.33 - November 12, 2026

- Crop: one Preset list holds the ratios, sizes and saved presets, with a button that swaps width and height ([issue 696](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/696)), ([issue 697](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/697)); a Straighten button turns the crop box level or upright along a line you draw ([issue 702](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/702)); the new Move Image Under Crop Box option keeps the box in place while a drag slides or turns the image behind it ([issue 699](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/699)); while a crop box is pending, O steps through the overlays, Shift+O turns the overlay, H shows or hides the cropped area and P switches Move Image Under Crop Box ([issue 698](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/698)); the Background layer stays a Background after a crop or Canvas Size unless its kept pixels reach beyond the canvas ([issue 701](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/701))
- Text: while a text box is being edited its edges resize from anywhere along each side, and a drag just outside a corner turns the box ([issue 684](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/684)); the edit box of point text scales with the zoom ([issue 707](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/707)); Update All Text Layers keeps each layer's baseline, anchor, kerning, anti-aliasing and text box from its file ([issue 686](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/686)); Arabic runs are shaped with their Photoshop language, right-to-left paragraphs keep their indents on the same side after a box resize, and a bottom-edge drag keeps the first line where it was ([issue 683](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/683)); Resolve Missing Fonts remembers a pick that equals the suggestion and lets earlier choices be removed ([issue 692](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/692)); symbol fonts show a readable name and sample in the font list, and Show font names in English no longer lists a font twice on Linux ([issue 691](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/691))
- Shapes: a shape dragged with Path Select, or with Ctrl held in a shape tool, snaps to guides, the grid, the canvas and other layers as a Move drag does ([issue 695](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/695)); Density under 100% on a shape with a stroke spreads the stroke's paint as Photoshop does; a fill and an Inside or Outside stroke meet without a seam; shapes saved by Photoshop keep their Feather and Density
- Layers and Properties: Blend Mode, Opacity and Fill stay on one line in a narrow Layers panel, and the Properties panel shows the size and position of pixel, text and smart object layers and folders in the ruler unit, where a typed value resizes or moves the layer ([issue 693](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/693)); with Auto-Select Group on, a selected layer inside a group moves on its own ([issue 703](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/703))
- Zoom: from 200% up, every zoom draws exact pixel blocks, fractional zooms and a rotated view included ([issue 706](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/706))
- Linux: on Wayland, drags that redraw as they go keep up with the pointer, among them moving a polygon, Path Select, gradient fill handles, text box resizes, the marquee, pan, rotate and scrubby zoom ([issue 678](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/678)), ([issue 700](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/700)), ([issue 705](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/705)); a progress dialog that names a file widens to show its whole title ([issue 704](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/704))
- Also: the Levels and Curves clipping view (Alt+drag) follows the handle on large images ([issue 687](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/687)); in Preferences > Hotkeys, a search with Show set to Commands with shortcuts matches only the keys ([issue 676](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/676)); Copy CSS takes z-index from the layer's place in the whole document ([issue 688](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/688)); open menus close when the window is moved or resized by its frame; the web build keeps the commands it cannot run in its menus and offers the desktop download when one is picked

## 1.32 - November 11, 2026

- Shapes: the shape tools show the active shape's box with eight handles, so it resizes without leaving the tool (Shift keeps the proportions, Alt resizes from the center); a polygon or star shows its center and one vertex instead, and a line shows its two ends ([issue 690](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/690)); a shape moved with Path Select or a Ctrl+drag of a shape tool stays live, so a moved polygon keeps its Polygon section in Shape Appearance ([issue 643](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/643)); a new line's stroke is centered, so its width makes the line thicker, and arrowheads can be up to 1000 times the weight; an arrowhead scrubbed in Shape Appearance no longer flips back to its old size; Inside and Outside strokes on open paths no longer throw spikes; the Path Select and Direct Select bars group their controls with separators ([issue 689](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/689))
- Copy CSS: Layer > Export > Copy CSS puts the selected layers on the clipboard as CSS classes with position, size and stacking order (z-index), opacity, blend mode, a shape's fill, border and corner radius, a text layer's font properties, and drop shadows ([issue 688](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/688))
- Levels: hold Alt and drag the black or white input slider to see shadow or highlight clipping on the canvas ([issue 687](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/687))
- Text: the Type menu adds Update All Text Layers, Replace All Missing Fonts and Paste Lorem Ipsum ([issue 686](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/686)); Resolve Missing Fonts remembers the replacement chosen for each font and lists earlier choices and suggestions at the top of the font list ([issue 692](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/692)), and names a missing font's family and style apart ([issue 669](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/669)); Preferences can show fonts named in another script under their English names ([issue 691](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/691)); right-to-left and Arabic text justifies over its word spaces, long right-to-left paragraphs re-render quickly, and PSD saves keep Hyphenate and Photoshop's Middle Eastern settings ([issue 683](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/683)); PSD saves keep a paragraph's Adobe Every-line Composer through a text update; on the Type bar, Justify sits beside Vertical and RTL, which are now icon toggles ([issue 673](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/673))
- PDF: importing several pages shows one progress dialog for all of them, and the Import PDF dialog shows the canvas size and memory a choice needs and warns before a very large one; Ctrl+W closes the PDF viewer on Windows
- Dynamic Vector Preview: its cost follows what is in view instead of the zoom level; a photo stays smooth between 100% and 400% in a document that holds a shape; it draws a shape's Feather and Density
- Also: the Airbrush keeps up when zoomed out and at Soft 100% ([issue 677](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/677)); Customize Layers Panel can hide the Fill field so the blend mode and Opacity fit one line in a narrow panel ([issue 680](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/680)); the GIMP-style shortcut set binds GIMP's size and opacity keys ([issue 645](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/645)); a file just opened is the first Recent Files row on the home screen; opening a PSD no longer adds a note about its editable smart object layers ([issue 669](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/669)); scripts can list, add, hide, reorder, edit and remove Smart Filters, get the CSS for layers, and place the view on a document point or rectangle

## 1.31 - November 10, 2026

- PDF viewer: on desktop a PDF opens in a fast viewer with continuous scrolling or page turns, one page or two side by side (with the cover alone for comics and books), right-to-left reading for manga, fit page, fit width or any zoom, rotation, full screen and page thumbnails. It reopens at the page you last read, Edit... opens pages for editing with the Import PDF options, and Preferences can skip the viewer
- Print as Book: prints a PDF as a saddle-stitched (fold-and-staple) booklet, a sewn book in signatures, cut-and-stack leaves for glue or comb binding, or eight-page mini zines, with a sheet-by-sheet preview, step-by-step assembly directions, creep compensation, optional page numbers, fold and cut marks, and two-sided printing by the printer or by hand. It splits PDFs that already hold two pages per page, such as a scanned booklet, and can save the imposed sheets as a PDF
- Scan a Book to PDF: page clicks respond at once; any earlier page or the cover can be adjusted or rescanned from its thumbnail; covers can be scanned one at a time for a book too large to lie open on the glass; some pages can be scanned in color or at another resolution and given their own output; pages reorder by drag and drop; the scanner list follows a scanner being switched on or off. A step bar and a setup page with a picture guide the cover scan, the top of the book can be set without a cover scan, and Tilt reaches 90 degrees
- Text: a box or area text frame being edited has side handles that move one edge ([issue 684](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/684)); each text run keeps its Photoshop language through PSD edits and saves ([issue 682](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/682)); Arabic text in PSDs keeps Photoshop's alignment, direction and leading ([issue 683](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/683)); a missing symbol font such as Wingdings gets only a font with the same symbols, never a text font ([issue 463](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/463))
- Layers panel: Customize Layers Panel adds compact rows without the blend mode and opacity line, row tooltips on or off, color labels as a strip or a tint over the whole row, and the thumbnail size and display ([issue 680](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/680))
- Also: picking a tool or letting go of Alt starts a new Shift+click line ([issue 662](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/662)); the blank space beside the menu overflow button drags the window again ([issue 685](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/685)); Preferences > Hotkeys puts Reset All in the Shortcut set section ([issue 676](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/676)); Shape Appearance's Fill fields line up with Stroke's ([issue 679](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/679)); in tiling mode the document outline is inverted so it shows on any texture; the Properties panel shows group and artboard bounds; on Windows, Patchy starts faster and no longer stalls for seconds when another app is holding the clipboard open

## 1.30 - November 9, 2026

- Scan a Book to PDF (experimental): File > Import > Scan a Book to PDF scans a bound book one spread per key press, finds each page, and writes a PDF whose pages all share one size, covers included. It keeps the book in a project folder you can continue later, sets pages per scan while scanning, lets you adjust the labeled spine lines, and exports in color, grayscale or black and white. So far it has only been tested by hand on Windows, so treat it as experimental, especially on macOS and Linux
- Brushes: Smudge has its own Strength setting (default 50%) instead of using the brush Opacity ([issue 675](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/675)); big Airbrush, Flow and soft brush strokes keep up with the cursor ([issue 677](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/677)), and so do 700 px and larger Dodge, Burn, Sponge and Smudge strokes ([issue 660](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/660)); a 1 px brush with Flow under 100 paints a continuous line; Blur, Smudge, Sharpen and the healing tools handle edges against transparency, and Blur softens a layer's edge; Alt+Shift shows the Shift+click line preview on Dodge, Burn, Blur and Sharpen ([issue 662](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/662))
- Text: the Type options bar has Bold, Italic, Underline, a Justify toggle and a right-to-left toggle, and anti-aliasing moves to the Character panel ([issue 673](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/673)); Paragraph adds Justify Last Centered, Justify Last Right and Justify All, read and saved in PSDs ([issue 672](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/672)); the new Type Mask tool turns typed letters into a selection ([issue 670](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/670)); a missing font shows its family and its style in separate fields, and the PSD compatibility report no longer lists preserved image resources or embedded smart object sources ([issue 669](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/669))
- Layers panel: the filter menu finds layers by type, properties, row color or Selected Only, combined with the name text ([issue 671](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/671))
- Shapes: dragging a whole shape with Path Select keeps up ([issue 678](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/678)); polygons reopened from a PSD stay live, and the Shape picker shows the shape that was stamped ([issue 643](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/643), [issue 644](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/644), [issue 622](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/622))
- Hotkeys: Preferences > Hotkeys has a Show list for all, menu, tool, other or assigned commands ([issue 676](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/676)); the GIMP-style and Krita-style sets put tool modifiers on Ctrl as their manuals do ([issue 302](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/302)), and in the GIMP-style set N, K and A pick the Pencil, Ink and Airbrush brush presets ([issue 645](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/645))
- Also: on Windows 11 the window keeps its minimize and maximize animations and gets a theme-colored border, and a narrow menu bar folds into an overflow menu before reaching the window buttons ([issue 674](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/674)); Customize Tool Palette can hide the scroll-wheel expand button and the scroll arrows ([issue 668](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/668)); filter dialogs put Preview beside Reset and remember it ([issue 667](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/667)); Export for Web encodes GIF and PNG previews of 3 megapixels or more once their row is selected, with a faster color quantizer ([issue 571](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/571)); the save prompt offers No to All when several unsaved documents close; commands that need a layer say so instead of doing nothing

- Also: holding an arrow key keeps nudging the layer, with one undo step per hold ([issue 659](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/659)); the multi-page TIFF question has a Don't ask again box and a Preferences setting ([issue 664](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/664)); Layer > Arrange has one Link/Unlink Layers toggle ([issue 656](https://github.com/SethRobinson/Patchy/issues/656)); View > Extras (Ctrl+H) also hides the Free Transform box and handles; number fields in Print, Levels, Export for Web, Clone Source, Perspective Planes and the crop ratio no longer rewrite themselves while you type, and the Print dialog's Units sit in the Paper group

Hey folks!  This release has a ton of bugfixes, tweaks and QOL stuff.   I've spent some time in designing a system and control panel that helps me do more quicker.   It's an extremely fun and interesting challenge to divide what I should be doing instead of AI, and what I've come up with is I need to approve and test each change that happens manually.  
  
Special thanks to the madman [philipzae](https://github.com/philipzae) who did a metric shite-ton of bug reports and suggestions for this release.
